## Claim / definitions
- A nonlinear Hall rectifier on a gyromorph photonic layer may show a feature in R(ω)=I_DC/P_abs near the photonic band edge. Straw man: ordinary field enhancement (raw DC output peaks at band edge). Normalized R cancels amplitude.
- E=A e; I_DC=|A|² e†Me; P_abs=|A|² e†Le; R=e†Me/e†Le. With P=ee†, g_ω=½Tr(Ṗ²): dR/dω = 2Re[e†(M-RL)ė]/(e†Le); |dR/dω| ≤ 2|e†(M-RL)u|√g_ω/(e†Le). If M∝L, R flat. Checked on 300 random cases: law matches finite differences 3e-10, bound never failed, M=cL gave 0 to 7e-10.
- Projector index n=√g∥ "a geometric index, not assumed equal to the electromagnetic refractive index". Two records of one device: G(b)=G0+g2 b². Two geometries kept apart. "In a two-band model det g=(B/2)² exactly". Response is curvature: H=δI+δσx+caσz, χ=2c²/δ=8δ g_aa(0) (VOTKP Theorem 4.1, Appendix A.2).
- Film dipole with isotropic absorption: M=xx^T, L=I, R=|e_x|²; linear polarization angle θ: |dR/dω|=|sin2θ|√g_ω (200 cases, 5e-10).
## Forward calculation (frozen Oct 13 2026)
2D model, 600 resonant point scatterers density 1/a², coupled dipoles, ω0=3.4c/a width 0.3c/a, light along x field along y; film = dipole film along x, R=Σ|E_x|²/Σ|E|² over a 3a×16a strip. Gyromorph patterns by spectral optimization: one ring of 22 peaks at 0.149-0.150 N each, next highest peak 0.056 N, closest pair 0.80a; three patterns. Result: film-averaged R inside gap (ka 3.66-3.80): gyromorph 0.193, 0.215, 0.229 vs random packings 0.251, 0.263, 0.274 ("about 0.05 lower, with no overlap"); triangular lattice no dip. Gap centre ka 3.715-3.730; interior intensity at gap ÷ median 0.07-0.12 (gyromorph) vs 0.02; above-gap R 0.196,0.278,0.285 / 0.176,0.202,0.476 / 0.293 / 0.213.
- What died: single-point reading (local polarization is speckle; R swings by median 0.85-0.98 in 0.3 window; rotation law holds point by point on 2,400 curves); the step into the gap as gyromorph mark (random 0.10-0.15 vs gyromorph 0.15-0.25 overlap). What survived: level inside the gap — film reads averaged partly polarized state Π=½(I+s·σ), |s|<1; "This is the projector picture with mixing, not rotation speed."
- Limits: point scatterers not holes; 2D; 600 scatterers; one film position; resonance chosen not fitted.
