## 2. The split: brightness and normalized direction

- (1) ds^2 = (1/h) sum a_i dx_i^2 / x_i. (2) split: (1/h) sum a_i dx_i^2/x_i = (d log h)^2 + sum dp_i^2/p_i. Proof: a_i dx_i = d(h p_i) = p_i dh + h dp_i; middle term vanishes (sum p_i = 1, sum dp_i = 0). ## 6. Colour selects the leader
- Origin: "The question that turned the deep dive into a theorem was asked on 24 October: what if colour selects the leader eigenvector?" Light bouncing between surfaces/layers = positive linear map x -> Mx on channels each pass.
- THEOREM 2 [Derived; Computed COLOUR-LEADER-01; REPAIR-CHECKS-01 R3]: M strictly positive entries; simple largest eigenvalue lambda_1 > 0, positive right/left eigenvectors v_1, w_1 (w_1^T v_1 = 1), all other |lambda| < lambda_1. r = max_{k>=2}|lambda_k|/lambda_1 < 1; m = largest Jordan block among eigenvalues of modulus r lambda_1. For positive start x: lambda_1^{-n} M^n x = (w_1^T x) v_1 + O(n^{m-1} r^n). In split (2):
  (i) normalized direction converges to Perron direction; for 0<r<1, distance to interior leader d_n = O(n^{m-1} r^n). If a unique eigenvalue has largest subleading modulus, real and simple, mode present in initial state: d_{n+1}/d_n -> r ("an asymptotic ratio, not an exact finite-step multiplier"). If r=0, subleading part nilpotent, finite termination.
  (ii) brightness: h(M^{n+1}x)/h(M^n x) -> lambda_1; log increment -> log lambda_1.
  (iii) near a tie, r = 1 - g: uniform pure-exponential bound d_n <= C(1-g)^n gives sufficient index n >= log(C/eps)/[-log(1-g)]; at fixed tolerance, bounded C, an O(1/g) upper estimate; matching lower estimate needs a uniformly excited mode; Jordan envelope retains n^{m-1}, can require O(log(1/g)/g) sufficient bound, "does not by itself establish a uniform pure inverse-gap law."
  (iv) exact leading tie requires leaving strict positivity: nonnegative M with semisimple leading eigenvalue lambda, spectral projector P: lambda^{-n} M^n x -> P x; if Px != 0 limiting normalized colour Px/1^T Px. "A multidimensional leading space can retain the initial preparation."
  Proof: Jordan form; (mu I + N)^n binomial sum; late-threshold equation y - k log y = log(C/eps) + k log(1/q) with k=m-1, q=-log(1-g), y=qn ("polynomial factor cannot be discarded"; "not a universal counterexample to every stronger family-specific inverse-gap estimate"). ## 7. The turning leaf
- "A turning leaf is Theorem 2 with pigments in place of channels, and it has a clock in it."
- (7) source-free independent decay c_i(t) >= 0, rates k_i, no production/conversion: d/dt log(c_i/c_j) = -(k_i - k_j); log(c_i(t)/c_j(t)) = log(c_i(0)/c_j(0)) - (k_i - k_j) t. "The pigment ratio reads elapsed time at a rate set by the gap between decay rates; recovering the time needs a known gap and a known starting ratio."
