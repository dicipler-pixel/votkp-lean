## Abstract (key claims, his words)
- "fully coordinate-free formulation of the Quantum Geometric Tensor on the Grassmannian manifold Gr(k,H) of rank-k orthogonal projectors associated with an isolated spectral subspace of a self-adjoint operator on a separable Hilbert space H".
- Hermitian form Q on the tangent bundle; "symmetric real part is the intrinsic Hilbert–Schmidt quantum metric and its skew-symmetric imaginary part is the Berry curvature 2-form. Every tangent deformation is generated by transitions across the spectral gap".
- "the determinant inequality ... extended to any pair of tangent directions on the Grassmannian, with its extremal partner given explicitly. Every bound in the paper is sharp and rigid".
- "the metric is the quantum Fisher information of the moving subspace"; friction on projector rotations = "the metric reweighted by the spectrum"; bounds excess work "from below by the squared intrinsic length over the time allowed"; friction weight checked against slow-driving simulations of two baths.
- Transport tensors (quantum metric dipole, nonlinear Hall, orbital moment) "arise as pullbacks of one cross-gap pair sum read with different powers of the gap". Geodesics of the pullback metric obey a refraction law "whose index is the rate at which the subspace rotates: strata where the subspace stops rotating reflect trajectories, while lines where the gap narrows guide them". "A stratum on which the metric collapses along itself is a single projector; a fold is silent only at first order and moves at second, and the bending of every geodesic is a quantum metric dipole."
- Mixed states via symmetric logarithmic derivative. Applied to public data from a superconducting heat engine and a BEC: "single eigenvectors trade identity where their gap is unresolved while the subspace keeps it. These data test the stability of the subspace, the reason for building the geometry on the projector, rather than the tensor itself. The Davis–Kahan bound, read as a rule for what can be looked at separately and what only as a whole, ties every result together."

## Sec 2 Framework — THE INTRINSIC METRIC ON THE PROJECTOR BUNDLE
- Gr(k,H): rank-k orthogonal projectors, real-analytic Banach manifold; Pi = Pi† = Pi^2, Tr Pi = k, k<∞.
- 2.1 tangent space: self-adjoint V with V = PiV + VPi (differentiate Pi^2=Pi). Hence Pi V Pi = 0 and (I−Pi)V(I−Pi)=0. (2.1): V = Pi V (I−Pi) + (I−Pi) V Pi = [[0, X],[X*, 0]], X = Pi V (I−Pi). "There are no intra-subspace contributions to the geometry." Figure 1 "Change crosses the gap."
- Lemma 2.1 (exact characterisation of tangent space): V bounded self-adjoint of form (2.1) is velocity of a curve; proof A = [[0, iX],[−iX*, 0]], Pi(t) = e^{itA} Pi e^{−itA}, Pi'(0)= i[A,Pi] = V; dimension 2k(n−k) = dim_R Gr(k,C^n).
- Remark 2.2: X has rank ≤ k so every trace converges (only place finiteness of k is used).
- 2.2 intrinsic QGT on complexification T_C = {A+iB}: (2.2) Q_Pi(A,B) := Tr(Pi A† B). Sesquilinear, reduces to Tr(Pi V W) on self-adjoint args, positive semidefinite: Q_Pi(A,A) = Tr(Pi A†A Pi) = ||A Pi||_HS^2 ≥ 0 (definite on real tangent space; only semidefinite on complexification).
- For V,W with blocks X,Y: VW = diag(XY*, X*Y), Q_Pi(V,W) = Tr(XY*).
- (2.3) Q_Pi(V,W) = g_Pi(V,W) − (i/2) Ω_Pi(V,W), with
  **g_Pi(V,W) = (1/2) Tr(VW) = Re Tr(XY*)** (intrinsic Hilbert–Schmidt Riemannian metric),
  **Ω_Pi(V,W) = i Tr(Pi[V,W]) = −2 Im Tr(XY*)** (intrinsic Berry curvature 2-form).
  Signs of standard convention Q = g − (i/2)F, F = dA, A = i<u|du> for k=1; trace of non-abelian curvature for k>1.
- Remark 2.3: g_Pi(V,W)=(1/2)Tr(VW) "appears to have forgotten Pi. It has not." Identity Tr(PiVW)+Tr(PiWV)=Tr(VW) fails for general operators; holds because V,W off-diagonal. "The dependence has migrated from the formula into its domain."
- Remark 2.4: factor 1/2 forced: Bloch sphere Pi_− = (1/2)(I − n̂·σ), (1/2)Tr(V^2)=(1/4)|δn̂|^2, g_θθ = 1/4 (Fubini–Study). Alternative normalisation Tr(VW) would give 1/2 and disagree with literature.
- 2.3 Riesz–Kato: (2.4) Pi_k = (1/2πi)∮_{Γ_k}(zI−Σ)^{-1}dz; gap Δ>0; I = indices inside Γ_k, I^c outside.

## Sec 3 Spectral realization
- Lemma 3.1 (3.1) V = (1/2πi)∮ (zI−Σ)^{-1} δΣ (zI−Σ)^{-1} dz; (3.2) <m|V|n> = <m|δΣ|n>/(λ_in − λ_out) if exactly one of m,n in I, else 0. Proof by residues (both inside: residues cancel; equal eigenvalues: order-2 pole residue vanishes; both outside: holomorphic).
- Remarks 3.2 degeneracy (gap hypothesis ensures no vanishing cross-gap denominator), 3.3 self-adjointness.
- Corollary 3.4: Pi V Pi = 0 and (I−Pi)V(I−Pi)=0; "the entire tangent geometry is generated by virtual transitions across the spectral gap." Logically distinct from Section 2's statement (covers all tangent vectors vs those generated by δΣ).
- **Proposition 3.5 (Gap bound): ||V||_HS ≤ sqrt(2k) ||δΣ|| / Δ.** Proof: ||V||_HS^2 = 2||X||_HS^2; X entries divided by gaps ≥Δ; ||Pi δΣ (I−Pi)||_HS ≤ sqrt(k)||δΣ||. Sharp (two-level, purely off-diagonal δΣ); equality in Thm 6.3(i).
- **Theorem 3.6 (spectral decomposition)**: (3.3) g_Pi(V,W) = Σ_{m∈I}Σ_{n∈I^c} Re[<m|δ1Σ|n><n|δ2Σ|m>]/(λ_n−λ_m)^2; (3.4) Ω_Pi(V,W) = i Σ_{m∈I}Σ_{n∈I^c} (<m|δ1Σ|n><n|δ2Σ|m> − <m|δ2Σ|n><n|δ1Σ|m>)/(λ_n−λ_m)^2. g(V,V) = Σ|<m|δ1Σ|n>|^2/(λ_n−λ_m)^2.
- Remark 3.7: antisymmetric numerator in (3.4) is gap-weighted (not the commutator [δ1Σ,δ2Σ] projected). Remark 3.8: operator statements (2.1), Lemma 2.1, (2.3), g=Tr(XX*), Prop 3.5 hold with no discreteness; only index sums need it. Remark 3.9: δΣ→V onto in finite dim (take <m|δΣ|n> = (λ_m−λ_n)X_mn); infinite dim may need unbounded δΣ. Remark 3.10: many-body form = quasi-adiabatic continuation (Hastings–Wen; BMNS); generator <a|Y|b> = F(E_a−E_b)<a|δΣ|b>, F(ω) = −1/ω for |ω|≥gap; Prop 3.5 weakens as N grows (||δΣ|| grows with system size); local-strength bound "is not derived here".

## Sec 4 Parametric realization (pullback)
- γ: M_Δ → Gr(k,H), a ↦ Pi_k(a), smooth on region where gap open; V_i = γ_*(∂/∂a^i) = ∂_i Pi_k.
- (4.1) g_ij(a) = Σ_{m∈I}Σ_{n∈I^c} Re[<m|∂iΣ|n><n|∂jΣ|m>]/(λ_n(a)−λ_m(a))^2; (4.2) Ω_ij likewise with antisymmetrisation; (4.3) g_ij = Re Tr(Pi ∂iPi ∂jPi), Ω_ij = −2 Im Tr(Pi ∂iPi ∂jPi). k=1: g_ii is fidelity susceptibility.
- Prop 4.1 (two ways the pullback degenerates): g_ij = Re Tr(X_i X_j*) is a Gram matrix (PSD); degenerate iff ∂iPi linearly dependent (γ not an immersion). Different from a closing gap: closing gap → metric large; failure of immersion → metric degenerate with gap open. "opposite limits".
- (4.4) γ*Q = g_ij da^i⊗da^j − (i/2) Ω_ij da^i∧da^j. "The conventional parametric quantum geometric tensor ... is not a primitive object: it is the coordinate representation of the pullback γ*Q." "The parameter manifold M does not generate the geometry; it merely selects a finite-dimensional trajectory".
- Remark 4.2 trace sector: for k>1, F_ij is k×k, Ω_ij = tr F_ij; traceless part invisible on Gr; non-abelian connection lives on Stiefel bundle V_k(H)→Gr (principal U(k) bundle). Stated open in S11.
- Prop 4.3 (first-order factorization through the gap): V_i, g_ij, Ω_ij depend on ∂iΣ only through cross-gap block Pi ∂iΣ (I−Pi) and eigenvalues; direction B with vanishing cross-gap block is silent (∂_B Pi=0). Not preserved at second order: numerical example three-parameter family of six-level operators, k=2, derivative of g_11 along block-diagonal B is 0.113 while ∂_B Pi vanishes to 1e-12.
- 4.3 second order: (4.5) ∂_k g_ij = (1/2)Tr(∂k∂iPi ∂jPi + ∂iPi ∂k∂jPi); ∂_kV_i = V_kV_i + Pi(∂kV_i) + (∂kV_i)Pi + V_iV_k; (4.6) ∂k∂iPi = (1/2πi)∮[R ∂k∂iΣ R + R ∂kΣ R ∂iΣ R + R ∂iΣ R ∂kΣ R]dz, R=(zI−Σ)^{-1}. Statement: spectral forms treating |m>,|n> as parameter independent "omit the three-state terms ... and [are] therefore incomplete".
- 4.4 redistribution operator: (4.7) F(Ω,Pi) = (I−Pi)ΩPi + PiΩ(I−Pi) = ΩPi + PiΩ − 2PiΩPi = [[Ω,Pi],Pi]; F=0 iff [Ω,Pi]=0; ||F||_HS = ||[Ω,Pi]||_HS; F is the intrinsic-metric gradient of Pi ↦ Tr(ΩPi): g_Pi(F,V) = (1/2)Tr(ΩV). (4.8) Pi' = [[Ω,Pi],Pi] is Brockett's double-bracket flow; carries Pi to top-k eigenprojector of Ω; reversed flow to bottom k.

## Sec 5 Spectral lower bounds on dissipation
- Lemma 5.1: rho = Pi_k/k, SLD L = 2 Pi', (5.1) F_Q(t) = Tr(rho L^2) = (4/k) g_Pi(Pi',Pi'); for k=1 (1/4)F_Q = g_Pi(Pi',Pi'). Metric is information-geometric speed, "does not by itself measure dissipation" (unitary motion leaves entropy fixed; SLD vs Kubo–Mori).
- **Theorem 5.2 (Minimum driving time)**: t_f − t_i ≥ Δ_min L / ( sqrt(k) sup_t ||∂_tΣ(t)|| ), L = ∫ sqrt(g_Pi(Pi',Pi')) dt, gap ≥ Δ_min. (Uses sqrt(g)=||Pi'||_HS/sqrt(2) and Prop 3.5.)
- 5.3 friction: relaxation model rho' = −iχ[Σ,rho] + (ω(Σ(t)) − rho)/τ, χ∈{0,1}; W_ex = W − ΔF. Slow driving: δρ_mn = −τ ω'_mn / (1 + iχτ(E_m−E_n)) + O(adot^2); ω'_nn = −β p_n(Σ'_nn − <Σ'>); ω'_mn = Σ'_mn (p_n−p_m)/(E_n−E_m). (5.2) ζ = τβ Var_p(Σ'_diag) + Σ_{m≠n} c_mn |Σ'_mn|^2 (p_m−p_n)/(E_n−E_m), c_mn = τ/(1+χ²τ²(E_m−E_n)²). Every term ≥0. χ=0: friction tensor of Sivak–Crooks (Kubo–Mori form of Scandi–Perarnau-Llobet).
- (5.3) ζ_mn = 2 c_mn (p_m−p_n)(E_n−E_m) g_mn, g_mn = |Σ'_mn|²/(E_n−E_m)². ζ_rot = Σ_{m∈I, n∉I} ζ_mn; ζ ≥ ζ_rot.
- **Theorem 5.3 (Spectral lower bound on dissipation)** to first order in rate: (i) two levels ζ_rot = 2c(Δ) Δ tanh(βΔ/2) g_Pi(Pi',Pi'); (ii) zero temperature, nondegenerate ground state, Δ_n=E_n−E_0: (5.4) min_n 2c(Δ_n)Δ_n g ≤ ζ_rot ≤ max_n 2c(Δ_n)Δ_n g, and (5.5) W_ex ≥ (min_{t,n} 2c(Δ_n)Δ_n)∫g dt ≥ (min 2c Δ) L²/T.

## Sec 6 Metric–curvature inequalities
- **Theorem 6.1 (Metric control of Berry curvature)** (6.1): |Ω_Pi(V,W)| ≤ 2 sqrt( g(V,V) g(W,W) − g(V,W)² ). Proof: Gram determinant of PSD Q ≥0; |Q(V,W)|² = g(V,W)² + (1/4)Ω². Credited as the determinant inequality 4 det g ≥ Ω² (Roy 2014; Mera–Ozawa 2021): "no priority is claimed". Weaker envelope |Ω| ≤ 2 sqrt(g_VV g_WW).
- **Corollary 6.2 (Sharpness, explicit extremal pair)**: equality iff Y = cX (c∈C); given V≠0 with block X, W with block iX satisfies g(V,W)=0 and |Ω(V,W)| = 2||X||²_HS = 2 sqrt(g_VV g_WW), every rank k. J: X↦iX is the complex structure of the Grassmannian.
- 6.1 geometric angle: g(V,W) = sqrt(g_VV g_WW) cos θ_G; (6.2) |Ω| ≤ 2 sqrt(g_VV g_WW)|sin θ_G|. Control is relative not absolute; near degeneracy both sides diverge at same rate; "the inequality is not a regularisation".
- 6.2 control hierarchy: g bounds Ω; "The ordering is one of control, not of generation".
- **Theorem 6.3 (Equality cases)**, B = Pi δΣ (I−Pi): (i) gap bound met iff δΣ couples sectors only through pairs with |λ_m−λ_n|=Δ exactly AND B has k nonzero singular values all equal to ||δΣ||; (ii) Thm 5.2 equality iff at a.e. time gap bound is equality at Δ(t)=Δ_min with ||∂_tΣ|| = sup; (iii) chain (5.5) equality iff ζ=ζ_rot, weight 2c(Δ_n)Δ_n equals its minimum on every pair that carries metric at every time, and intrinsic speed constant; (iv) curvature: equality iff W in complex line of V; (6.3) g_VV g_WW − g_VW² − (1/4)Ω² = g_Pi(V,V) g_Pi(W⊥,W⊥), W⊥ has block Y − cX, c = Tr(YX*)/Tr(XX*).
- Numerical: k copies of two-level meet bound to 1e-8 (k=1,2,3); moving one partner to 1.5Δ drops ratio to 0.67–0.90; unequal strengths 0.79–0.87. Rigid rotation Σ(t)=(Δ/2)n(t)·σ along great circle at constant rate meets Thm 5.2 ratio 1.000000; breathing gap 0.667. Constant speed ∫g dt = L²/T ratio 1.00000000; speed modulated 60% overspends 19%. "The cheapest protocol therefore moves the subspace along a geodesic at constant intrinsic speed" (Salamon–Berry). (6.3) holds on 300 random pairs to 1e-6 relative. "two directions saturate the bound exactly when they span a complex line."
- **Proposition 6.4 (trace condition)** (6.4): for two-parameter family, any Riemannian h on parameter surface, tr_h g ≥ 2 sqrt(det g/det h) ≥ |Ω_12|/sqrt(det h); first equality iff g conformal to h; second iff curvature bound saturated. With h Euclidean on BZ: trace condition tr g ≥ |Ω| of ideal bands (Roy 2014). With h round metric of parameter sphere: two-level band g=(1/4)h, |Ω_θφ|=(1/2)sinθ; lowest spin-1 band g=(1/2)h, |Ω_θφ|=sinθ (both to 6e-16). Breaking symmetry with 0.8 S_z² lifts strictly above by 0.04–0.46. Same equality for lowest band in every spin j (lowest Landau level on sphere, App C).
- Example 6.5 (a loop that comes back still remembers): lower two-level band around circle of polar angle θ0=60°: projector returns to 1e-16; Berry phase −(1/2)(solid angle) = −π(1−cosθ0) = −π/2, computed −1.570797. "The subspace is back where it began; the history of the path is kept only in the phase".

## Sec 8 Geodesic refraction across degenerate strata

- **Prop 8.2**: ||Pi1−Pi2||²_HS = 2Σ sin²θ_i (principal angles); sqrt(g) = sinθ to leading order; "the refractive index n = sqrt(g_∥) is the rate at which the occupied subspace rotates".
- **Prop 8.3 (Total internal reflection)**: C = g_∥ sin²θ conserved; geodesic with C>0 cannot reach stratum g_∥→0, turns where g_∥=C (sinθ=1); only C=0 reaches it. "The degenerate stratum is repulsive".
