## Abstract (his words, condensed)
Three finite spectral constructions connected "while retaining the information lost by each". Block operator on unitary graph connection has two quadratic energies = Hermitian and skew halves of DA; spectral decomposition expresses both as Born-weighted squared eigenvalue jumps. For positive square-root masses Koide is equivalent to a half-overlap and to a nonzero nilpotent auxiliary matrix. "Subtracting the smallest scalar loss that makes this auxiliary generator contractive produces a damped oscillator with zeta^2 = 3Q - 1. Under specified resource constraints its asymptotic decay exponent is maximized at Q = 2/3; this is a conditional variational characterization, not a mass-generation mechanism." Arithmetic: at a good prime of an elliptic curve, normalized Frobenius gives a unitary edge with energies 4p - a_p^2 and a_p^2; adjoint transport invariant under a_p -> -a_p so energies discard the sign needed by twist comparisons and signed Frobenius statistics; local complementary twist vs fixed global quadratic twist repaired. Mass-root circulant has primitive triangle factor 1 - 2cos(3 phi) u^3 + u^6; its cycle coefficient recovers the cubic invariant sqrt(m1 m2 m3) omitted by the quadratic Koide constraint; noncentral rank-two connection retains it; twisted Bass factorization supplies the graph-prime bridge. "The results are finite identities and information-recovery statements; they do not prove BSD, RH, a universal exceptional-point selection law, or a prime origin of lepton masses."
