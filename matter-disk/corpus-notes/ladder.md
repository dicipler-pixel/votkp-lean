# PART A. "The Angle Between the Rails" (31 August 2026) — Jeromie Beasley
Subtitle: "A reciprocal spectrum, its Gudermannian, and the census coefficient it fixes". Grade key on p.1: [T] proved here, [V] verified numerically, [C] cited, [O] open.

## Abstract (his words, key sentences)
"A three-term recurrence is a discrete Schrödinger operator, and its transfer matrix having unit determinant is exactly what makes it one. When that holds, the two solutions — the growing rail and the decaying rail — are exact reciprocals, and I show this is not an arithmetic accident but the Pythagorean identity in disguise: writing the limiting trace as T = 2 sec ψ, where ψ is the angle between the two rails, gives λ± = sec ψ ± tan ψ, so λ+λ− = sec²ψ − tan²ψ = 1 identically."
"The growth rate is then the inverse Gudermannian of that angle, log λ+ = gd⁻¹(ψ) ... and the band edge |T| = 2 is ψ = 0, where the rails merge and the transfer matrix becomes parabolic."
Consequences: (1) odd degree self-reciprocal spectrum forced to carry a mode exactly at rest (λ=1 fixed point of λ↦1/λ) — explains Catalan transport = Apéry ζ(3) transport with λ=1 mode adjoined, reducing the coincidence to the single Pell solution 17²−2·12²=1. (2) Rice–Mele chain: elliptic modulus ũ = tan(ψ/2) exactly; inverse correlation length, inverse Gudermannian and arccosh ρ are one quantity. "Counting the rungs of the resulting entanglement ladder against a resolution floor then derives the census coefficient A = 2c/π² from the closed-form spacing Δ = 4πK(ũ)/K′(ũ), with the same π² cancelling in the entropy and surviving in the count. Every number is produced by the single script in Appendix A."

## Sec 1 Two rails and the angle between them  (THE RAIL ANGLE)
Recurrence a_n u_{n+1} + b_n u_n + c_n u_{n−1} = 0 = eigenvalue equation of a Jacobi matrix (discrete Schrödinger operator); transfer matrix in SL2 is exactly what makes it one. Limiting companion of λ² − Tλ + D in coordinates (u_n,u_{n−1}); eigenvector for λ is (λ,1). Angle ψ between the two eigendirections:
 (1) cos ψ = (D+1)/sqrt(T² + (D−1)²); for D=1, cos ψ = 2/T. [T] (uses λ+λ−=D, λ+²+λ−²=T²−2D ⇒ (λ+²+1)(λ−²+1)=T²+(D−1)²). Closed form checked against direct eigenvector computation at every point of Table 1. [V]
Definition (his words): "I call ψ the rail angle: the angle between the growing and decaying solutions, measured in the coordinates the recurrence itself supplies."
Table 1 (T, D, ψ in degrees, T²−4D, regime): Apéry ζ(3): 34, 1, 86.6277133, 1152, gap. Catalan block: 34, 1, 86.6277133, 1152, gap. π+e: 2√2, 1, 45 exactly, 4, gap. ζ(2)+ζ(3): 5/4, 1/4, 30.9637565, 0.5625, gap. band edge: 2, 1, 0, 0, rails merge. "π + e sits at exactly 45°; T = 4 would give exactly 60°."

## Sec 2 Reciprocity is the Pythagorean identity
Theorem 1 [T]: D=1, T=2 sec ψ: λ± = (T ± sqrt(T²−4))/2 = sec ψ ± tan ψ, so λ+λ− = sec²ψ − tan²ψ = 1. Proof: sqrt(T²−4)=sqrt(4sec²ψ−4)=2 tan ψ.
Regimes: |T|<2: no real ψ, elliptic, rails on unit circle, spectral parameter in a band; |T|>2 hyperbolic, one rail grows one decays, in a gap; |T|=2: cos ψ=1, ψ=0, rails coincide, parabolic, non-diagonalisable [T]. Quote: "The band edge of the Schrödinger operator is an exceptional point, and the rail angle is what closes at it."

## Abstract (key points)
X = [ρ1..ρ_{N−1}] (mass-weighted Jacobi, d×(N−1)) is square exactly when N=d+1; then G = XᵀX is an unconstrained PSD form; trace-one slice Σ_d = {G ⪰ 0, tr G = 1} is a smooth (d(d+1)/2 − 1)-dimensional convex body; rotation-reduced shape space is its orientation double cover branched over det X = 0; tangent space splits into d−1 eigenvalue directions + d(d−1)/2 eigenframe directions. d=2: Kendall shape sphere radius 1/2, equator collinear. d=3: five-dimensional four-body shape space, coplanar locus as branch divisor.
Transport operator A(H) = TH + HT − 2 tr(TH) G on symmetric traceless H: block diagonal in the eigenvalue/eigenframe split; eigenframe spectrum exactly {t_i + t_j}; eigenvalue block is a rank-one modification of a diagonal matrix with secular equation Σ g_i t_i/(t_i − λ) = 1. Positive weights g_i t_i ⇒ one root strictly between each pair of consecutive t_i: A is non-normal with strictly real interlacing spectrum for every d on the whole regular stratum; exceptional points only on boundary det G = 0 (eigenvalue pinned at 2t_k; a secular root may meet it giving a genuine Jordan block). Binary collision loci have codimension d, not one.
Metric-free: operator uses only matrix trace; spectrum unchanged in non-orthonormal basis. Diagonal similarity D_t − g tᵀ = S(D_t − u uᵀ)S⁻¹, u = sqrt(g)⊙t, S = diag sqrt(g/t).
Volume part: Koszul–Vinberg characteristic function; log φ = −(d+1) log Vol, Vol = |det X| simplex volume; metric is Hessian of log volume. Both operator families self-adjoint for induced metric iff parameter is dual coordinate G⁻¹. Boundary: eigenvalues diverge as Vol⁻² and Vol⁻¹, no defectiveness; congruence operator = unique positive square root of volume metric; additive one its logarithmic generator; cubic form is the connection (Christoffel = half third derivative), geodesics G^{1/2} e^{tV} G^{1/2}; at the corner of the divisor the two rates merge. "the divisor is smooth precisely where the rates split and singular precisely where they merge." Off ladder (N−1>d) tangent split fails; threefold split survives (eigenvalue, frame-within-support, support-moving).
