## Abstract (five results, his words abridged)
Proposal: no absolute reference of size; shrinking matter in static space observationally equivalent to fixed matter in expanding space ("take the strongest version seriously, stress-test it, and then ask: if matter has no absolute scale, what is matter?").
(1) Closed units dictionary, unique: holding h, c, α fixed, atomic redshift forces ℓ,t ∝ (1+z), m ∝ (1+z)⁻¹ (Wetterich's direction); Bohr rod a0 m = h/(αc); assignments scaling size and mass together "miss by four powers of (1+z)".
(2) Closed theory bifurcates: pure reframing (identical predictions) or environment-dependent scaling (scalar–tensor theory facing lunar-laser, equivalence-principle, clock walls).
(3) In the operator framework: "difference observables are exactly invariant under K ↦ K+cI, spectral projectors, principal angles and the infrared boundary census are exactly invariant under K ↦ cK, and the single failure is an ultraviolet logarithm with coefficient A = c/π² per boundary point." New in v5: Majorana-chain computation at c=1/2 brackets predicted 1/π² from both sides, "more than forty times closer to it than to the c = 1 value"; additivity extends to the parameter-free linearity law A = 2c/π²; entanglement ladder measured directly at c = 1/2 (abstract prints "c = 12" — extraction of 1/2); dictionary closed over gravity, αG = Gm²/hc invariant. "A rescaling of the substrate can only be registered as a running, never as a power."
(4) Matter = "a gapped substrate wearing a boundary — gapped census finite and area-law, directions priced, records protected — while gas is the gapless phase: census flooded, directions free, failures non-modal, and walls only where the gas manufactures them." Matter classified by three scale-free ratios.
(5) Environmental branch's confrontation with RAR baseline explicit: a G that grows with emptiness predicts a negative residual–compactness dependence at fixed acceleration and mass; every measured sign in the single SPARC regime that retains structural information is positive; each escape collapses it to a reframing or strands it below the 0.106 dex extraction floor.

## Sec 2 Strongest version of shrinking matter
- Theorem 1 (Closure) [T]: c,h constant, α invariant, redshift atomic ⇒ ℓ ∝ (1+z), t ∝ (1+z), m ∝ (1+z)⁻¹, e²=αhc fixed. Invariants: α by construction; mp/me; Rydberg–second product (Ry = ½α²mc² ∝ (1+z)⁻¹, atomic seconds ∝ (1+z)). Uniqueness is "under the stated assumptions (fixed c, h, α and the atomic redshift condition), not over every conceivable varying-constant theory". Matches Wetterich's direction; "the closure does not merely permit that lineage; it derives it."
- Theorem 2 (Gravitational closure) [T]: M_Pl ∝ m ∝ (1+z)⁻¹ ⇒ G = hc/M_Pl² ∝ (1+z)²; αG = Gm²/hc invariant; Branch 1 is M_Pl=M_Pl(z), Branch 2 is M_Pl=M_Pl(z,E). Doesn't attempt early-universe evolution; BBN is the downstream test [28,29].

- 2.2 rigid rod: (1) a0 m = hαc (written "h/(αc)" in abstract/table; text eq. printed "a0 m = hαc"... as extracted from the Drive text; the abstract and Sec 1 table print "a0 m = h/(αc)"). Size and mass must scale oppositely; "it changes the rod, and the rod is measured."

- 4.2 Three scale-free ratios [H]: (i) relative departure from normality Δ²_F/‖K‖²_F (dilation-invariant; gas at rest zero; star = matter held off the normal manifold by throughput; black hole = pinned at a wall that never answers); (ii) census occupancy against geometric cap; (iii) which records cross walls: "Metric-protected amplitude dies at a rigidity wall as 1/√2λ; integer-protected phase crosses." Context: environmental branch (dark matter as G varying with mean emptiness between masses = original contribution of Gradenwitz [5]). "It is fitting that the informative regime is the gas phase of Sec. 4.1."
Run in script SPARC-SIGN-03. Residual: Δ = log10 g_obs − log10 g_RAR(g_bar) (McGaugh interpolating function [23], g_RAR = g_bar/[1−exp(−sqrt(g_bar/a0))], a0=1.2×10⁻¹⁰ m/s²). Differs from Newtonian variable-G residual log10(g_obs/g_bar) by baseline boost B(g_bar) = log10(g_RAR/g_bar), 0.57 dex at regime's split boundary (log10 g_bar = −10.926) to 1.06 dex at faint end (g_bar≈10⁻¹²). Minimal monotone mechanism G_eff/G_N = 1 + ηE (E normalized emptiness): Δ = log10(1+ηE) − B(g_bar). Two ways to inhabit data: (a) mechanism is origin of the boost ⇒ ⟨log10(1+ηE) | g_bar⟩ = B(g_bar), i.e. conditional geometric mean of 1+ηE between 3.7 at split boundary and 11.5 at faint end (script prints 3.7, 6.8, 11.5 as geometric means → slopes −(etaE/gm)); residual is fluctuation of emptiness about its acceleration-conditional mean, with

("for every η > 0 and every E decreasing in C: compact galaxies below their acceleration-mates, diffuse ones above"). The printed sign is NEGATIVE (minus sign in front, "< 0" after).
