## Abstract (his words, condensed in order)
McCormick showed free-streaming neutrinos raise PBH-formation threshold "by about 0.235 R_nu in the linear compaction", effect fades under gradual decoupling. This paper asks what sets the fading.
1. "the neutrino collision operator is read exactly as a cut between what is kept and what streams. All of its non-normality, (1/8)||[L,L*]||^2 = kappa^2 k^2/3, lies on the single link between density and flux. With separate rates for flux and shear it is k^2 kappa_1^2/3 + (4k^2/15)(kappa_2 - kappa_1)^2, and the weight across the cut equals the sound speed, sqrt(E_B) = k/sqrt3."
2. McCormick's code run on five cases outside his paper, predictions and kill lines written before runs. Broad profile (collapse near eps ~ 7-9) keeps same share of neutrino effect as Gaussian (collapse near eps ~ 4) or less (G = 0.181 and 0.045 vs 0.190 and 0.063): "collapse time alone does not set the clock". Steeper history kappa/H = (eps_dec/eps)^6 gives G = 0.648, 0.317, 0.154 at eps_dec = 1,2,3 vs 0.683, 0.431, ~0.30 for standard: "how the neutrinos leave matters, not only when". Thresholds unchanged by tenfold higher collision-rate cap and finer grid. "One reading survives every run: the memory of the kept sector, the population the density keeps under the decoupling operator, read mostly at the first compaction maximum. With one weight fitted to McCormick's curve and then frozen, it predicted 0.663, 0.246 and 0.118 before the runs, all within the kill line of 0.08; a history-blind rule fails at eps_dec = 3 by 0.146."
3. "the memory has a location": two neutrino states with same density, flux and anisotropic stress, differing only in finer angular structure, give later density/flux/stress differing by several per cent if the difference is set at or after decoupling; set deep in the coupled era it is erased by factor exp[-eps_dec^n/(n tau_s^n)]. "An exact block elimination puts the whole effect in one hidden-source term, and the kept population obeys (A')^2 <= 4 E_B A(1-A). A closure on density, flux and stress cannot carry this memory." Algebraic identities, counterexample, verdict arithmetic machine-checked in Lean (App C). Four further tests stated for the e+- annihilation epoch and collision terms beyond relaxation time.

## §1 The question
- PBHs heavier than ~10^6 Msun form while neutrinos (41-49% of radiation) stop scattering and stream freely. McCormick [McC] evolved neutrinos with angle-resolved transport inside spherical GR: free streaming makes collapse harder. Threshold on linear compaction C_l rises by Delta C_{l,c}/C_{l,c} ~ 0.235 R_nu; threshold delta_c on compaction peak rises ~ 0.15 R_nu. Gaussian: perfect-fluid beta becomes ~ beta^1.18 above 6e5 Msun.
- Gradual decoupling kappa = H (eps_dec/eps)^3, eps = 1/(a H r_m) (Hubble radius in units of perturbation size). Define G = (delta_c - delta_c^fluid)/(delta_c^inst - delta_c^fluid) (eq. 1), share of instantaneous free-streaming shift that survives. McCormick's Table II: G = 0.959, 0.867, 0.683, 0.431, 0.190, 0.063 at eps_dec = 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8. He notes linear theory gives estimates of opposite sign and "does not determine which diagnostic, if any, predicts the nonlinear threshold" [Sec. IV C].
- Two questions: what in the neutrino transport operator carries the effect (§2)? which clock and which part of the decoupling history sets G (§3-§5)?

## §2 The collision operator as a cut (label sec:cut)
- Setup: one Fourier mode k, linearised neutrino intensity in McCormick's relaxation model: d_t f = L f, L = A + D, A = -i k mu, D = -kappa (I - P); mu = cosine of angle to wave vector, P projects onto isotropic part. A anti-Hermitian; D Hermitian, conserves isotropic part (neutrino energy in plasma frame), removes flux (goes to plasma). Normalised Legendre basis |l>; streaming couples neighbours: <l+1|mu|l> = c_l = (l+1)/sqrt((2l+1)(2l+3)); operator lives on a chain l = 0,1,2,... (Fig. 2 chain).
- "Non-normality is connection energy." For Hermitian D and anti-Hermitian A, [L,L*] = 2[A,D]; diagonal D: [A,D]_ij = A_ij (D_j - D_i). Eq: (1/8)||[L,L*]||_F^2 = sum over links l->l+1 of k^2 c_l^2 (D_{l+1} - D_l)^2. "This is the scalar case of the identity in [CE]: the self-commutator energy of a transport operator is the energy of its Hermitian field failing to be parallel along the links [derived; Appendix A.1]." One rate: only link 0->1 has a jump: (1/8)||[L,L*]||_F^2 = kappa^2 k^2/3 [derived; checked to 1e-12, I1]. "All the non-normality of the single-rate model lies on the link between density and flux, the same channel as the momentum drag kappa q in McCormick's plasma equation [Eq. (6)]."
- Beyond relaxation time: flux relaxes at kappa_1 (momentum transfer), shear and higher at kappa_2: (1/8)||[L,L*]||_F^2 = k^2 kappa_1^2/3 + (4k^2/15)(kappa_2 - kappa_1)^2 [derived; checked, I2]. Stationary in kappa_2 at kappa_2 = kappa_1. If nu-nu scattering damps shear but not flux, with rates relative to e+- scattering of 1.5 for nu_e and 7 for nu_mu,tau, the shear link carries 64% and 97.5% of the non-normality [computed, under those assumed ratios].
- "The weight across the cut is the sound speed": H = k mu, weight between kept and streaming parts E_B = ||P H (I-P)||^2 = k^2 c_0^2 = k^2/3, so sqrt(E_B) = k/sqrt3 = c_s k, the radiation sound speed [WB] [derived; I3]. "A rate bound built on E_B therefore sees the root-mean-square streaming speed 1/sqrt3, not the light-speed front that empties the overdensity [OSL]."
- "The kept sector, exactly": isotropic part has closed propagator <0|(s-L)^{-1}|0> = g/(1 - kappa g), g = (1/k) arctan(k/(s+kappa)) [derived; checked, I4]. Two-moment truncation (l = 0,1) has eigenvalues -kappa/2 +/- sqrt(kappa^2/4 - k^2/3), merging at kappa = 2k/sqrt3 [derived; I5]. "This exceptional point belongs to the truncation. With 200 moments the least-damped mode turns real only at kappa ~ 0.646 k, and in relaxation kinetic theory the hydrodynamic poles meet a branch cut instead [Rom, Hu] [computed]."
- Fig. 2 caption: "The neutrino moments form a chain. Streaming links each moment to its neighbours; collisions put a rate on each. Non-normality sits only where neighbouring rates differ: on the density-flux link (k^2 kappa_1^2/3) and, if shear relaxes at its own rate, on the flux-shear link. Every other link carries none."

## §6 Where the memory sits (label sec:witness)
- Fluid closure carries moments l = 0,1,2 (density, flux, anisotropic stress). Question: can history sit in moments the closure does not carry?
- Exact elimination: v = (a,h), a = v_0, h = (v_1, v_2, ...). In relaxation model every hidden moment damped at same rate, collision on hidden block sigma(tau) times identity, commutes with streaming. Hidden propagator U_Q(t,r) = e^{-[Sigma(t) - Sigma(r)]} e^{-ik(t-r) M_QQ}, Sigma(tau) = -eps_dec^n/(n tau^n), Sigma' = sigma. Kept amplitude: a'(t) = S(t) - k^2 Int_s^t K(t,r) a(r) dr, K = c_0^2 [U_Q(t,r)]_11, S = -i k c_0 [U_Q(t,s) h(s)]_1 [derived; A.5]. "The kernel K is the memory; the source S is everything the hidden moments held at time s."
- Matched pair: two states with same (v_0, v_1, v_2) = (1, 0.2, 0.1), differing by +/-0.05 in v_3 (or v_4), scaled to unit norm; both positive angular distributions (f(mu) >= 0.50). Same history, eps_dec = 0.5, planting time tau_s scanned across decoupling. Readout: profile-weighted change in (v_0,v_1,v_2) relative to size, R_ret; witness "on" if R_ret > 1e-3. Every on/off call written before run, from survival factor exp[-eps_dec^n/(n tau_s^n)] and leakage (k/sigma)^l through damped rungs (B.14).
- Table R_ret at tau = 4 (tau_s/eps_dec; n=3 l=3; n=3 l=4; n=6 l=3; n=6 l=4): 0.1: 7e-15, 5e-19, 8e-24, 5e-31. 0.25: 6e-10, 6e-11, 2e-15, 7e-20. 0.5: 8.6e-3 (on), 7.9e-3 (on), 3e-6, 3e-6. 1: 0.081, 0.074, 0.094, 0.086 (all on). 2: 0.095, 0.085, 0.098, 0.088 (all on) [computed]. "Every call came out as predicted. The survival law is quantitative: for n=3 the ratio of entries at 0.5 and 1 is 0.106 against e^{-8/3}/e^{-1/3} = 0.097, and for n=6 at 0.5 the law gives 1.4e-6 against 1.6e-6 at tau = 1.7. Planted in the retained moments instead, deep in coupling the order is l=1>2>3>4, each rung costing about 1e-4 ~ k/sigma; after decoupling all four are of the same size. A hidden difference set at decoupling changes the later density, flux and stress as much as a visible one (Fig. 6)."
- Checks: doubling moments 60 -> 120 and tightening tolerances change every entry by at most 1.4e-5 of its value. Solving elimination equation from closed-form U_Q alone reproduces full difference to 3e-7 - 7e-7, falling fourfold with each halving of step; leaving out the kernel misses by 19-234% [computed; B.14].
- Speed limit on kept population: collisions conserve |0> and remove the rest so ||v|| never grows; streaming links |0> only to |1> with weight k c_0 = sqrt(E_B). For A = |v_0|^2: (A')^2 <= 4 E_B A(1-A), E_B = k^2/3 [derived; A.6; Lean]. "On every trajectory computed here the largest value of the ratio is 0.87 [computed]. The bound limits how fast the kept sector can be emptied; it does not set the threshold."
- Reading: "A closure on density, flux and stress is exact only for structure set while the neutrinos are tightly coupled. Structure set at or after decoupling survives in the finer angular moments and streams back into the visible ones by several per cent. The memory McCormick's runs record therefore has a place to live that no fluid closure contains. Whether realistic initial data carry such structure, and how far it moves the nonlinear threshold, are untested: the memory curves of Section 5 start with the hidden moments empty." (Fig. 6 'the door': log10 R_ret at tau = 4; erased down to 1e-30 deep in coupling; returns at 1e-1 level at/after decoupling; steeper n=6 closes door more sharply.)

## §7 Tests for McCormick's code (open items)
1. Annihilation control: during e+- annihilation plasma sound speed falls to c_s = 0.519 at T = 0.131 MeV while neutrinos' rms speed stays 1/sqrt3. Contest between front and sound f = (1 - c_s)/(1 - 1/sqrt3) reaches 1.135 at 1.6e7 Msun (c_s = 0.521 there). If softer plasma enhanced the effect the shift would reach 7.5%, outside McCormick's envelope 5.3-6.2%, "so that reading already fails". If it reduces it, shift stays 5.5-6.3% [computed; reading]. At 1.6e7 Msun the two readings differ from unmodified 6.31% by +13.5% and -11.9%. The run McCormick lists as missing (same transport at fixed R_nu with p = e/3 vs full EOS at 3e6-1.6e7 Msun) would confirm the sign.
2. Shear against flux: by §2 a collision term damping shear faster than flux puts most non-normality on flux-shear link. Whether threshold follows shear rate more than momentum-transfer rate needs the response itself, not the energy. Three moments, k = s = kappa_1 = kappa_2 = 1: R_00 = 32/37, dR_00/dkappa_2 = -5/1369 != 0 while dE/dkappa_2 = 0 [derived; A.7; Lean]. Proposed run: vary kappa_2 at fixed kappa_1 in McCormick's code and read threshold [proposed].
3. Independence of R_nu: kept-sector form contains no R_nu. One run at R_nu = 0.2, eps_dec = 2, n=3 should give the form's own value G = 0.411, which McCormick's R_nu = 0.409 run puts at 0.431 [proposed].
4. Which mean of the rates: McCormick calibrates a single rate to an energy-group calculation. Arithmetic mean of group rates reproduces his calibrated decoupling temperatures: 1.62 MeV vs 1.6 (e+- only), 1.02 MeV vs 0.93-1.0 (with nu-nu), mass step 2.53 vs 2.5-3. Harmonic and geometric means fail; mean of squares also passes, "so the class of means is identified, not one mean [WB] [computed; reading]."

## §8 Relation to other work and §9 Summary
- Closest analogue: bulk viscosity raises threshold roughly in proportion to strength in full NR [Yuwen]; free streaming a second dissipative channel with same sign. Papanikolaou: thresholds at horizon crossing for time-dependent EOS, delta_c following c_s^2 [Pap]. Escriva ties them to initial profile and curvature of compaction at peak [Esc]. "Test A is consistent with both: the neutrino clock is set early." 2026 thermal-history mass functions through nu decoupling and e+- annihilation treat neutrinos as part of the fluid [Gonin]; for supermassive seeds, mu-distortion bound leaves only heavy-tailed statistics [Dave], McCormick finds raised threshold costs factor 1.2-8 in abundance. "A search of the literature to October 2026 found no other work on thresholds with free-streaming components, on gradual kinetic decoupling in numerical relativity, or on how the threshold depends on when a collision rate switches off. Tests A-D are, as far as that search shows, the first such runs."
- Summary: "The neutrino collision operator is a cut whose whole non-normality sits on one link, density to flux, with weight equal to the sound speed. McCormick's code, run on five cases outside his paper, shows that the collapse time alone does not set the neutrino clock, and that the decoupling history changes the threshold, not only its end point. The kept-sector memory, read mostly at the first compaction maximum and fitted once, predicted three histories it had not seen to within 0.071, 0.014 and 0.037, while a history-blind rule failed. The steep-history thresholds do not move with the rate cap or the grid. The memory sits in the angular structure a fluid closure drops: set at or after decoupling, it returns into density, flux and stress; set before, it is erased."

## Appendix A proofs (as in tex)
- A.1: L* = D - A; [L,L*] = (A+D)(D-A) - (D-A)(A+D) = 2(AD - DA) = 2[A,D]; (1/8)||[L,L*]||_F^2 = (1/2) sum_{i != j} |A_ij|^2 (D_i - D_j)^2; |A_{l,l+1}| = k c_l; with D = diag(0, -kappa_1, -kappa_2, -kappa_2, ...), c_0^2 = 1/3, c_1^2 = 4/15 gives k^2 kappa_1^2/3 + (4k^2/15)(kappa_2 - kappa_1)^2; kappa_1 = kappa_2 = kappa gives kappa^2 k^2/3.
- A.2: P H (I-P) with H = k mu has single entry <0|k mu|1> = k c_0, so E_B = k^2/3; variance of k mu in isotropic state k^2 <mu^2> = k^2/3, same number.
- A.3: u = (s-L)^{-1}|0>: (s + kappa + i k mu) u = |0> + kappa P u; <0|(s+kappa+ik mu)^{-1}|0> = (1/2) Int_{-1}^1 dmu/(s+kappa+ik mu) = k^{-1} arctan[k/(s+kappa)] = g; u_0 = g(1 + kappa u_0), u_0 = g/(1 - kappa g).
- A.4: on span{|0>,|1>} L = [[0, -ik/sqrt3],[-ik/sqrt3, -kappa]], char poly lambda^2 + kappa lambda + k^2/3, discriminant kappa^2 - 4k^2/3 = 0 at kappa = 2k/sqrt3 (not diagonalisable).
- A.5: elimination (see §6); time-independent case e^{t(X - sigma I)} = e^{-sigma t} e^{tX} checked in Lean. Difference of two states with equal a(s) obeys same equation with source S[Delta h(s)], Delta a(s) = 0; if Delta h(s) = 0 vanishes identically.
- A.6: D = -sigma Q <= 0 so d/dtau ||v||^2 = -2 sigma ||Qv||^2 <= 0; <0|D = 0, <0|M = c_0 <1| so v_0' = -i k c_0 v_1; A' = 2 Re(conj(v_0) v_0'), |A'| <= 2 k c_0 |v_0||v_1|; |v_1|^2 <= 1 - A; (A')^2 <= 4 E_B A(1-A). Core inequality |2 Re(conj(a)(-ih) b)| <= 2|h||a||b| checked in Lean.
- A.7: three moments l = 0,1,2, c_0^2 = 1/3, c_1^2 = 4/15, rates (0, kappa_1, kappa_2); R_00 = <0|(s + ikM + D)^{-1}|0> = 1/(s + k^2c_0^2/(s + kappa_1 + k^2 c_1^2/(s + kappa_2))). At k = s = kappa_1 = 1: R_00(kappa_2) = (1 + (1/3)/(2 + (4/15)/(1 + kappa_2)))^{-1}; R_00(1) = 32/37, R_00'(1) = -5/1369; E = k^2 kappa_1^2/3 + (4k^2/15)(kappa_2 - kappa_1)^2 has dE/dkappa_2 = (8k^2/15)(kappa_2 - kappa_1) = 0 there. "Energy stationarity is not response stationarity."
