## Abstract (v12), key claims
H = D + A on a finite graph, Hermitian vertex blocks D_i, unitary edge blocks A_ij. "the Frobenius self-commutator energy (1/8)||[H,H*]||_F^2 is exactly the connection energy CE ||D_i - A_ij D_j A_ij*||_F^2 of the obstruction field D, and (1/8)||H^2 - H*^2||_F^2 is exactly its opposite CE ||D_i + A_ij D_j A_ij*||_F^2. The two are the Hermitian and skew halves of the single product DA: they share the fixed budget 2 CE_i deg(i)||D_i||^2, and their difference is the indefinite trace form 2 Re Tr(DA)^2, whose sign says which half wins." Both books are Born sums (squared jumps (lambda-mu)^2 and squared sums (lambda+mu)^2 weighted by the doubly stochastic Born matrix). On rank-k subspace fields they are 2k(1 -/+ p). Sector sign anticommuting with coupling = quarter turn rX = iUXU*. Zero sets = holonomy-fixed and sign-twisted holonomy-fixed fields; odd loops frustrate the opposite. Balancing both books on every edge of an odd loop: impossible for rank-one fields in 2D, possible in 3D; triangle = one complete measurement (weights sum to one), pentagon = KCBS configuration with weights reaching sqrt5 > non-contextual bound 2. Two-loop Pauli bow ties: least energies 6 - 3sqrt3 and 4 - 2sqrt2. Twisted ring: largest total Born probability (N/2)(1+cos(delta/N)). Twisted Ihara zeta reads the two books at u = +/-1. "Where the trace form vanishes on a real traceless pair the operator is a nonzero nilpotent: the wall of the spinning key, of critical damping and, in an explicit encoding, of Koide's lepton relation, which there reads zeta^2 = 3Q - 1. Fastest asymptotic settling is the one selector found that lands on this wall."

## Section 1: The operator and its two energies [v12 text]
Definitions: G=(V,E) finite, r>=1. D = diag(D_i), D_i = D_i* in C^{r x r}. A* = -A, block-supported on E, A_ii = 0, A_ij = 0 off edges, A_ij unitary on edges, A_ji = -A_ij*. H = D + A. U_ij(X) := A_ij X A_ij* the induced edge transport on End(E); since A_ji = -A_ij* "the central sign cancels and U_ji = U_ij^{-1}, so U is a connection on End(E) in the ordinary sense." D = "obstruction field", A = "the transport".
- (1) E_- = (1/8)||[H,H*]||_F^2 = CE_{{i,j} in E} ||D_i - A_ij D_j A_ij*||_F^2. Proof: H* = D - A so [H,H*] = -2[D,A], [D,A]_ij = D_i A_ij - A_ij D_j = (D_i - A_ij D_j A_ij*) A_ij; unitary invariance + two orientations.
- (2) H normal iff D_i = A_ij D_j A_ij* on every edge, "exactly when D is parallel for the induced connection on End(E)".
- (3) Connected G, base vertex i0: path independence iff [D_i0, W_C] = 0 for every loop C (W_C = product of A_e along C, up to central sign that Ad ignores; U_C = Ad W_C). ker E_A ≅ Fix_{Herm(r)} Hol_Ad A(i0) = {X in Herm(r): W_C X W_C* = X for every loop C}; E_A(D) := (1/8)||[H,H*]||_F^2. Quote: "Hence the Frobenius self-commutator energy of D + A, the standard measure of non-normality [1], is exactly the connection energy measuring the failure of its Hermitian obstruction field to be parallel under the induced transport."
- The opposite: H^2 - H*^2 = (D+A)^2 - (D-A)^2 = 2{D,A}; diagonal blocks of {D,A} vanish; {D,A}_ij = D_i A_ij + A_ij D_j = (D_i + A_ij D_j A_ij*) A_ij.
- (4) E_+ := (1/8)||H^2 - H*^2||_F^2 = CE_{{i,j} in E} ||D_i + A_ij D_j A_ij*||_F^2. Orientation reversal harmless. Notation: E_-^A := E_A when both appear. (Fig. 2: edge read twice; connection energy vanishes when parallel, opposite when anti-parallel; their sum depends only on the two norms.)
- Born reading: D_i = CE_a lambda_a P_a, D_j = CE_b mu_b Q_b (rank-one spectral projectors); W_ab = Tr P_a U_ij(Q_b) = |<a|A_ij|b>|^2, doubly stochastic (rows and columns sum to 1), "the Born probability that the eigenstate |b> of D_j, carried along the edge, is found in the eigenstate |a> of D_i."
- (5) E_e^{-/+} = CE_{a,b} W_ab (lambda_a -/+ mu_b)^2; E_e^- - E_e^+ = -4 CE_{a,b} W_ab lambda_a mu_b. "The connection energy is the Born-weighted squared jump of the eigenvalue across the edge ...; the opposite is the same sum of squared sums; and their difference is minus four times the Born cross-moment of the two spectra [DERIVED]." Needs unitary transport; non-unitary edge map: weights not stochastic, (5) has no probabilistic meaning. (Fig. 1: Born matrix for r=3; quarter turn.)

## Section 2: One product, two halves [v12 text]
X = DA; X* = A*D = -AD. Herm X = (1/2)[D,A], Skew X = (1/2){D,A}. So
- E_-^A = 2||Herm(DA)||_F^2, E_+^A = 2||Skew(DA)||_F^2. Halves orthogonal. ||DA||_F^2 = CE_{i,j}||D_i A_ij||^2 = CE_i deg(i)||D_i||^2.
- (6) BUDGET: E_- + E_+ = 2 CE_{{i,j}} (||D_i||^2 + ||D_j||^2) = 2 CE_i deg(i)||D_i||_F^2 = 2||DA||_F^2. Edge by edge = parallelogram law ||a-b||^2+||a+b||^2 = 2||a||^2+2||b||^2 with a = D_i, b = U_ij(D_j).
- (7) E_- - E_+ = 2 Re Tr(DA)^2 = -4 CE_{{i,j}} Tr(D_i U_ij(D_j)); Re Tr X^2 = ||Herm X||^2 - ||Skew X||^2. Quote: "The budget does not depend on the transport at all. The transport decides only how the fixed amount 2||DA||^2 is shared between the two halves, and the difference (7) is the signed correlation of each block with the transported block at the other end."
- Corollary 1: If H is normal and H^2 is Hermitian, then D_i = 0 at every vertex that has an edge (both energies vanish so budget is zero). [proved in text; Lean eq_zero_of_isStarNormal_of_mul_self_eq]
- Product: E_- E_+ = ||DA||_F^4 - (Re Tr(DA)^2)^2; vanishes exactly when one of the two is empty. (Lean connectionEnergy_mul_oppositeEnergy.)
- Sector flip: q(X) = (1/2) Re Tr X^2 = (1/2)(||Herm X||^2 - ||Skew X||^2) "says which half wins, and one operation reverses it." r = r*, r^2 = I a sign (+1 on one sector, -1 on other). X = A (crossing, rAr = -A) + B (inside, rBr = B). Tr(AB)=0.
- (8) Herm(rA) = r Skew A, Skew(rA) = r Herm A, Tr X^2 + Tr(rX)^2 = 2 Tr B^2. Crossing coupling alone: flip exchanges the two halves exactly, keeps budget and self-commutator, reverses trace form.
- (9) rA = i U A U*, U = e^{-i pi r/4}: "a unitary change of frame followed by multiplication by i, which rotates the spectrum by a right angle [DERIVED]." "What the flip cannot move is the coupling inside a sector: 2 Tr B^2 is exactly how far the reversal falls short. This is a signed quantity, a size only when B is Hermitian." Bipartite with r = +/-1 on two sides: every edge crosses, B = 0, (8) becomes the sign flip (12).

## Section 3: Zero sets and why odd loops frustrate [v12 text]

- (11) ker E_+ ≅ {X in Herm(r): (-1)^{l(C)} W_C X W_C* = X for every loop C at i0}. Even loop asks what connection energy asks; odd loop asks W_C X W_C* = -X. "an odd loop with no twist admits no anti-parallel field at all. The sign is a frustration in the sense of signed graphs, carried here by every odd cycle." Opposite energy = connection energy of sign-twisted transport X -> -U_ij(X), loop of length l picks up (-1)^l.
- (12) Bipartite (sides S,T): (sigma D)_i = D_i on S, -D_i on T gives E_-(sigma D) = E_+(D), E_+(sigma D) = E_-(D); "on bipartite graphs the two energies carry the same information. The difference between them lives in the odd cycles."

With fields S,T on G, F_(i,+/-) = S_i +/- T_i: E_-^{~A}(F) = 2E_-^A(S) + 2E_+^A(T). "The two energies are the even and the odd half of one connection energy on the double cover."

A matched edge (d_i = d_j) has no connection energy, and the opposite carries everything."

## Section 9: The wall [v12 text]
