## The connected result (page 1)
"a present appearance can be an incomplete account of what an interaction has left behind. The missing distinction may be spectral composition, relative phase, material structure, ordered transport, melodic approach, or position in a rhythmic cycle." Examples: two gray reservoirs with identical current brightness decay differently; two optical preparations with identical local populations differ after the same mixer; a pitch histogram preserves counts but discards melodic order. "None of these statements identifies brightness with time, a signed occupation statistic with gravity, or musical richness with entropy." Gray-decay tests: positive mixtures of independent exponentials have nonnegative log curvature equal to variance of rates; passive defective transfer chain has the opposite sign; static nonlinear sensor can bend without storing state; "Equal present brightness followed by different futures is the stronger discriminator; curvature alone is insufficient." Turkish music "supplies an essential part of the question, rather than a decorative example". The original exchange explaining "ancient richer Turkish music" "has not been recovered."

## 2 Mathematics of an incomplete present (all statements are derivations in the text, no Lean grade; the Weight Between v2.3 later attributes Lean to them)
- State z, reading y=Rz, evolution z_dot = Lz. Two preparations differing by δ look identical now when Rδ=0; distinguishable futures under same evolution exactly when Rδ=0 and R e^{Lt} δ ≠ 0 for some admitted t (eq 1). "an exact mathematical witness of missing predictive information, not proof of a new microscopic force."
- Cayley–Hamilton: permanently invisible subspace N = ∩_{k=0}^{n-1} ker(R L^k) (eq 2). Present reading sufficient iff N = ker R, equivalently L ker R ⊆ ker R (eq 3). Proof sketch given (invariant kernel stays invisible; conversely differentiate at zero gives R L δ = 0; reduced generator on range of R).
- "The quotient V/N identifies exactly those states with the same complete future readout. It is the smallest sufficient linear predictive description for unrestricted preparations." Any sufficient linear F: ker F ⊆ N, rank F >= dim(V/N). "a minimality statement within the stated linear problem, not a claim about every nonlinear or biological memory."
- Intervention family: N_U = ∩_{u in U} ker(R T_u) (eq 4); include identity protocol; closure under concatenation guarantees invariance.
- Quantum case: linear state space Hermitian matrices, L ρ = -i[H,ρ]/ħ, R = partial trace or list of expectations.
- 2.2 Memory kernel after reduction: x_dot = Ax+By, y_dot=Cx+Dy (eq 5); x_dot(t) = A x + B e^{Dt} y(0) + ∫_0^t B e^{D(t-s)} C x(s) ds (eq 6).
