## Abstract claims (his)
Dominant right singular direction v1(N) of Frobenius-normalized cumulative transfer matrix, spectral stress Phi, and curvature of the spectral projector. Exact order-2 limit theorem (v1 -> Casoratian dual of the minimal solution); convergence criterion (v1 converges iff dominant mode simple and strictly dominant; ties among non-dominant modes immaterial); exact multiplicativity under Kronecker products; for polynomial continued fraction limiting slope = value of fraction, Phi_inf = K^2/(1+K^2); intrinsic gauge-free Casoratian pairing conserved by any invertible transport; second CMF direction for Problem 2.5's Meijer-G function, connection exactly flat, but "Hermitian Berry curvature vanishes identically for structural reasons, but the biorthogonal connection appropriate to a non-normal transport carries a nonzero, gauge-invariant curvature, validated against an exact sum rule and confirmed by an independent sqrt2/n^2 drift law." Calibrated against all eight challenge recurrences + Apery zeta(3). Two earlier claims corrected: Problem 2.2's regime misassigned (recurrence is inverse transpose of trajectory); previously reported 0.14% residual is "a stable invariant of the reverse-ordered product". Spectral gap predicts convergence across CMF trajectory directions within 1% and ranks the challenge's own trajectory optimal per unit work. Thanks to the Ramanujan Machine Challenge organizers for releasing proofs; "several of the corrections below - including two to earlier claims of our own - were found by testing against their proofs."
