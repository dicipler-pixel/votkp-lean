## Abstract (his claims)
Three separated quantities: instantaneous screening of target state; yield-equivalent value within one acquisition interval; global constant-model fit to an energy sweep. Order of energies + fitted normalization shifts constant fit "above the endpoint for a high-to-low sweep and below the starting value for a low-to-high sweep". Drift models identifiable but severely ill-conditioned in a single sweep; a sweep returning over same energies reduces start-value error four- to fivefold at fixed fluence. Protocol: charge-normalized, rapidly interleaved two-energy measurements at controlled accumulated fluence; budget ~7.2 eV per dose step. Third interleaved energy = conditional curvature test, expected separation ~4 sigma from homogeneous target for 150 eV spread, cannot by itself separate screening spread from additive thermal contribution. "The paper reports no new nuclear data; it is a measurement-design analysis with reproducible calculations."
