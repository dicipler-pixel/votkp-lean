## Abstract (his claims, paraphrase kept close)
- Garrett's tree: six Lipschitz quaternions of norm 5, 1 +- 2i, 1 +- 2j, 1 +- 2k; each step multiplies norm by 5.
- Peel coordinate of VOTKP: a step multiplying a ratio by z has Smith coordinate Gamma = (z-1)/(z+1), hyperbolic length ln z, steps compose by Einstein addition. Norm-5 step: Gamma = 2/3, length ln 5.
- Each generator -> Lorentz boost of rapidity ln 5 along its own imaginary axis; conjugate -> inverse boost. Places tree in Poincare ball with true hyperbolic distances.
- "Over the reals the unit quaternion (1 + 2u)/sqrt5 acts as a rotation, not a boost; the boost is the peel-coordinate reading of the norm step, and its length ln 5 is the length of one edge of the 5-adic tree."
- cosh ln 5 = 13/5, sinh ln 5 = 12/5, rational coordinates. Exact facts: straight paths add lengths; one right-angle turn gives cosh d = cosh^2(ln 5); straight path is farthest among words of its length; two perpendicular steps leave a Thomas rotation with cos theta = 65/97 and tan(theta/2) = Gamma^2 = 4/9.
- For every word length, 5^L cosh d never divisible by 5 => no non-backtracking word returns to origin, three boosts generate a free group. Same residue argument for every integer z>=2 not a power of 2; ping-pong at prime 1+i of Gaussian integers covers every even ratio; so free for every whole-number ratio. Garrett's own drawing uses ln 7; caption numbers (3 ln 7 = 5.838; 4.742 for word i j i) come out exactly.
- "The line Gamma^2 = 1/2 divides two behaviours sharply." Above (ln 7, Gamma = 3/4): Schottky group, tree sits in ball with distances kept in proportion. Below (ln 5, Gamma = 2/3): commutator of two perpendicular steps is a rotation through an angle that is no rational part of a turn. "So the norm-5 group is free but not discrete, and its placement does not keep distances in proportion: the word (i j i-bar j-bar)^n has 4n letters yet never lands more than 3.5255 from the centre."
- Knot side: Riley meridians of a two-bridge knot on its real peripheral curve are boosts of the Smith disk of length 2x; longitude is a boost of length 2|y|; knot relation closes the word walk into a loop. Trefoil and figure-eight relations factor through Riley polynomials over any commutative ring.
- Relator lifts to identity in universal cover, but area of the drawn relator polygon depends on base point; vanishes on a whole geodesic (reflection swaps the meridians, maps polygon to itself with orientation reversed).
- tr[a,b] = 2 - v((s-1/s)^2 - v): rotation only when meridian axes cross, and "below the same line Gamma^2 = 1/2, always when they do". T(2,3), T(2,5), T(2,7): axes never cross (proved). Census of 26 two-bridge knots: nineteen have rotating commutator on some real branch, seven never.
- Boost group at ln 5 not conjugate to the Lubotzky-Phillips-Sarnak group, though both act simply transitively on the same 5-adic tree. Distance constants at ln 7 proved and optimal; ping-pong at ln 7 checked in Lean.
- "One hundred and fifty-nine statements are checked in core Lean 4 without Mathlib, in five files."

## Sec 1 Introduction / "A plain statement of what is chosen"
- Garrett's X post [1] gives caption "3 ln 7 = 5.838 along a straight path and 4.742 along a path with turns". Question: is the drawing only a picture?
- Tool: peel coordinate of VOTKP [2] (What Survives the Peel, doi 10.5281/zenodo.23011140; Theorem 8.1, Theorem 15.1, Corollary 15.2, Sec 15.2).
- Placement is a choice, not forced by the group: "the length of a step is the logarithm of its norm ratio; its direction is the quaternion's own axis." Three consequences: (1) real unit quaternion (1+2u)/sqrt5 acts as rotation; boost is what the peel coordinate makes of the norm step, length ln 5 agrees with one edge of the 5-adic (Bruhat-Tits) tree; (2) in the tree's own metric every reduced 3-step path has length 3 edges, "the fact that a turned path is shorter than a straight one is a property of the placement, not of the tree"; (3) LPS freeness of the quaternion group does not by itself make the boost group free; proved separately as Thm 5.1.
- The boost of a product quaternion is not the boost along the product's axis (two boosts compose to boost then rotation, Sec 4.4): the map respects word structure, not quaternion multiplication.

## Sec 2 The peel coordinate
- Def 2.1: Gamma(z) = (z-1)/(z+1) for z>0. Einstein sum on (-1,1): x (+) y = (x+y)/(1+xy).
- Thm 2.2 (VOTKP Thm 8.1): z = e^tau => Gamma(z) = tanh(tau/2). [proved here] (Mathlib draft Smith.lean: smith_identity.)
- Thm 2.3 (VOTKP Thm 15.1): Gamma(A) (+) Gamma(B) = Gamma(AB), A,B>=0; equivalently (+) is addition of rapidities. [Lean: gam_mul]. Proof: numerator 2(AB-1), denominator 2(AB+1) over (A+1)(B+1).
- Cor 2.4 (VOTKP Cor 15.2): on the Poincare disk the distance from 0 to Gamma(z) is 2 artanh Gamma(z) = ln z. [proved here from 2.2]
- Thm 2.5 (norm ladder): Gamma_L = L copies of 2/3 combined by (+): Gamma_L = (5^L - 1)/(5^L + 1), d(0,Gamma_L) = L ln 5. [Lean: peel_ladder, induction]. Euler four-square identity (Lean norm_mul) gives norm 5^L for a word of length L, so Garrett's norm ladder is exactly the peel ladder.
- Thm 2.6 (boost entries from Gamma): cosh ln z = (1+Gamma^2)/(1-Gamma^2) = (z^2+1)/(2z); sinh ln z = 2Gamma/(1-Gamma^2) = (z^2-1)/(2z). [Lean: cosh_from_gam, sinh_from_gam]. z=5: 13/5, 12/5; z=7: 25/7, 24/7. z times the boost has integer entries.
