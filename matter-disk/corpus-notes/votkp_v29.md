## PART I
### 1 Central question
Three scales: matter (fermionic exclusion, spacings, couplings, geometry set response of a surface when specimen changed); elements (does shell structure reappear in how measured records change under controlled peels?); knots (can topology, field loading, geometry, response be measured as distinct layers; which changes under each peel?). Expanded element question: how structure persists and changes, how energy and coupling participate, how a transformation changes conditions for the next. "Electron removal preserves the nuclear proton number while changing charge, occupation, correlations and the next removal requirement. Excitation and nuclear decay use different state and product ledgers. Sections 20 and 21 keep these distinctions." Evidence convention: E evaluated, S semi-empirical, T theoretical; "Evaluated is not synonymous with detector-level raw measurement"; "Missing uncertainty is not zero, and independent numerical stress tests do not create a missing covariance matrix."
### 2 What a peel is
"A peel is operational, not a cartoon of spherical shells." Can be: remove an electron, change oxidation state, attenuate a channel, eliminate hidden coordinates, thin a film, change outside dielectric, cross a pressure boundary, change a crossing, band move, change field numerator, Dehn-fill a cusp. Classes: channel peel (fixed response directions lose weight), coordinate peel (hidden coordinates eliminated exactly), physical peel (specimen changes). Knot moves declared by what they preserve. Evidence levels 0-4 (detector counts ... theory variables incl. peel coordinate, spectral stress); data grades A-D. Data sources: NIST ASD 5.12 (Nov 2024) via mendeleev 1.3.0; search pass 2024-2026 literature Z<=80 found no new ground-state threshold (one new excited-level helium IE, Clausen 2025, not a rung); polarizabilities Schwerdtfeger-Nagle 2025 edition; electron affinities Ning-Lu 2022 plus Sc, Y (Zhang 2023), Cl (Maier 2025) (Sc, Y, Cr entries move up to 0.010 eV); covalent radii Cordero 2008.
