## Abstract (his, key sentences)
- "We ask one question across five subjects: what must the present keep in order to predict the future we admit, and what does it cost to move what it keeps?"
- First half "has a machine-checked answer in finite dimensions: the dirac-time Lean library proves that two states agree on every future reading exactly when they agree on the first dimV readings, that every sufficient linear summary has rank at least the dimension of the predictive quotient, and that the present reading closes exactly when its kernel is invariant."
- "the 'weight between' a kept sector and the rest is one quantity, the cross-sector coupling energy E_B = ||P H (1-P)||_F^2, and ... it appears, with different powers of the gap, as projector geometry (memory), as static response (light), as energy variance (the speed of time), as the friction charged for rotating a record, and, at the top of the ladder, as the non-normality (connection) energy of the coupled operator."
- Main result: "a sharp speed limit of Mandelstam–Tamm type for the signed occupation offset tau = Tr[log(I-C) - log C] of a cut: tau_dot^2 <= 4 g Tr[(C(I-C))^-1], with equality on an explicit trajectory."
- Scalar cut (one accessible, one hidden mode): bound, exact gap 4gS - tau_dot^2 = 4(Im b̄ḃ)^2/(c(1-c))^2 and equality case are machine-checked in a LOCAL Lean build not in a public repo. General cut: algebraic core machine-checked "conditional on the trace-derivative step tau_dot = Re Tr(W C_dot) (with W = -(C(I-C))^-1), and the rest is a paper proof."
- Combined with Lean-checked identities: |tau_dot| <= 2 sqrt(E_B S) ("a record moves only as fast as the weight between allows") and, under the proposed relaxation model, tau_dot^2 <= 4 S zeta / w_min ("a record moves only as fast as one pays in friction").
- Colour "enters through the scale/shape split and through one map, tanh(1/2 · log-odds), that is proved separately in four of the author's public Lean libraries at pinned commits (a fifth copy is listed without a commit)." Music "as organised history: a present note does not determine its continuation." Also "a corrected Rule 30 result: the overdispersion of branch splits is entirely explained by exactly merged histories, and the earlier 'colour steers the bulk' gain vanishes when merged rows are counted once." "Every claim carries an evidence grade. Mathematics is separated from physical reading throughout; the physical readings are proposals."

## 1 Thesis
Two questions: (1) What must be kept? (2) What does keeping cost? Q1 answered "exactly, in finite dimensions, by observability theory ... machine-checked in the author's dirac-time-lean". Thesis (verbatim): "The weight between a kept sector and its complement is the cross-sector coupling energy E_B = ||PH(1-P)||_F^2. Read at different powers of the gap it is memory geometry, optical response, the speed of time, the friction of a record, and the non-normality of the coupled operator. Every bound in this paper is a statement that one of these cannot exceed what the weight between allows." "The thesis is part theorem and part proposal ... the claim that the five subjects are 'one' is an organising reading [ANALOGY / OPEN] supported by the identities, not a further theorem."

 Proof (his): C and I-C commute, d/dt Tr log(I-C) - d/dt Tr log C = -Tr[((I-C)^-1 + C^-1) C_dot] = Tr(W C_dot) (trace differentiation does not need C, C_dot to commute). With C_dot = E† P_dot E, tau_dot = Tr(F P_dot). Differentiating P^2=P shows P_dot is a tangent, hence off-diagonal, so Tr(F P_dot) = Tr(T P_dot) with T = PF(I-P)+(I-P)FP = [P,[P,F]]. Cauchy–Schwarz for real inner product Re Tr(XY) on Hermitian matrices: tau_dot^2 <= ||T||^2 ||P_dot||^2 = 2g||T||^2. Finally ||T||^2 = 2Tr(PF^2 - PFPF) = 2Tr(C W^2 - C W C W) = 2Tr(W^2 C(I-C)) = -2Tr W = 2S, using [W,C]=0 and C(I-C) = -W^-1. Equality in C–S is P_dot = λT; T off-diagonal so [P,[P,T]] = T, so -i[iλ[T,P],P] = λT.
 Relation to known speed limits: "a Cauchy–Schwarz bound of Mandelstam–Tamm type [20]. For rank-one P (which forces m=1) it is exactly the observable speed limit |d<F>/dt| <= 2 ΔF ΔH [Mohan–Pati; García-Pintos et al.] [COMPUTED: identity_check_v2_2.py]. What is new is the identity ||[P,[P,F]]||_F^2 = 2 Tr[(C(I-C))^-1] and its use for the signed offset."
