## Abstract claims
Axiom = law of an oscillator; wheel-shaped "cosmic pendulum" chart "is, exactly, an oscillator's phase portrait"; rule that streams flowing the same way join and against part "is Ampere's law of currents", image = Faraday-Maxwell stress. "His planet laws are wrong for the solar system. Taken together, though, they describe one consistent world, the world of an inverse-cube force, in which every orbit spirals. That world exists: general relativity reaches it at six gravitational radii from any compact mass." Errors from three habits: grouping all attractions into one column, carrying laws of a rigid wheel into free orbits, replacing gravity's pull with buoyancy. "Seven of his original charts are reproduced beside corrected redrawings. Every number is recomputed by scripts shipped with the paper, and the mathematical steps are checked in Lean."
