\section{The Lean files}
Two separately built suites check the algebraic core.

\paragraph{VOTKP.MatterDisk.} Module \texttt{VOTKP.MatterDisk} of the repository \texttt{dicipler-pixel/votkp-lean} (Lean 4 with Mathlib v4.34.1). Its twelve theorems are: \texttt{bridge}, \texttt{census\_square}, \texttt{census\_complement} (A.3, A.6); \texttt{rapidities\_add} (A.8); \texttt{topleft\_sq}, \texttt{idempotent\_cross} (A.5, A.6); \texttt{exit\_line} (A.14); \texttt{outside\_rim}, \texttt{rim\_distance}, \texttt{cayley\_pole} (A.12); \texttt{saha\_ratio} (A.11); \texttt{last\_step} (A.10). The build record is GitHub Actions run 41729732315 at commit \texttt{0e5792a} (152 theorems across the repository; the axiom audit finds only \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}). False controls live in \texttt{FalseControls/}, outside the positive build globs, and are run separately, where each must fail on a mathematical goal.

\paragraph{MatterGas.} The formal suite of the 3 November development: 34 positive declarations compiled under Lean 4.34.1 and Mathlib commit \texttt{d13f23b723b8a846827a245b89c10fc7d3f11612}, with no \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, custom axiom or \texttt{native\_decide}; five false controls (a common scale changes the Cayley ratio; disconnected thermal variance is zero; the maximum covariance defect is at most $1/5$; the first three moments fix the third; mean charge rises with electron density) are rejected on unsolved mathematical goals. Coverage: \texttt{Boundary} gives the defect split, positivity, $0\le BB^*\le A-A^2\le I/4$, the projector reduction, the disconnected zero and the trace-zero criterion (Theorem~\ref{thm:weight}), and the complex identity $1-4\nu(1-\nu)=(1-2\nu)^2$ with the idempotent embedding $\mathcal P(A)$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:open}); \texttt{Scalar} gives Cayley scale invariance, bounds, inverse and anchored reconstruction (Theorem~\ref{thm:anchor}); \texttt{Saha} gives three-charge uniqueness, monotone mean charge and the population cancellations (Theorems~\ref{thm:saha}, \ref{thm:inverse}); \texttt{Witness} gives the $m=3$ moment witness, with weights $(7,11,5,9)/32$, a different amplitude of the same construction as the text's $(3,7,1,5)/16$. Ordered spectral comparisons, stability, the information bounds, general-$Z$ Saha, the response laws, the kinetic witness and the Petermann statements are proved by hand in this paper and are not machine-checked.

\section{Scripts and outputs}
Every script below is printed whole, followed by its verbatim output. They run with Python 3, numpy, scipy, sympy and matplotlib; the nuclear script also needs pynucastro (for the NUBASE2020 file). Repository: \texttt{dicipler-pixel/votkp-lean}, folder \texttt{matter-disk/}. Scripts \texttt{s06}, \texttt{s07}, \texttt{s08}, \texttt{s11}, \texttt{s12} and \texttt{s13} carry a kill condition written before their run; the others are observational scans or figure builders and carry none. \texttt{s03c} is the raw metric scan that \texttt{s03d} repeats with a rank check. The \texttt{g} scripts are the formal development's own checks, rerun for this edition from \texttt{external/gpt\_formal\_20261103}; the gas checks follow a protocol fixed before their execution. \texttt{s09} reads the formal package from a local checkout path and its printed output is the record of that run.
