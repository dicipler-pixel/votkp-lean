With unit v_a, v_b, gauge <v_a|v_b> = cos(alpha/2) real >= 0 (alpha = Bloch angle between the two fixed directions):
  d_n (chordal distance on the unit sphere to the attracting point) = 2 sin(alpha/2) |w_n| / sqrt(1 + |w_n|^2 + 2 cos(alpha/2) Re w_n).

Why the earlier 4-pass fit missed (3e-2, 5e-3, 1e-4): the exact d_n carries the factor 1/sqrt(1+|w_n|^2+2cos(alpha/2)Re w_n), which is not constant until |w_n| << 1; fitting log d_n against n over passes 1-4 absorbs that drift into the slope. ## 3. Exit line against the loop
Map EXISTS. Facts (z = 1.5 ... 206.768, 60 digits):
- c with log(-W) = c.sigma, c = a + i b: a.b = 0 at every z tested (residual <= 5e-56): since tr W is real, c.c is real, i.e. Im(c.c) = 2 a.b = 0. So a real rotation of axes ALWAYS brings c.sigma to (real, real, imaginary) with real part orthogonal to imaginary part (b along z, a in xy), for all z>1. (b=0 would be Hermitian h, only at z=1.) 
- Which log: use -W (same Moebius map, same eigenvectors, same K). With W itself the quasi-energy is theta/2 and the wall is at E=pi, not an exceptional point; with -W, E' = pi - theta/2 -> 0 at the wall and the wall (W+1 nilpotent, rank 1) is exactly h's exceptional point u = |v+we^{ik}|.
- Scale: inside the wall |a| < |b| so the factor i is needed (H = i log(-W), the Floquet reading); past the wall |a| > |b| and the factor is 1. 
- Map: u = |b-part|, |v + w e^{ik}| = |a-part| after the swap, E^2 = |v+we^{ik}|^2 - u^2.
  Inside (tested, residual 1e-56..1e-61): E = 2 arccos(Gamma^2/(1-Gamma^2)); u = E sinh(eta); |v+we^{ik}| = E cosh(eta); cosh^2(eta) = 1/(1-2Gamma^2), sinh^2 eta = 2Gamma^2/(1-2Gamma^2). So u/E = sqrt2 Gamma / sqrt(1-2Gamma^2).
  Past the wall: E = lambda/2 = 2 arccosh(Gamma^2/(1-Gamma^2)); u^2/E^2 = 1/(2Gamma^2-1); |v+we^{ik}|^2/E^2 = 2Gamma^2/(2Gamma^2-1).
  Numerics (z: u, |v+we^ik|, E): z=3: 2.4619, 3.4817, 2.4619; z=5: 3.6402, 3.8610, 1.2870; z=8: 4.3214, 4.7533, 1.9797 (in the scale where the loop's own log is used; overall real scale s is free).
- Only u and the modulus |v+we^{ik}| are fixed by z; (v, w, k) enter only through v^2+w^2+2vw cos k, so a one-parameter-plus family of (v,w,k) gives the same matrix up to similarity. The map is to h(k) at ONE k (or w=0).
- Petermann: K_h = 1 + u^2/E^2 equals K_W (biorthogonal, computed independently from W's eigenvectors) to <= 1e-59 relative at all 13 z, INSIDE and PAST the wall (z=3: 2 = 2; z=5: 9 = 9; z=5.8: 289 = 289; past: 52.97 at 5.99, 14.235 at 6.5, 2.0398 at 206.768). Inside K = cosh^2 eta = 1/(1-2Gamma^2). Past the wall K = 2Gamma^2/(2Gamma^2-1).
  The brief's closed formula K = |a|^2/(|a|^2-|b|^2) holds only for |a|>|b| (real E); it is that formula after the factor-i swap, which is why the pairing above is the right one (it applied unswapped to log W inside gives a negative number).
