Abstract

VOTKP began as the volume of the knot potential, an attempt to read the volume of the space around a knot from its polynomials. It then became the vector of traits kept under peeling. This paper joins the two meanings through the two books of the intrinsic quantum geometric tensor Q=g−i2ΩQ = g - \tfrac{i}{2}\Omega: a climb (metric, size) and a turn (curvature, phase). The reading is set on a Smith-type disk whose centre is the vacuum and whose rim is ground.

  • The vacuum disk. Every ideal tetrahedron of a hyperbolic knot complement is placed on a disk centred on the regular tetrahedron. On that disk:
    • the three ways of reading a tetrahedron are exact 120∘120^\circ turns, which is the tennis-racket switch: the axis changes, the volume does not;
    • flat tetrahedra lie on the rim, and mirrors lie outside it.
  • Decay. A tetrahedron’s volume is a bowl around the vacuum, with the exact series D=V0−334|G|2+38(G3+G‾3)+3316|G|4+O(|G|5)D = V_0 - \tfrac{3\sqrt3}{4}|G|^2 + \tfrac{\sqrt3}{8}(G^3+\bar G^3) + \tfrac{3\sqrt3}{16}|G|^4 + O(|G|^5). Summed over the pieces, with nothing fitted, it gives the volume of 12,954 knots to a median error of 0.076%.
  • Direction. The Chern–Simons twist lives in integer windings. With the pieces read in a branching order it is reproduced exactly (454 of 454 runs).
  • Our polynomial. The knot potential read straight off a drawing gives one (decay, direction) root per geometry.
    • On 79 of 79 hyperbolic knots, its top root is the knot’s complex volume.
    • Its spectrum is the same from different drawings (13 of 13 knots tested).
    • The true geometry is always the root nearest the vacuum (31 of 31).
    • Torus knots give pure-direction roots: zero decay, a rational fraction of a turn. The knots that assemble themselves in chemistry are exactly of this kind.
  • Supporting lines. Light strings (Jones, HOMFLY along its sl(N) lines), the colour of the elements on the same disk, and the electron’s group SL(2,ℂ)\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C) complete the chain.
  • Status. Reproductions of known mathematics, new readings and new results are marked throughout and listed in Section 12.

1. Where this comes from

Three papers feed this one.

  • IQGT (Intrinsic Quantum Geometric Tensor, v7.3.1). For a projector path, the tensor Q=g−i2ΩQ = g - \tfrac{i}{2}\Omega splits into two books.
    • The metric g=12Tr(VW)g = \tfrac12\mathrm{Tr}(VW) measures how far a state moves: the climb.
    • The curvature Ω=+iTr(Π[V,W])=−2ImQ\Omega = +i\,\mathrm{Tr}(\Pi[V,W]) = -2\,\mathrm{Im}\,Q measures how it turns: the turn.
    • A loop that comes back still remembers, through its phase (IQGT Ex. 6.5).
    • The Smith coordinate Γ=(z−1)/(z+1)=tanh(12lnz)\Gamma = (z-1)/(z+1) = \tanh(\tfrac12\ln z) puts any impedance-like quantity on a disk: the centre Γ=0\Gamma=0 is the matched point (vacuum) and the rim |Γ|=1|\Gamma|=1 is total reflection (ground).
  • Dirac Time (Dirac Time of the Gigantefermion, v3.1). Torus knots, their arcs and signatures, and the chiral pairing ΓP+Γ=P−\Gamma P_+\Gamma = P_-. The torus knots return here as the pure-direction roots.
  • VOTKP (Elemental Peeling, v2.9). Peeling kept a vector of traits, Levine–Tristram walls and colour; v2.9 Table 6 has 575 double roots. Its first meaning, the volume of the knot potential, mixing HOMFLY and coloured Jones to map volume, is the question this paper answers.

The tennis racket. A body spun about its middle axis flips over and over (the Dzhanibekov effect). The angular momentum keeps its size and its direction in space. What builds up is a turn, a geometric phase set by the area the switching sweeps (Montgomery). In an earlier part of this research the climb rate of that switch matched the Connection Energy formula (0.577350), and the memory was a lune 4arctank4\arctan k that reaches 45∘45^\circ when the middle level sits midway. The rule the axis switches, the direction stays is the organising rule of everything below.

2. The vacuum disk for knot pieces

A hyperbolic knot complement is glued from ideal tetrahedra. Each has a shape zz with Imz>0\mathrm{Im}\,z>0, a cross-ratio of its four ideal vertices. Its volume is the Bloch–Wigner dilogarithm D(z)=ImLi2(z)+arg(1−z)log|z|.D(z) = \mathrm{Im}\,\mathrm{Li}_2(z) + \arg(1-z)\,\log|z| . Let w=eiπ/3w = e^{i\pi/3}, the shape of the regular ideal tetrahedron, with V0=D(w)=1.0149416064…V_0 = D(w) = 1.0149416064\ldots, the largest possible. Define the vacuum disk G(z)=z−wz−w‾.G(z) = \frac{z-w}{z-\bar w}.

Proposition 1 (vacuum, rim, mirror, racket).

  1. G(w)=0G(w)=0: the regular tetrahedron is the vacuum point.

  2. Real zz (a flat tetrahedron, D=0D=0) gives |G|=1|G|=1: flat pieces lie on the rim.

  3. The mirror shape z‾\bar z gives |G(z‾)|=1/|G(z)||G(\bar z)| = 1/|G(z)|: mirrors lie outside the disk.

  4. The axis switch T(z)=1/(1−z)T(z) = 1/(1-z), which relabels the tetrahedron’s edges (z→z′→z″z\to z'\to z''), acts on GG as an exact rotation, G(Tz)=e2πi/3G(z)G(T z) = e^{2\pi i/3}\,G(z), while D(Tz)=D(z)D(Tz)=D(z).

Proof.

    1. is immediate.
  • (b): for real zz, |z−w|=|z−w‾||z-w| = |z-\bar w|, because w‾\bar w is the reflection of ww in the real line.
  • (c): G(z‾)=(z‾−w)/(z‾−w‾)=(z−w‾)/(z−w)¯=G(z)¯−1G(\bar z) = (\bar z - w)/(\bar z-\bar w) = \overline{(z-\bar w)/(z-w)} = \overline{G(z)}^{-1}.
  • (d), step 1: TT is a Möbius map of order 3 whose fixed points are the roots of z2−z+1=0z^2-z+1=0, namely ww and w‾\bar w.
  • (d), step 2: a Möbius map with fixed points w,w‾w,\bar w becomes, in the coordinate GG that sends w↦0w\mapsto 0 and w‾↦∞\bar w\mapsto\infty, multiplication by its multiplier at ww.
  • (d), step 3: that multiplier is T′(w)=1/(1−w)2=1/e−2iπ/3=e2πi/3T'(w) = 1/(1-w)^2 = 1/e^{-2i\pi/3} = e^{2\pi i/3}.

3. Decay: the vacuum bowl

Theorem 2 (bowl series). Near the vacuum point, D(z)=V0−334|G|2+38(G3+G‾3)+3316|G|4+O(|G|5).D(z) = V_0 - \frac{3\sqrt3}{4}|G|^2 + \frac{\sqrt3}{8}\left(G^3+\bar G^3\right) + \frac{3\sqrt3}{16}|G|^4 + O(|G|^5). Status: derived symbolically (sympy expansion of DD in the coordinate GG) and confirmed numerically. (V0−D)/|G|2→1.2990(V_0-D)/|G|^2 \to 1.2990 against 33/4=1.299043\sqrt3/4 = 1.29904, and the residual scales as |G|5|G|^5.

What each term means.

  • The quadratic term is a round bowl: volume drops with squared distance from the vacuum.
  • The cubic term is the racket written exactly. It has the three-fold symmetry of Proposition 1(d) and nothing else.
  • The quartic term is the next radial correction.

The knot law (nothing fitted). A knot’s volume is the sum of its pieces, Vol(K)=∑jD(zj)≈∑j[V0−334|Gj|2+…].\mathrm{Vol}(K) = \sum_j D(z_j) \approx \sum_j \Big[V_0 - \tfrac{3\sqrt3}{4}|G_j|^2 + \dots\Big].

4. Every geometry, and the one nearest the vacuum

A knot’s gluing equations (edges plus meridian completeness) have more than one solution. Besides the true hyperbolic structure there are its mirror, flat solutions and Galois-conjugate ones. Each is a different way of putting a geometry on the knot.

Method. All solutions were found by damped Newton iteration from 3,000 random starts.

5. Direction: the twist lives in integers

The complex volume Vol+iCS\mathrm{Vol} + i\,\mathrm{CS} has a second half, the Chern–Simons invariant.

Null result first. In the experiments it was not found in the angles of the pieces: the summed three-fold angle of GG against CS gives ρ=0.02\rho = 0.02.

Neumann’s formula. Give each tetrahedron log-parameters w0=logz+pπi,w1=logz′+qπi,w2=−w0−w1,z′=11−z,w_0 = \log z + p\pi i,\quad w_1 = \log z' + q\pi i,\quad w_2 = -w_0-w_1, \qquad z' = \tfrac1{1-z}, with integers p,qp,q (the windings) chosen so that every edge and both cusp curves have total log-parameter 00 (a flattening; Zickert, Def. 6.1). Then i(Vol+iCS)=∑jεjR(zj;pj,qj),i(\mathrm{Vol}+i\,\mathrm{CS}) = \sum_j \varepsilon_j\,R(z_j;p_j,q_j), R(z;p,q)=Li2(z)+12logzlog(1−z)+πi2(qlogz+plog(1−z))−π26.R(z;p,q) = \mathrm{Li}_2(z) + \tfrac12\log z\log(1-z) + \tfrac{\pi i}{2}\big(q\log z + p\log(1-z)\big) - \tfrac{\pi^2}{6}.

Results.

  • The integer system was solved exactly (PARI matsolvemod) for 79 hyperbolic knots of 4–9 crossings.
  • Volume needs no integers: it matches SnapPy to 5×10−145\times10^{-14}.
  • Chern–Simons, with the windings, first matched only up to ±π2/6\pm\pi^2/6 on 54 knots.
    • The cause: RR is not invariant under relabelling a tetrahedron. R(z′;q,r)−R(z;p,q)=(1+3q)π2/6R(z';q,r)-R(z;p,q) = (1+3q)\pi^2/6.
    • The fix: read every tetrahedron in a branching order (a vertex order with order-preserving gluings, found on all 79). Odd permutations invert the shape and negate the integers (ε=−1\varepsilon=-1).
    • With that, CS is exact in 454 of 454 runs (79 knots × up to 6 branchings), to 2.7×10−142.7\times10^{-14}, modulo SnapPy’s period π2/2\pi^2/2.
  • The windings are essential. With them dropped, the continuous part alone matches 1 of 79.

Reading. The two books separate in the knot’s own geometry. Decay (volume) is continuous: distance from the vacuum. Direction (twist) is integer: how many half-turns each piece’s phase winds. This is the IQGT split Q=g−i2ΩQ = g-\tfrac i2\Omega again, and it is the phase winding the author’s work began with.

Status: reproduction of Neumann’s theorem; the branching fix is standard. The two-book reading is ours.

6. Our polynomial, read off the drawing

The potential. Cho–Kim–Kim (2013) define a potential of a knot diagram.

  • One variable zkz_k per side; each crossing with sides (a,b,c,d)(a,b,c,d) adds Li2(zbza)−Li2(zbzc)+Li2(zdzc)−Li2(zdza).\mathrm{Li}_2\!\left(\tfrac{z_b}{z_a}\right)-\mathrm{Li}_2\!\left(\tfrac{z_b}{z_c}\right)+\mathrm{Li}_2\!\left(\tfrac{z_d}{z_c}\right)-\mathrm{Li}_2\!\left(\tfrac{z_d}{z_a}\right).
  • The hyperbolicity equations exp(zk∂V/∂zk)=1\exp(z_k\,\partial V/\partial z_k)=1 are products of (1−ratio)±1(1-\text{ratio})^{\pm1}, so no logarithm branch has to be chosen.
  • The modified potential V0=V−∑k(zk∂kV)logzkV_0 = V-\sum_k (z_k\partial_k V)\log z_k gives i(Vol+iCS)i(\mathrm{Vol}+i\,\mathrm{CS}) at the geometric solution (CKK Thm 1.2).
  • This is, literally, the volume of the knot potential.

Reading any drawing.

  • The placement of the four sides at a crossing appears in the source only as a figure, so all 64 placements were tested against SnapPy.
  • One rule survives, up to the term’s symmetry (a↔c,b↔d)(a\leftrightarrow c,\ b\leftrightarrow d): (b,d)(b,d) is the under-strand pair of the PD crossing X[i,j,k,l]X[i,j,k,l], the same for both signs.
  • A kinked crossing X[i,j,j,l]X[i,j,j,l] is singular and must be removed.

The finite object.

  • The solutions come in continuous families: no single ratio or cross-ratio is fixed along them.
  • Only V0V_0 is constant on each family. So the drawing gives one complex number per family, that is, per boundary-parabolic geometry.
  • Its imaginary part is the decay (volume of that geometry); its real part is the direction (−CS-\mathrm{CS} mod π2\pi^2).
  • We define the knot’s decay–direction polynomial PK(x)=∏ρ(x−eλV0(ρ)),P_K(x) = \prod_{\rho}\Big(x - e^{\lambda V_0(\rho)}\Big), one root per geometry, with λ\lambda chosen to map the π2\pi^2 period to a full turn. Its roots carry the two books directly.

The circuit polynomial (first attempt).

  • Construction. Shade the diagram. Shaded regions are nodes and crossings are branches; a right-handed crossing has admittance zz, a left-handed one 11. Q(z)Q(z) counts spanning trees by right-handed branches.
  • Q(1)=detKQ(1) = \det K on 1,846 of 1,846 alternating knots.
  • QQ is unchanged on 180 of 180 reduced alternating redrawings. Proof sketch: a flype acts on the Tait graph as a Whitney twist, which keeps every spanning tree, and keeps every crossing’s handedness; by Tait’s flyping theorem QQ is then an invariant of alternating knots.
  • It is coarse: knots with all crossings of one hand give a constant.
  • Its lesson: on the diagram alone, the only fully invariant circuit weights lead back to the Kauffman bracket, that is, to Jones. A better polynomial needs the geometry the diagram lacks, which is why Section 6 reads the potential and its geometries.

8. Lessons from surfaceology

  • What Figueiredo’s group found. Carolina Figueiredo, with Arkani-Hamed, Cao, Dong and He, found hidden zeros of Tr(ϕ3\phi^3) amplitudes and the one kinematic shift that keeps them. That shift turns a theory with only denominators into pions and gluons, with numerators.
  • Reproduced here:
    • the 6-point amplitude vanishes exactly on its hidden-zero locus;
    • the shifted 4-point amplitude has leading term −(X13+X24)-(X_{13}+X_{24});
    • the shifted 6-point amplitude equals the NLSM amplitude (ratio 1 at three random points).
  • Her u-variables are cross-ratios. They satisfy uC+∏uD=1u_C + \prod u_D = 1 (checked to 4×10−164\times10^{-16}) and are unchanged by Möbius maps. They are the same kind of number as the tetrahedron shapes, and Möbius maps are the Smith chart’s own moves.
  • Lessons taken:
    • find the zeros first;
    • a denominator-only object, like the circuit polynomial, needs a shift to gain numerators.

9. Colour: the elements on the same disk

Reflection tracks. The reflection Γ=(1−N)/(1+N)\Gamma = (1-N)/(1+N) of each element across the visible band traces a track on the Smith disk, using 41 elements from refractiveindex.info.

What showed up.

  • The Schottky line. |Γ|2=1/2|\Gamma|^2 = 1/2, averaged over the visible, separates metals (35 of 35 above) from non-metals (0 of 6 above).
  • Hidden phase. Bulk colour hides the phase of Γ\Gamma, and a thin film writes it back. Over 39 metal pairs that the eye cannot tell apart in bulk, the phase gap predicts the colour gap under a film at ρ=0.64\rho = 0.64. Pairs riding the rim under-read (ρ=−0.52\rho = -0.52, p=0.0007p = 0.0007): near ground the phase is present but pinned.
  • One handedness. Every element’s track winds the same way (38 of 41, the other three about 0). Every causal model medium does the same, through absorption lines too: causality and passivity, in the family of Foster’s reactance theorem. Status: recognised physics.
  • The space between. Molecular solids crossing the 2/32/3 vacuum-mix line under squeeze rank their measured metallization pressures at ρ=0.89\rho = 0.89 (Te, P, Se, I, Br, S). Network solids (Si, Ge, B) already sit on the line and need a flip, a change of structure, not a squeeze.
  • The film winding is an integer index of the substrate. It is exact to 10−1510^{-15} and grows by one per 247 nm of film. It jumps across the circle |r12|=|r01||r_{12}| = |r_{01}|; selenium is the one element inside it.
    • Lightness follows the bulk reflection (continuous).
    • Chroma follows the integer order.
    • Hue follows the leftover phase.
    • The shape matches volume beside windings; the physics is Newton’s colour orders.
  • Knots rendered as colours. Jones strings rendered as colours carry volume through lightness (ρ=0.96\rho = 0.96; R2R^2 0.707 → 0.878), but that colour is a compression of |V||V|.

What is not yet joined. The knot pieces fill the inside of the disk; the metals sit near the rim (Figure 1). The bowl region is barely reached by the elements. Clear materials (oxides, salts, glasses) live inside the disk and are the next colour test.

10. The electron

The electron’s group is the chain’s group.

  • An electron’s spin lives in ℂ2\mathbb C^2, turned and boosted by SL(2,ℂ)\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C), the double cover of the Lorentz group.
  • Dropping the sign gives PSL(2,ℂ)\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb C). That group is at once the Möbius maps (the Smith chart’s moves) and the symmetries of hyperbolic 3-space, where the knot’s volume lives.
  • Every geometry of Section 4 is a representation of the knot group into this group.
  • So the chain light → electron → knot runs through one group. Status: standard mathematics; its use as the spine of the chain is ours.

The electron decides where matter sits on the disk. Free electrons push reflection to the rim (ground); bound electrons keep it near the vacuum. The metal/non-metal line and the 2/32/3 space-between line are the electron’s choice to conduct.

The double cover and the last open piece of the twist (conjecture, untested).

  • The twist of Section 5 is exact modulo π2/2\pi^2/2; whether it is exact modulo π2\pi^2 is open.
  • Lifting a knot’s geometry from PSL\mathrm{PSL} to SL(2,ℂ)\mathrm{SL}(2,\mathbb C) is a choice of spin structure: the electron’s 720∘720^\circ return, the −1-1 of Dirac Time §5.2.
  • The conjecture: the remaining π2/2\pi^2/2 is that choice, so fixing the spin lift closes the direction book.
