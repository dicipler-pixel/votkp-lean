# AI use

The synthesis in this folder, the choice of what to combine, is Jeromie Beasley's. AI tools
drafted the work under his direction: Claude built the *Knot Atlas in 3D* artifact and wrote its
mathematical text and code; Grok-based assistants checked its numbers and wording, wrote the
scripts in `checks/`, and drafted this folder. Jeromie Beasley is the only human reviewer.

No statement here rests on an AI's say-so. Each claim in `CLAIMS.md` is marked as proved in the
cited literature, proved in Lean in this repository, checked numerically by the scripts in
`checks/` (whose output is included), a drawing convention, or a proposal or observation.
Citations that were not opened and checked are marked as such.
