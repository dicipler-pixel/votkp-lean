Caveat printed: LPS uniqueness holds only after normalisation; 2+i has norm 5 but is not one of the six.
- The placement (Def 4.1, l.117): B_u = [[c, s u^T],[s u, I + (c-1) u u^T]], c=(z^2+1)/(2z), s=(z^2-1)/(2z). Generator 1+2u -> B_u; conjugate 1-2u -> B_{-u} (= inverse). Product-quaternion boost is not the boost along the product's axis (Sec 4.4: two boosts = boost then rotation).

- Knot side (Sec 6, l.267-271): Riley meridians a = [[s,1],[0,1/s]], b = [[s,0],[-v,1/s]]; relation aW = Wb (Schubert word W, Riley normal form).
