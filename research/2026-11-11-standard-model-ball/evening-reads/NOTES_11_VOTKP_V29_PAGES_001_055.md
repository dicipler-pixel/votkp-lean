### p9 Sec 3 Theorem 3.1 (finite linear predictive quotient) [Theorem]
L linear evolution on V (dim V = n), R: V->Y observation, O v = (Rv, R L v, ..., R L^{n-1} v), N = ker O. Then: (1) two states give identical futures iff v - w in N; (2) N is L-invariant; (3) dim V/N = rank O, so rank O - rank R extra coordinates are needed beyond present observation; (4) among output-preserving autonomous summaries (maps F: V->W with F L = A F and R = Q F) every one has ker F within N and quotient V/N is the minimal one; (5) present observation closes autonomously iff ker R is L-invariant. ### p35-36 Sec 8.7 The step splits into a shell wall and a screening step  (KEY FOR 5/8, WALL, FRANCIUM HOOKS)
 Hydrogen-like form: IE_k = Ry Z_eff,k^2 / n_k^2 where n_k = highest principal quantum number in the ground configuration of the ion ionized at stage k; defines Z_eff,k = n_k sqrt(IE_k/Ry) from data; "nothing is modelled".
 THEOREM 8.2 (split of the peel step) [Theorem, elementary]. With Delta x = x_{k+1} - x_k: tau_k = 2 Delta ln Z_eff - 2 Delta ln n; Gamma_k = Gamma_k^scr (+) Gamma_k^sh, with Gamma^scr = tanh(Delta ln Z_eff), Gamma^sh = tanh(-Delta ln n), (+) addition law of Sec 15.2. Inside a shell Gamma^sh = 0. At a shell wall (any step where highest n drops to n-1, including 4s->3d steps in d-block ions): Gamma^sh = (n^2 - (n-1)^2)/(n^2 + (n-1)^2) = (2n-1)/(n^2 + (n-1)^2) = 3/5, 5/13, 7/25, 9/41, 11/61, 13/85 (n = 2,...,7). "odd leg over the hypotenuse of the Pythagorean triples (2n-1, 2n(n-1), n^2 + (n-1)^2). The form is Euclid's: tanh ln(p/q) = (p^2 - q^2)/(p^2 + q^2) ... the triples carry no physics beyond the hydrogen-like ratio (n/(n-1))^2."
 Proof: (i) IE_{k+1}/IE_k = (Zeff,k+1/Zeff,k)^2 (n_k/n_{k+1})^2; (ii) logs and halving: tau_k/2 = Delta ln Zeff - Delta ln n; (iii) Gamma_k = tanh(tau_k/2) by Thm 8.1; tanh of a sum is the (+)-sum of tangents; (iv) at a wall z = (n/(n-1))^2, Gamma^sh = (z-1)/(z+1).
 Checked on all 5,714 NIST steps to Z = 110, holds to 4e-16; every wall from n=2 to 7 carries exactly its fraction [Exact, split_theorem_check.py]. One step (gadolinium, stage 14) changes n by other than 0 or -1. "What it is": a bookkeeping identity; Zeff read off the data; shell part fixed by n alone, rest is screening. Lithium's first step: wall Gamma_1 = 0.867 = 3/5 (+) 0.556 (Fig 15a).
 What the split shows [Exact, RHYTHM-20]: shell part carries most of variation of step; share = var(-2 Delta ln n)/var(tau); two parts covary negatively so shares do not add to one (Fig 15b). Carries 75% of the variance of tau over the 3,655 steps to Z=86 (exploratory); 85% over the 2,059 steps of Z=87-110 (T01 passed; 96% of those stage values theoretical). As explained variance R^2 = 0.70 for Z<=86 and 0.59 for Z=87-110; with no fitted constant (tau = shell part + median within-shell step) 0.68 and 0.55; line fitted on Z<=86 predicts Z=87-110 with out-of-sample R^2 = 0.52 [Exact, post-hoc, split_explained.py].
