### p64 — 15.1 The electron is the unit removed; the onion belongs to the state
- IE_{q+1} = E(X^{(q+1)+}) - E(X^{q+}), difference of many-electron total energies; not a property of the electron that leaves. Same for tau_q = ln(IE_{q+1}/IE_q) and Gamma_q = tanh(tau_q/2). "The electron is the unit of the peel; the layering belongs to the interacting state. That is why the ridges move in deep ions (§8.4) while the electron does not change."
- Imaged many-electron correlation: Pauli exchange hole in a Fermi gas resolved atom by atom (Yao et al. 2025); exchange-correlation hole ~2 A in beryllium, extending to 4-5 A at the 21 eV plasmon (Bista et al. 2026). Photon: probe; "reads a boundary, and is not part of the layered object."
- 15.2 One addition law for the atom and the film. THEOREM 15.1 (peel steps add like velocities) [Theorem, elementary]: u (+) v = (u+v)/(1+uv). For k < l, Gamma_{k->l} = tanh( (1/2) ln(IE_l/IE_k) ) (reflection of the whole block of removals k to l). Then
  Gamma_{k->l} = Gamma_k (+) Gamma_{k+1} (+) ... (+) Gamma_{l-1}.
  Derivation: (1) ln(IE_l/IE_k) = sum_{j=k}^{l-1} ln(IE_{j+1}/IE_j) = sum tau_j; (2) tanh(a+b) = (tanh a + tanh b)/(1 + tanh a tanh b); (3) apply to halves a = tau_k/2, b = tau_{k+1}/2, repeat. (+) is Einstein's collinear velocity addition in units of c: beta = tanh phi, rapidities add; Gamma_k a velocity, tau_k/2 a rapidity.

### p65 — Corollaries 15.2, 15.3; radar reading
- COROLLARY 15.2 (peel coordinate is a hyperbolic length) [Theorem, elementary]: on the Smith disk read as the Poincare disk, distance from centre to w is d(0,w) = 2 artanh|w| = ln((1+|w|)/(1-|w|)). For w = Gamma_{k->l} this is exactly ln(IE_l/IE_k). Bullets: atom's peel = walk along one diameter (a geodesic); steps have hyperbolic lengths tau_k, lengths add; disk squeezes steps toward rim. Sodium's ten steps from IE_1 to IE_11 reach Gamma = 0.9938, hyperbolic distance ln(IE_11/IE_1) = 5.77 (Fig 25 left).
- COROLLARY 15.3 (film has same algebraic form) [Theorem, elementary; Reproduction of Vigoureux 1992]: film reflection of §6.4:
  r(d) = (Gamma + x)/(1 + Gamma x) = Gamma (+) x,   x = r_12 e^{4 pi i N d / lambda}.
  First-order echo coefficient of Theorem 6.1 is its derivative at x = 0:  d(Gamma (+) x)/dx at x=0 = 1 - Gamma^2. For real Gamma = tanh(tau/2) this is sech^2(tau/2) = 1/gamma^2, gamma = cosh(tau/2) the Lorentz factor of the equivalent rapidity.
  Bullets: atom adds real steps along a geodesic; a metal's Gamma is complex so the film's path starts off the diameter; for complex Gamma the map x -> (Gamma+x)/(1+Gamma x) is not a hyperbolic isometry of the disk (isometry needs conj(Gamma) in the denominator); film shares the algebraic form, hyperbolic reading holds only for real Gamma (Fig 25 right).
- Radar reading [Cited: Einstein 1905 §8 moving mirror, §5 addition law; Bondi 1964]: light frequency nu reflected at normal incidence from mirror approaching at beta c returns at nu (1+beta)/(1-beta). Set ratio = z_k = IE_{k+1}/IE_k; solve beta = (z_k - 1)/(z_k + 1) = Gamma_k. Each peel ratio is the frequency ratio of a radar echo from a mirror moving at Gamma_k c. Bondi factor k = e^{tau_k/2} = sqrt(z_k). Successive peel steps compose as successive boosts. "This is a representation: nothing in the atom moves."
- Prior art (continues p66): reflection by stratified media obeys complex Einstein addition (Vigoureux 1992; Vigoureux & Grossel 1993); lossless multilayer = SU(1,1) (locally SO(2,1)), absorbing = SL(2,C) (locally SO(3,1)); phase in compound stacks is a Thomas rotation (Monzon & Sanchez-Soto 1999a,b; Yonte et al. 2002); hyperbolic distance from Smith-chart centre measures mismatch (Allen & Healy 2004).

### p69-70 — 15.3 What changes when the particles change
- THEOREM 15.4 (scale blindness) [Theorem, elementary; standard mass-scaling argument]: Coulomb Hamiltonian H = sum_i p_i^2/(2 m_i) + sum_{i<j} q_i q_j/(4 pi eps_0 r_ij), multiply every mass by lambda. (1) substitute r = r'/lambda, then nabla = lambda nabla'; (2) kinetic term becomes -hbar^2 lambda^2 nabla'^2/(2 lambda m) = lambda (-hbar^2 nabla'^2/(2m)); (3) potential becomes lambda q_i q_j/(4 pi eps_0 r'_ij); (4) so H_lambda is unitarily equivalent to lambda H: every energy scales by lambda and every ratio IE_{k+1}/IE_k is unchanged. "The peel coordinate is blind to which particle carries the charge, except through charges, mass ratios and statistics."
- Measured two-step peels along the mass-ratio axis [Exact, Reproduction]. For M+ e- e- the two removals are electron affinity (EA) then ionization energy (IE) of the neutral, so tau = ln(IE/EA). TABLE (system / mass ratio m_e/M / tau / Gamma / inputs):

  - D-: 1/3670.5 ; 2.89180(10) ; 0.89488 ; EA 6086.2(6) cm^-1 (Lykke et al. 1991)
  - H-: 1/1836.2 ; 2.89206(2) ; 0.89491 ; EA 6082.99(15) cm^-1 (Lykke 1991); IE NIST
  - Ps- (theory): 1 ; 3.0361 ; 0.9084 ; nonrelativistic binding 0.0120050702 a.u. over spin-free Ps level (Grigorescu & Drake 2003)
  - Ps- (measured): 1 ; 3.0355(4) ; 0.9083 ; threshold to ortho-Ps, 326.88(13) meV (Michishio et al. 2020); IE of Ps from Rydberg energy
- What the table shows: replacing an infinitely heavy positive particle by a positron moves tau by +0.145 (theory to theory); measured H- to measured Ps- gives +0.143.
