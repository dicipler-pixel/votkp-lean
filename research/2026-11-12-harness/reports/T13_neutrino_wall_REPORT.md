## B1. Speed limit = peel-disk bound: HOLDS
Algebra. G=1-2A gives dG/dt=-2 dA/dt, so (dG/dt)^2=4 Adot^2. 1-G^2=1-(1-2A)^2=4A-4A^2=4A(1-A). Then (dG/dt)^2<=4g(1-G^2) reads 4 Adot^2<=16 g A(1-A), i.e. Adot^2<=4gA(1-A): his bound with g=E_B=k^2/3. sympy difference of the two forms: 0 (s1_b1.out).

Free two-moment (kappa=0): equality, |ratio-1|=8.6e-11 with exact expm. The bound is saturated exactly by the unitary two-level rotation (geodesic of the disk); every damped chain sits below it, slack up to 5-20%.
Two kill lines were hit and resolved (s1b_b1_recheck): central-difference Adot deviated up to 6e-4 (it falls x3.95-4.00 per halving of h: truncation), and the BDF free-two-moment test gave 3e-5 (solver artefact; expm gives 8.6e-11).

## B2. Two-moment truncation = loop element: HOLDS
L2=[[0,-ik/sqrt3],[-ik/sqrt3,-kappa]] = -kappa/2 - i h, h=[[iu,q],[q,-iu]], u=kappa/2, q=k/sqrt3: the loop's chain element at one k. With a=k/sqrt3, x=kappa/(2a)=sqrt3 kappa/(2k): traceless part M=a(x sigma_z - i sigma_x), M^2=a^2(x^2-1).
Hand result, checked at 50 digits on 120 values of x (biorthogonal K, independent of formula): K=1/(1-x^2) (x<1), x^2/(x^2-1) (x>1); eigenvector overlap cos^2(alpha/2)=x^2 (x<1), 1/x^2 (x>1). These are the loop's 1/(1-2G^2), 2G^2/(2G^2-1), 2G^2, 1/(2G^2) with 2G^2=x^2, G^2=3kappa^2/(8k^2). Worst relative miss 5.7e-50; K(x)=K(1/x) to 6.7e-50. Eigenvalue-type: x<1 complex pair (turn), x>1 real pair (run), 0 violations; tan(E'/4)=sqrt(1-x^2) and tanh(lambda/8)=sqrt(x^2-1) to 8e-62 (loop quasi-energy E'=2 arccos q, run rate cosh(lambda/4)=q=G^2/(1-G^2)).
Answers. (My pre-registration expected the candidates to fail: wrong, recorded.) Pairing G G'=1/2 <=> x x'=1 <=> kappa'=4E_B/kappa, same K, one underdamped one overdamped. Turn = kappa<2k/sqrt3 (free streaming), run = kappa>2k/sqrt3 (coupled): matches "coupled era runs, free streaming turns" at the two-moment level. Exact limits: (1) real peel steps (G<1) cover x<sqrt2 only (kappa<1.633k); x>sqrt2 is formal (G>1, z<0). (2) The loop's per-pass rate lambda(x)=4 arccosh(x^2/(2-x^2)) is NOT the two-moment rate ratio 2 arccosh x (x=1.2: 6.39 vs 1.245): the identification matches eigenvector geometry (K, angle), not eigenvalues per pass.

## B5. Order of history as the loop: HOLDS in a modified form
The defect U_ab U_ba^-1 = B_b B_a B_b^-1 B_a^-1 (B=exp(dL), det 1) is exactly the loop's commutator. General identity: tr W = 2 + 4 f_a^2 f_b^2 (p^2 - mu_a mu_b), N=traceless part, mu=N^2 (scalar), p=tr(N_a N_b)/2, f=sinh(d sqrt mu)/sqrt mu (sin for mu<0). Loop pair (perpendicular Hermitian boosts, p=0, mu=1): 2-4 sinh^4(r/2) (checked 1e-67). Two-moment pair: p^2-mu_a mu_b = a^2(kappa_a-kappa_b)^2/4, so tr W = 2 + a^2 (kappa_a-kappa_b)^2 f_a^2 f_b^2 (6400 points, 4 durations, 70 digits: 8e-61; min tr W = 2.0 at kappa_a=kappa_b). Hence tr W>=2: the defect is always a run (positive real eigenvalues mu,1/mu), never a turn, never a Jordan block, and there is no wall in (kappa_a,kappa_b); W=I only for kappa_a=kappa_b or f=0. The loop's turn and wall (tr<2, tr=-2) need p^2<mu_a mu_b (perpendicular-like Hermitian axes); the damped streaming steps are not that. 60-chain (330 defects): one real positive dominant eigenvalue >1 (min 1.0012) and 56-59 eigenvalues within 1e-6 of the unit circle; no Jordan, no turn.
Sign ("rate falling late keeps more" = A_lowfirst >= A_highfirst, A=<0|U|0>^2): holds at the window end at all 12480 two-moment points x 4 durations (0 violations) and with a coupled tail for d<=1. FAILS with a free-streaming tail (e.g. 780/780 grid points at tail 3, 403 at d=2 tail 1) and for the 60-chain at k=1 (296/1092); profile-averaged over his 36 k: 3/84. So the sign is the sign of the defect at the window end, a tail rotates it, and the profile washes the reversal out (consistent with his 33-72% readout differences being profile quantities). Double-precision control flagged 2 round-off cases at e^16 (70-digit run decisive).
