Garrett’s ratio-5 tree · one loop feeds phase, pressure and turn

One operator feeds the ball: the tree’s own four-step loop at step ratio z. Inside the wall the loop turns, at the wall it locks, past the wall it runs. Light is a prepared direction on the rim, carried by that loop.

The phase view is fed by the loop: κ is half the loop’s turn and φ is the angle from the prepared direction to the loop’s axis. The tune-out view is a separate three-level response model placed at the end of a three-step route. The particle and Higgs views place Standard Model masses in the same ball; the feed does not move them.
