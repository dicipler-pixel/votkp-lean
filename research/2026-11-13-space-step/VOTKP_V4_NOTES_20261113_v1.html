2. The new piece: the space between as one peel step

The 6 November work measured the space between atoms as E = 1 − (r_cov / r_room)³, one minus the atom's own volume over the volume it has in the material. That is a ratio of two volumes, so it is a step in the peel coordinate with no new definition:

zE = Vroom / Vown = 1/(1 − E),     ΓE = (zE − 1)/(zE + 1) = E/(2 − E).

Exact: the line E = 2/3 is the step z = 3, where ΓE = 1/2 and K = 2. That step was already singled out three times on 12 and 13 November without any element data: it is where the loop's K equals 2, it is the only whole-number solution of the pressure balance P₁ + P₂ = 1 between two facing balls, and it is the point of the exit-line band where K equals its own mean. So the map that was missing between "the 2/3 line" and the disk is this: the 2/3 line is Γ = 1/2 of the space step. It is not Garrett's Γ = 2/3, which is the step z = 5.

Seen, with no line written first

  • Three families, three steps. Covalent and p-block matter switches at z = 3. Mercury starts leaving at about 3.1 and has its critical point at the wall, z = 5.83. The one-electron atoms switch near z = 11 to 13: caesium, potassium and rubidium at their critical points (11.1, 11.4, 12.6) and fluid deuterium and hydrogen turning metal (11.6 to 13.2; one older hydrogen value at 19.6). This is "one size does not fit all" in numbers. The alkalis are also the one family past the half line in the ionization ladder, and z = 11 is the partner across the wall of the first shell step z = 4.
  • Diamond and the quarter turn. The loop's turn is 90° at z = 3.2202. Diamond carbon sits at 3.213 (89.6°) and tellurium at 3.223 (90.1°). Silicon and germanium are at 81°, boron at 77°. Every metal is below 54°.

Cautions. A filled fraction of 1/3 is also the conductor threshold of symmetric effective-medium theory (Bruggeman), and the 6 November ledger records it; so E = 2/3 as a conduction line has prior art, and what is his is the coordinate that puts it on the same disk as the loop, the wall and the neutrino chain. E depends on which radius is called the atom's own (Pyykkö covalent radii here); the mercury number moves with that choice. The alkali metal-to-non-metal densities are quoted in the sources only as "around the critical density". The densities, radii and switch points are other people's published measurements.
