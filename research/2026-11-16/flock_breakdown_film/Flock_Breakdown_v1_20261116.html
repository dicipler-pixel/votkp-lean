1 · The spine, in eight steps

1 · One stepThe peel step B(u, r) is a boost of length r = ln z along an arm u. Its Smith coordinate is Γ = tanh(r/2) = (z − 1)/(z + 1).
All later parts are built from this one operator.
2 · One faceA face stands halfway along the step, at r/2. Light splits there: Γ² returns, 1 − Γ² passes. From the other side the sign of the return flips.
Nothing is lost: the two shares add to one.
3 · One ballIn the drawn ball the step lands at radius Γ. Refraction, total return, pressure 2R, the Thomas turn and the rim all follow from the same step.
4 · Two faces and the gapTwo arms at right angles give g = sinh ha · sinh hb (h = r/2). If g < 1 the faces cross. If g > 1 they stand apart by δ = arccosh g. The wall is g = 1, z = 3 + 2√2.
5 · Three balls and the matrixOne two-level block per ball, one rotation per link, added into H = D + A. A frame carried round a triangle comes back turned by the triangle's area.
6 · The flockLinks become real stretches of field. Light takes time to cross, scatters at each face, and each ball keeps its own clock. The books stay exact: light in balls plus light in gaps equals one.
7 · Swinging togetherYour picture: the electron as a swing of chains moving with the universe, a flock of birds, a flock of clocks. Outside work on coupled clocks (Kuramoto with delay, Huygens) is the nearest known ground.
8 · Where it goesOpen: the electron sea with travel time, gap lengths that follow the breathing drive, and what makes clocks lock. Listed under "Not claimed".

2 · The chain, link by link

2.1 The peel step, the face and Smith addition

One operator feeds everything. A face at r/2 returns Γ² and passes 1 − Γ². Steps along one line add like velocities, by Smith addition ⊕: the sum of a layer's echoes equals the ⊕-sum of its steps.

Γ = tanh(r/2) = (z − 1)/(z + 1)  ·  return R = Γ²  ·  pass T = 1 − Γ²  ·  reverse side: return sign flips

Why it is there. It is the one fact that makes light behave the same at every scale: a step is a ratio, so what happens at a face depends only on the ratio. The Flock uses it at every face (K3 below).

All named theorems were found at the cited lines in the five files with no sorry, axiom or native_decide. Lean was not recompiled in this session; the repositories state their own builds.

2.2 The gap between two faces

On two perpendicular arms the faces either cross or stand apart. Where they stand apart, light between them tunnels. The gap acts as one more peel step of length 2κδ. At κ = 1 and incidence φ = π/2 it passes an amplitude of exactly 1/g.

g = sinh ha · sinh hb  ·  g < 1: faces cross at angle arccos g  ·  g > 1: gap δ = arccosh g  ·  tunnel amplitude sech(κδ)

CHECKED The gap formula was derived and matched against direct wave matching on 2,000 random cases, worst error 1.3e-15. HYPOTHESIS That the Flock's gap tunnels by sech(κδ) at φ = π/2 is the Flock's own choice. It is the film theorem at its largest passing setting. κ has no source and is a free parameter.

2.4 Three balls and the matrix

Each ball carries a two-level block Di = di·σ that says which way its field points and how far apart its levels sit. Each link carries a rotation Rij with an SU(2) lift Uij. They add into H = D + A. Link strength is c0·sech(d/2).

turn round a triangle = area of the triangle = 2 arctan(ΓI ΓJ)  (0.7110545751 for the three-ball case, three ways equal)

LEAN The connection energy identity (non-normality is connection energy). CHECKED on 3 and 7 balls: both sides 4.48386946121. MODEL CHOICE The hop strength c0·sech(d/2) is not found in any paper. MODEL CHOICE The sea as one projector with the cut from chooseCut.

Two things the sources do not say, found while reading: the matrix that moves the light is Hermitian, so the connection energy belongs to its companion matrix (same D, hop turned by a quarter turn); and the offset speed limit τ̇² ≤ 4gS is PROVED in the papers and LEAN for one mode only. In the runs its ratio stays at or below 0.021 (3 balls) and 0.026 (7 balls).

The clock ratio. Nothing in the sources derives a ball's rate from its step. Two numbers are candidates. For z = 5 the Doppler factor is 5 and the time-dilation factor is 2.6. A ball standing still at a fixed place sees a static redshift, which has the structure of time dilation. The Flock uses z, the Doppler number. Between balls B and C (not one step apart) the Flock uses 5/3 where the radar ratio of that link is ed = 8.55. This stays a hypothesis until a derivation or a different choice settles it.

4 · What the checks found

4.1 Sign of the faces on arm–arm links OPEN

On a centre–arm link the centre side sees +Γ and the ball side sees −Γ, as step_matching and reciprocity require. On an arm–arm link the Flock v1 code gives the first face +Γa and the second −Γb, the reverse. An independent first-principles check by the watcher found 48 of 60 faces differ from the centre–arm convention. Reversing the sign is not a rescue, because then the centre–arm faces would be wrong.

The effect on the run is large: light in ball B at t = 3 is 0.0325 as built and 0.4212 with the signs reversed. Kick B, read at t = 2: ball B holds 0.1136 as built and 0.3250 reversed. The books hold in both, and K3 and K4 pass in both, because they measure squares. The existing checks cannot see it.

Smallest repair. Swap the two signs. This is built as fl_math_v2.js (option armSign); the v1 signs remain selectable as asBuiltV1 and are labelled a MODEL CHOICE. All kill lines K1–K7 hold in v2 too. The choice between them is yours; both can be shown side by side.

4.2 The flock does not lock CHECKED

A hunt of 384 cells covered both sign conventions, loss, an absorbing end, torque reversed and up to 10, and κ up to 4. Of 1,920 ball pairs with different free rates, none had measured rates agreeing in both windows. The balls share spectral lines in 27 percent of pairs against 2.75 percent by chance, but the lines are as common with the torque off: they are normal modes of a linear coupled system, not a Kuramoto pull. The instrument was checked on a Kuramoto-with-delay control, where it reports locked at K = 3 and not locked at K = 0.

The order parameter r is not a result. It drifts with step size after t ≈ 20 and sits only slightly above a random-phase baseline for z clocks. OPEN Not scanned: a driven flock, N above 7, a single dominating mode, and a push carried by a field on the link.

The readout FL.clockPhase mismeasures turning rates when the torque is on (ball D reads −0.013 against a true 1.04). A frame-free spectral check was used instead.

4.3 Why the delayed face push is unstable CHECKED

The delayed push arrives late, and nothing carries or books its energy, so the rest state grows. For two balls the growth rate is the real part of a root of λ = −i a (1 + e−λτ), about 2a²τ for small delay, positive for any delay. A two-ball integrator matched that to 0.2 percent at every delay tried. In the code the field update uses the neighbour's value from the start of the step, so the effective delay is one step longer than the stored one. The shipped default is the static push (held toward rest); the delayed push is an option, labelled.

4.4 Wording fixes for the page OPEN

The Flock page says light between two faces "only tunnels". In the code the light travels the whole stretch inside the centre cell freely, and is also cut once by sech(κδ). Those are two different lengths: for z = 7, 9 the stretch is 1.2846 and δ = 0.9730. δ is the least distance between the two face planes, on their common perpendicular, which is not the link. The wording will say which length is which.

7 · Not claimed

  • The electron sea with travel time; no result exists.
  • The energy of light in transit, and gap lengths that follow the breathing drive.
  • Any law of motion for the clock ratio, and any Doppler from a moving face.
  • A minimum gap: it is neither enforced nor derived. Below the wall there is no gap between equal-step faces.
  • The face push 2R as a physical pressure: untested.
  • The Thomas turn as a turn of light's polarisation: no claim.
  • Locking of the flock: not found; the order parameter is not a result.
  • Lean was not recompiled; the numerical Flock results are first order in step size (good to about 1e-3 at h = 0.005).
  • The "conserved to 2e-11" witness for card B30 was not found on disk.
