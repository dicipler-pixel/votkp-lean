## The one question the whole piece answers
Where does each piece of mathematics in the Flock of Balls come from, why is it there, and where does it go next?

## The spine (same order in the page and in the film)
1. **One step.** The peel step, ratio z, length r = ln z, Smith coordinate Γ = tanh(r/2). Everything else is this step used again.
2. **One face.** Halfway along a step stands a face. It returns Γ² and passes 1 − Γ². (Lean: step_matching, flux_balance.)
3. **One ball.** Six arms, six faces, a rim. Rays bend (a sin²θ), return whole past the critical angle, stack by ⊕, push by 2R,
   turn a frame by the Thomas turn. (Light at the Hypersurface.)
4. **Two faces and the gap.** g = sinh h_a sinh h_b. Below 1 the faces cross; above 1 they stand apart by δ = arccosh g; the wall is z = 3 + 2√2.
5. **Three balls and a matrix.** H = D + A; connection energy; the loop turn equals the triangle's area. What the check found:
   the jump was instant, the clock was shared, the gap was not in the link.
6. **The flock.** The gap made real: travel time, faces on the way, tunnelling across a gap layer (hypothesis). A clock per ball,
   ratio z (hypothesis). The books: balls + gaps = 1.
7. **Swinging together?** Huygens' beam, the flock of clocks, the order parameter. What the runs show, plainly.
8. **Where it goes.** The sea with its own travel time; energy in transit; more balls; the Sun as leader.

Each link of the spine carries: the statement in plain words, its source (file and line, or card), its grade, what it rests on,
where the code uses it, and one moving picture.
