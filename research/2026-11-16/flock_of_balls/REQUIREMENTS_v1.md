## C. Ambiguous — flagged, not guessed

1. **Which box is "the data control box on the right" (req 11).** Two boxes sit on the right of the three-ball pages: `#tb` (top right, title "Three balls on the field": buttons, kicks, eigenvector slider and the numbers) and `#strip` (bottom right: τ, Play, Step, Speed, drive, hypothesis toggle). `#tb` is the one that holds both data and controls and is the likelier meaning; he did not say which. See CODE_MAP item 5. Also unstated: where the controls and numbers that live in that box should go once it is removed.
2. **"zoom a percet" (req 8).** Not stated: what 100% means (whole field in frame, or one ball), the range, and whether he wants a number he can type, a slider, or step buttons.
3. **"move on the field and go over it all" (req 9).** Not stated: dragging the view across the field, flying a camera path over it, stepping from ball to ball, or all of these. The assistant's wording was "move across the field and fly over all of it" (L107).
4. **"1 integration of all the views" (req 10).** "Views" could mean the ten eyes A–J of the Mixer ball, the layer switches of the white (Surprise) ball, the two ball types of Two Balls Linked ("Both as one"), or all of them. Not stated which ball page is the base: all three pages were attached.
5. **How many balls and in what arrangement (req 7).** He said only "more". The brief suggests 7 as an example. Centre plus six arms, a second level, or a free layout is not stated.
6. **"via the fields and layers" (req 1).** Which objects carry the push is not spelled out: the three-ball matrix has the light, the electron sea and each ball's field; the single ball has layers, faces, pressure and the kick record. The agreed wording is "your layer and face maths" (L141).
7. **The z clock ratio in detail (req 5a).** Not stated: which ball of a pair ticks faster; what ratio applies between balls that are not one step apart (B and C); and that z differs per arm (I, J, K) and changes in time under the breathing drive, so the ratio is not one fixed number. L152 says "ticks at a rate shifted by z"; L162 uses ω(1 − 1/z).
8. **Travel time (P2).** "The link's length in time" fixes speed 1 in hyperbolic length; whether the length is the centre-to-centre step d, or only the gap δ between faces, is not stated. The film formula (P4) needs an incidence and a frequency, which are not specified.
9. **Has he approved the four-point build (Clocks, Gaps, Beam, Sync meter)?** The chat ends at L182 with the assistant's plan. His last words are L148 ("yea lets do z but also think first ..."). There is no reply from him after the plan.
10. **Sync meter (P8).** Kuramoto's measure needs one phase per ball. Which phase (turning of the ball's field, phase of its light, its offset clock τ) was not said.
11. **"The sea is the shared beam" (P7).** In the current maths the sea is one projector of the whole matrix, updated everywhere in the same instant. How a shared beam is to respect the travel time of req 6 was not said.
12. **Scope (req 13).** The limit "just the multi balls on the field" comes from the launching brief. The attachment also contains unrelated earlier matter (L1–86: the Dead Photon mixer, its backup drawing mode, wording fixes, recordings); those are treated as out of scope on the strength of the brief alone.
