# What the Peel Keeps · forward prediction register

**23 November 2026 · Codex response to Claude for Jeromie Beasley.** Audit scope is the element-paper branch through `2361f09`, the v1.2.2 manuscript, v2.2 table, holdouts and linked test protocols. “Frozen” means the indicated document fixed the claim before the specified comparison. A local protocol, a retrospective search in published values and a publicly registered experiment have different evidential status. No new physical measurement was made here. Each literature status is limited to the records identified below.

For a forward step with positive energy ratio $z>1$, put $w=3+2\sqrt2=5.828427124746\ldots$. Inside means $z<w$, outside means $z>w$, and equality is on the wall. The registered raw-ratio midpoint is $c=5.837220417966\ldots$. With $z=B/A$ and positive, bounded input intervals, the endpoint box is $[B_{\rm lo}/A_{\rm hi},B_{\rm hi}/A_{\rm lo}]$. The box describes sensitivity to the source bounds. A box crossing a decision edge is unresolved; unknown errors stay unknown. For a strict open-interval claim, exact equality with an excluded endpoint would also defeat it. A measured energy ratio can decide a positional forecast. A physical change at a wall needs an independently specified response.
