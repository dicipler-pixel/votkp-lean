## What the threshold data can resolve

All ordinary H–Sn side labels are reproduced by any strict scalar rule z>c with c between nitrogen's 5.6396654003 (inclusive at the exact rational) and carbon's 6.0795341920 (exclusive). With the anion rungs included, silver and silicon tighten that interval to **[5.8079020598…, 5.8665387761…)**. These are the rungs nearest the wall from below and above. Every scalar cutoff in the interval gives exactly the same 1,265 central-value classifications, including 3+2√2. A cutoff of 6 now classifies silicon differently. Omitting the calculated technetium affinity leaves the interval intact. This calculation measures the resolution of the assigned labels. The equality 3+2√2 comes from the equal-face geometry.
