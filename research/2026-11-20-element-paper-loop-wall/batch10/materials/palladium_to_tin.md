# Palladium, silver, cadmium, indium and tin

Jeromie Beasley's *The Loop Wall: Peeling the Elements from Hydrogen to Zinc* (21 November 2026) supplies the distinction between the internal peel clock, the matched external junction and the filled part of the electronic gap. This supplement puts measurements alongside the companion energy ledger. Shell radii, nuclear radii, lattice lengths and the dimensionless VOTKP face apex each have their own definition.

**MEASURED / isotope control.** A direct four-specimen experiment gives superconducting transitions at 0.5218 K (cadmium-110), 0.5173 K (natural), 0.5148 K (cadmium-114) and 0.5115 K (cadmium-116). This fitted exponent and the peel ratio z are different observables. [Cadmium isotope experiment, 1970](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.2.4442).

**MEASURED / source discrepancy.** Ekin's palladium row carries an unqualified 1.4 K. For indium, the NIST row labels the solid “bcc,” whereas high-purity diffraction resolves body-centred tetragonal I4/mmm. The thermal fixed point and the measured crystal symmetry are taken from their respective sources. Conventional indium, silver and tin fixed-point values specify the ITS-90 scale. A zero indicated uncertainty there is a scale assignment, not perfect experimental realization. ## B, D, F and G. Internal peels versus the solid

**DERIVED from the adjacent archive ledger.** The closed 4d¹⁰ is the neutral atom in palladium, then occurs in the singly charged silver ion, doubly charged cadmium ion, triply charged indium ion and quadruply charged tin ion. Palladium needs the anion input to form a ratio straddling the neutral filled shell. The filled-4d ratio from palladium through tin is respectively 14.830539, 2.835762, 2.215951, 1.977239 and 1.890771. For silver the anion-rung ratio 5.807902 lies just inside 3+2√2≈5.828427. Cadmium lacks a bound ground-state negative ion. The first three elements' positive valence closures differ sharply even while all are normal metals at room temperature. Between palladium and silver the terminal σ screening readout also changes sign in the adjacent ledger. This nuclear/atomic-scale sign, the signed magnetic moments below and a crystal transport coefficient need independent definitions.

**HYPOTHESIS.** Beasley places the nucleus at the centre, the electron cloud on the wall, and the mobile electronic sea between neighbouring walls. In his internal metric the apex is tanh((ln z)/4). A pair of microscopically equivalent sites in the ideal matched-clock model has external z=1 and Γ=0. Conductivity requires an electronic state in the specified structure. The proposed local test compares Xᵢⱼ=‖Pᵢⱼ‖² with Tr(Cᵢ−Cᵢ²)/(3nᵢ), and B, n and X must be evaluated consistently. The side of the local cutoff has not been established here for any of these measured specimens. A separate Bruggeman 1/3 claim requires a defined connected phase and a measured volume fraction. Geometrical close packing alone supplies neither.

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** [crystals.csv](crystals.csv) contains 31 dated COD records with cell metrics, CIF-expanded populations, occupancies and shortest distances. These are atomic centre separations. Each COD identifier resolves at https://www.crystallography.net/cod/ID.html. Enumeration in each conventional cell used all site pairs and translated images up to ±2 in each index, ample for these orthogonal and standard hexagonal cells. For the oxide-stabilized palladium structure the independent split sites have partial occupancy 0.185 and 0.165. A nearest separation between simultaneously populated split sites has been withheld. The two nearly equal candidate distances in a nanocrystalline tin refinement reflect the as-listed unequal a,b in an effectively tetragonal cell. Resolution and sample conditions matter before assigning a coordination change. [COD](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/) · [palladium disorder record](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/2002851.html).

## E. Isotopes, orientation and a second kind of clock

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** [isotopes.csv](isotopes.csv) preserves all 208 ground-state records in the IAEA LiveChart API for these elements. The underlying API extraction states 18 October 2023 and each row preserves its own ENSDF cut-off. Empty fields mean unavailable in this source. The raw magnetic-dipole field is not guaranteed to be a ground-state assignment: a positive number appears against several spin-zero rows. A spin-zero ground state's permanent magnetic dipole is exactly zero by rotational symmetry. The derived column and the ground-level corrections below take precedence for physical use. For isotope A of proton number Z the centre has N=A−Z neutrons, Z protons, net up count 2Z+N and net down count Z+2N. **DERIVED conditional clock test.** A junction hypothesis should say whether its clock is an isotope-averaged internal peel, a *specified optical transition*, or a local phase-dependent electronic frequency. As a control, take the measured indium UV transition and use the magnitude of the 101–115 isotope shift, 2401(7) MHz. From the nominal λ=246.8 nm, ν≈c/λ=1.21472×10¹⁵ Hz. Two such transition frequencies separated by |Δν| give |Γ|=|Δν|/(ν₁+ν₂)≈9.88×10⁻⁷. Reversing their order flips the sign. Perfectly identical clocks give zero. This control uses an atomic optical line and makes no quantitative assignment for a lattice peel or junction. A proposed *isotope-independent optical* clock on all atoms of an element fails this test, while a coarse-grained model with a single stipulated clock per element retains exact matching by construction. An experiment on neighbouring lattice sites would need the isotope-resolved site resonance in the actual crystal. [Indium measured shifts, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-024-02612-y) · [SI definition of c, BIPM](https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/si-defining-constants).

**Mainstream-blend watch / set aside.** The atomic layer metric supplies directions and dimensionless wall coordinates. Angstrom spacings and isotope-specific nuclear radii are independently measured lengths. A two-phase tin experiment is a stronger control of a proposed link to conduction than identifying a metallic element in isolation. No bond-resolved projector was available for the five specimens surveyed here. A structure at every pressure and temperature, a full polarization-resolved bulk absorption spectrum and unique closed curves for the atomic polynomial remain open. Keep each claim with the physical state and observable that could rule it out.
