## Rung zero and the first Xe-core opening

**MEASURED.** For ground-state atoms, the recommended detachment energies in eV are Sb 1.047401(19), Te 1.970875(7), ¹²⁷I 3.0590465(36) and Cs 0.471626(25). For xenon the evaluation lists an estimated negative sign and no bound ground anion, so anion detachment supplies no positive denominator. Molecules, surfaces and excited resonances would define different experiments. [Ning & Lu, Table III, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0080243) · [Sb experiment, 1997](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.79.4104) · [Te experiment, 1996](https://doi.org/10.1007/s004600050085) · [I experiment, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-4075/42/12/125001) · [Cs photodetachment, 1978](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.17.201)

**DERIVED.** The source edition matters at the last digit. The Te experiment's quoted 1.970876(7) eV and the iodine experiment's 3.0590463(38) eV differ slightly from the above evaluated conversions. For example, 24,672.874(29) cm⁻¹ × 0.000123984198433… eV/(cm⁻¹) gives 3.05904650… eV with uncertainty 0.00000360 eV. Use the common 2022 evaluation consistently. A 2026 plasma review prints 3.0590465(37) eV for iodine. Its central value agrees, and its last uncertainty digit differs. [NIST WebBook Te](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C22541497&Mask=102F&Units=SI) · [iodine primary measurement](https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-4075/42/12/125001) · [iodine review](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6463/ae2b7e)
