# Antimony, tellurium, iodine, xenon and cesium

**HYPOTHESIS (Jeromie Beasley).** The wall of the atomic ball surrounds the central nucleus. A peel interrogates one atom. A junction joins neighbours. The working question here is which measured changes require a phase, direction, pressure or isotope input in addition to the internal atomic clock. The accompanying energy ledger gives all ionization energies, affinities, rungs and shell openings.

## Five balls and their distinct tests

**HYPOTHESIS / common test.** For any positive internal clock ratio z the wall face is tanh(ln(z)/4). Equal clocks at facing ports give a zero junction reflection. A proposed carrier criterion still needs the site projectors, filling, coordination and relevant neighbour links for the *measured phase*. The score compares Xᵢⱼ with Tr(Cᵢ−Cᵢ²)/(3 nᵢ). Atomic radii and fractional occupied volume alone do not specify the result. For each of these five elements its position relative to that model threshold remains undetermined by these bulk data. The held-out conductivity data can test a specified phase model.

**MEASURED / DERIVED — antimony.** R−3m antimony in the 298 K refinement has a=4.3084, c=11.274 Å and near/next distances 2.90821/3.35494 Å. At 7.7 GPa a separate A7 refinement gives 2.87809/3.09806 Å. The *difference* narrows from 0.44673 to 0.21997 Å. Beyond the A7 range incommensurate structures intervene before bcc near 28 GPa. Incommensurate guest and host periods require separate coordinates. The bcc 28 GPa cell has a=3.45 Å. Quenched simple-cubic, fcc, body-centred tetragonal and hcp films are listed as metastable in the CSV. A 2022 calculated onset around 0.3 K for a specified rhombohedral model should not replace the older measured high-pressure phase transition. [298 K data](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/5000214.html) · [7.7 GPa](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9013397.html) · [lattice dynamics and phase ordering, 2019](https://www.osti.gov/pages/servlets/purl/1594632) · [observed superconductivity, 1969](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-3697(69)90202-9) · [calculated rhombohedral case, 2022](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevMaterials.6.054801).

**MEASURED / DERIVED — tellurium.** The 1989 P3₁21 refinement gives a=4.456, c=5.921 Å, radial displacement R=0.2636a, and an axial advance h=c/3. The ideal screw distance is √(3R²+h²)=2.83451 Å, below the next contact at 3.49117 Å. In a right-handed Cartesian frame the determinant of the three consecutive bond vectors is +(3√3/2)R²c = +21.22404 Å³. Reversing site order preserves its sign; a mirror reverses it. Independently tabulated P3₂21 yields −21.99003 Å³ at its own cell parameters. A 2024 polarization-resolved Raman study measures crystal handedness. The two readings support a mirror test without imposing a knot closure. Under pressure, 2023 transport and Raman distinguish trigonal Te-I, triclinic Te-II, modulated monoclinic Te-III and bcc Te-V. The critical temperature reaches 4.0 K at 4.5 GPa and 7.2 K at 34.8 GPa. On decompression it reaches 8 K at 31.4 GPa. A model of the gap layer should condition on pressure history. [1989 coordinates](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/2020222.html) · [opposite hand](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9008580.html) · [2024 Raman experiment](https://doi.org/10.1039/D3TC04333A) · [2023 pressure measurements](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.214518).

**MEASURED / DERIVED — iodine.** At 100(2) K, the 2014 Cmce coordinates give a,b,c=7.1589,4.6915,9.8014 Å, molecular distance 2.71780 Å and next contact 3.50096 Å. The 1978 high-pressure data yield 2.78191 and 2.96497 Å. Their separation shrinks from 0.78316 Å to 0.18306 Å across samples. Neither distance is a dimensionless face apex. Single-crystal work in 2023 found Cmc2₁ molecular iodine near 16 GPa, followed near 20 GPa by an incommensurate phase with molecular and zigzag motifs. A 2024 study traced modulated structures further at high pressure. Resistance and magnetization measurements on iodine above 22 GPa found superconductivity below 1.2 K. Later diffraction revises the structural picture associated with older transport. [100 K coordinates](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/4511285.html) · [compressed molecular structure](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/1525259.html) · [Bykova et al., 2023](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.108.024104) · [2024 diffraction](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.110.104111) · [1994 superconductivity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0921452694914796).

**MEASURED / DERIVED — xenon.** A gas has no permanent crystalline nearest neighbour. In fcc solid xenon the measured 1962 cells give nearest neighbour a/√2=4.33584 Å at 5 K and 4.38569 Å at 75 K. A separate 26 K hcp preparation gives a=4.34, c=7.09 Å. The under-pressure metal remains a stringent control against the conductivity interpretation of a single free-atom shell. At 155 GPa its transport was metallic over the experimental temperature range extending down to 27 mK. These measurements establish no superconducting transition. The visible discharge comes from an excited gas and has different electronic occupation. [5 K](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012450.html) · [75 K](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012464.html) · [hcp](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012405.html) · [Eremets et al., 2000](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.85.2797).

**MEASURED / DERIVED — cesium.** At 78 K the bcc cell gives a=6.067 Å, d=√3a/2=5.25418 Å. At 4.1 GPa the fcc structure with a=5.984 Å has d=4.23133 Å. The pressure sequence continues through an 84-site orthorhombic phase, tetragonal Cs-IV, orthorhombic Cs-V around 12 GPa and a high-pressure close-packed phase. The Cs-V CIF gives 3.23371 Å at 12 GPa versus 3.17764 at 19.6 GPa and 3.19267 at 25.8 GPa. Even this within-phase *shortest* contact need not fall monotonically as other contacts rearrange. The three distances use the independently refined cells. No propagated coordinate uncertainties are available in the CSV. Compressed cesium becomes superconducting around 1.5 K near 12.5 GPa. [78 K](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9011011.html) · [fcc](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9011550.html) · [Cs-V synchrotron refinements, 1998](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.81.2711) · [phase/electride review, 2024 preprint](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2024-vx1df) · [comparison with Wittig's 1970 cesium experiment](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.100.041109).

## Cross-element tests and coverage

**RECOGNISED.** From antimony to xenon the successive neutral 5p occupations 3, 4, 5, 6 coincide with falling recommended scalar polarizabilities. Adding cesium's 6s electron gives a factor 400.9/27.32≈14.67 increase in response. Isoelectronic xenon and Cs⁺ have 54 electrons each, while their nuclear charges differ. A neutral xenon gas and a cesium metal supply very different junctions even before structural compression. The same caution applied to sodium and argon. The isolated anion step in antimony lands outside the loop wall, those in tellurium and iodine inside; neutral polarizability alone supplies no wall classification.

**Mainstream-blend watch.** The SI second uses exactly 9,192,631,770 Hz of the unperturbed ground-state hyperfine splitting of cesium-133. This is an isotope-specific laboratory clock. The VOTKP rung ratio concerns two ionization energies. The physical units and the two pairs of states would have to be mapped explicitly before a common time interpretation could be tested. A longer lattice spacing in ångströms does not establish a particular dimensionless intrinsic metric. [BIPM SI second](https://www.bipm.org/en/si-base-units/second).

**Set aside, with why.** [crystals.csv](crystals.csv) contains 35 explicit CIF models with lengths from symmetry-expanded sites and periodic images. Each COD number specifies the source CIF. In antimony record 2310880, a=b=3.3012 Å is anomalous relative to neighbouring measurements near 4.30 Å and is quarantined. The 1991 high-pressure cesium two-site CIF is a historical hcp model and cannot resolve four-layer stacking. A monoclinic average tellurium cell cannot reproduce distances along the incommensurate modulation. Complete experimental coordinate sets for every modulation, stacking variant and preparation remain open. The strongest optical lines in this file apply to atoms or ions and cannot determine a visible absorption coefficient for each bulk phase. A complete null search for every possible element–knot assignment is also unavailable. No canonical element-specific HOMFLY, coloured Jones, Alexander or Beasley polynomial was located for these five in the material inspected.
