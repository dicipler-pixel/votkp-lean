# Barium through neodymium

The [five-element ladder](ladder/README.md) contains every archived ionization energy and all five bound-anion rungs. All 285 ordinary steps fall inside the wall. The anion steps fall outside. The latest ASD neutral neodymium entry differs from the pinned archive; both readings, uncertainties and their wall consequences appear in the ladder audit.

| Question | Result and evidence |
|---|---|
| How sharp is the cutoff? | The complete-box interval through Nd still has Ag and Si as its endpoints. [Exact window checks](lean/window/README.md) |
| Which shell is opening? | Different atomic-structure sources disagree about the configuration of the 54-electron Nd ion. The count-based ratio survives both readings. [Xenon sequence](xe_sequence/README.md) |
| Does chemical hardness give the same wall? | The identity depends on which of the two established hardness conventions is used. [Source and notation audit](chemistry/README.md) |
| Can adjacent-atom sharing distinguish a metal? | The Rb and Kr nearest-shell fractions both pass. The spatial second moments separate more strongly with increasing mesh. [Cell-centre and microscopic responses](localization/README.md) |
| Do different optical channels have the same sign? | Absorption and screened loss can change their ordering with energy and state. [Physical controls](controls/README.md) |
| Which structural traits distinguish neighbouring sites? | The two Nd sites can agree on all 12 distances and on their second moments yet have distinct angular environments. [Rotatable comparison and primary structures](site_symmetry/README.md) |
| What is measured per element? | Radii by convention, ambient and pressure phases, heat, transport, optical and nuclear records, with named gaps. [Material cards](materials/README.md) |
