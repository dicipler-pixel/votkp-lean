# The same twelve distances can surround two different sites

**Falsifier.** Suppose the atomic peel, number of neighbours and complete radial list determine a site's local symmetry. Two sites in a single crystal with the same element and the same distance list but different numbers of antipodal bond pairs disprove that claim. A second, independent question is whether the sites have the same magnetic response. The structural comparison and the response measurement have different units and tests.

**DERIVED from crystallographic models.** Seven complete structures give 24 site environments. In double-hexagonal close-packed lanthanum, praseodymium and neodymium the ABAC stacking contains two distinct local cages. In neodymium, every site has six neighbours at 3.627864 Å and six at 3.657900 Å in the mean-position model. The thirteenth is at 5.151857 Å. The A sites have **six opposite bond pairs**; the B and C sites have **three**. All twelve neighbours of an A site have an antipode. Only the six in-plane neighbours of a B or C site do. In both environments there are six same-type and six other-type neighbours. The face-centered control has six opposite pairs at every site and the hexagonal close-packed control three. [Neodymium basis](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9008526.html) · [Lanthanum basis](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9008525.html) · [Praseodymium basis](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9008527.html) · [all site results](site_cages.csv)

**DERIVED — the directional information first appears at third order.** Take the twelve measured-neighbour vectors from a site, using ideal symmetry positions of the refinement. Both site types have zero vector sum. All entries of their second moment Σ rᵢrᵢᵀ are equal between sites of a given crystal. For the basal axes with angle 120°, take ω = exp(2πi/3), so (rₓ+i rᵧ)/a = u+ωv in fractional basal coordinates u,v. The third moment T₃ = Σ(u+ωv)³/12 is zero on A. On the B and C sites it is ±(1/18 + ω/9), with |T₃|²=1/108. These values use exact rational coefficients in the basis 1,ω. Rotating the Cartesian in-plane reference direction changes its phase by three times the angle, leaving the magnitude invariant. The opposite site values cancel when averaged over a cell. This is a directional structural moment. A nonzero value assigns neither a knot nor a handedness.

**MEASURED — an independent site-resolved test exists in this element.** Moon, Lebech and Thompson deduced the two sublattice magnetic susceptibilities of elemental neodymium from polarized-neutron diffraction over 1.7–100 K. The two susceptibilities agreed above 40 K in their measurements. Below 19.9 K, the near-cubic site had the larger susceptibility. This identifies a physical response for which the two environments can differ at fixed chemical composition, as well as a temperature range where they agree within the experiment. The geometric distinction alone gives no magnetic transition temperature. [Primary measurement, 1983](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.27.354)

**HYPOTHESIS — what this adds to the ball map.** If every neighbour is assigned the free-atom clock, Γ at every junction is zero. The peels and radial gaps alone cannot select the two site responses in this crystal. A model that also keeps how the twelve faces are oriented can distinguish the sites before computing electronic response. A further test would calculate a site-resolved susceptibility with the diffraction structure and temperature dependence, then compare its ordering with the neutron result. Treating these two magnetic susceptibilities as peel clocks would require a separate, justified mapping to the model's clock variable.

**Reproduction.** Fetch the seven CIFs in [source_models.csv](source_models.csv) into one directory and run `python check_cages.py /path/to/cifs` with Gemmi installed. Each CIF is hash-checked. Fractional coordinates are snapped to symmetry twelfths only after a tolerance check. Expanding two and three periodic images on each side gives the same thirteen neighbours. All selected input structures have a clear 12–13 distance break. The distance and directional results refer to mean positions. Thermal fluctuations and instantaneous broken symmetries require additional data.
