# Promethium to terbium · the atom, the crystal and the light between

**HYPOTHESIS, Beasley's framework.** Put the nucleus at the centre and the electron cloud at the wall. The internal face apex is tanh(ln z/4). Identical, identically prepared external ports match at z=1. A spatial separation in ångströms, the dimensionless internal step and the optical response of two bulk media are three independently testable readings. The model's claim that vectors create space and volume, with light leading, remains a hypothesis. The loop test uses the intrinsic step; no spacetime metric enters. ## Which optical reading changes when the direction changes?

**MEASURED and DERIVED.** Zhou et al. (2025) used reflection electron energy loss spectroscopy to estimate bulk n and extinction κ for Sm, Gd and Tb. For three preselected visible energies, take the complex index N=n+iκ and the normal-incidence reflected electric-field coefficient r=(N_A−N_B)/(N_A+N_B), assuming isotropic homogeneous media, unit relative permeability and an ideal planar interface. The electrical conduction projector X and the optical modulus |r|² are separate functions. A bulk phase's colour also depends on surface oxidation, texture and illumination. Values below are calculated fields at hypothetical interfaces, without a direct interface reflectometry result or reported per-row uncertainties. [2025 study and authors' data](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpcc.5c00770) · [preselected tests](OPTICAL_PROTOCOL.md) · [n,κ and both dielectric columns](optical_inputs.csv) · [all junctions](optical_junctions.csv)

**DERIVED.** The identical-media field coefficient vanishes and swapping different media negates the complex field in every case. The real-index-only ranking fails at all three preselected energies on the 2025 rows. An earlier independent extraction for Sm materially matters: replacing only the 2025 Sm optical constants with the 2023 Sm constants leaves the *complex-index ordering* the same, but now the real-index-only comparator agrees at all three energies. This is a central-value sensitivity result. At 2.5 eV the 2025 n,κ,ε₁,ε₂ each agree to 5×10⁻⁶ with their separately interpolated midpoints at 2.4 and 2.6. Directly squaring N at 2.5 therefore need not exactly reproduce those columns: for Tb the ε₂ discrepancy is 0.0072451. Reconstructing N from the tabulated ε instead leaves all the complex-index rankings unchanged. [2023 Sm measurement and inversion](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-30770-1) · [code](optical_junctions.py) · [cross-check](optical_audit.txt)

**HYPOTHESIS / mainstream-blend watch.** Within this optical comparison the phase carries information lost by squaring. Optical reciprocity does not prove Russell's claims about the directions of uncharged flows or decay. A metal's interband absorption is a measurable setting in which a model that keeps only one real clock must specify exactly what happens to the second optical component. The three central ranks above offer a test, contingent on the chosen source and a material-specific mapping.

## What remains open

**HYPOTHESIS.** The VOTKP v2 link threshold Xᵢⱼ>B/(3n) uses the projector, a site budget B and a specified neighbour graph. Volume fraction, ordinary metallic conduction and a first-shell count do not give those quantities. In two measured crystal geometries our one-orbital four-contact calculation passes for *every finite staggered gap* and for the zero-gap case. At zero mass it assigns X≈0.050574 versus 1/48 to the shortest links of both Sm and Tb. Real materials need pressure-specific orbital and spin projectors; the other three have no such run here. There is no empirical Bruggeman filling-side classification for any of the five. The distinct x-ray pressure volumes are directly testable even when the reduced projector cannot tell the materials apart. [derivation, converged grids, falsifiers and true knot test](CONTACT_MODEL.md)

**Set aside, with why.** No complete measured cell basis was secured at every temperature and pressure. The [13 COD refinements](crystals.csv) carry original source, condition when given and calculated first distances. Some historical metadata lack P or T; no numerical phase boundary is inferred from these. Eu-IV/V need their actual displacement functions to determine the full contact distribution. An average monoclinic cell alone cannot supply it. The Pm boiling value is tabulated, with no primary measurement located in this pass. For spin-zero ground states with a nonzero raw LiveChart dipole field, keep the raw column for traceability and use exact zero for the ground-state dipole. Where a fitted optical inversion has neither covariance nor uncertainties, a strict numerical rank is conditional. The normalized HOMFLY, Jones and Alexander results apply only to the explicitly selected single shortest cycles of the two pressure phases.
