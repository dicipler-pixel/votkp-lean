# Dysprosium through ytterbium

[All 340 archived energies and 335 ordinary ratios](ladder/README.md) stay on the inside for the ordinary wall test. Dy and Tm have estimate-grade anion inputs outside. Ho and Er have experimental upper limits without a secure ground-anion assignment; Yb lacks an adopted stable ground anion.

The [holmium–dysprosium same-charge comparison](materials/holmium_dysprosium_decay.md) uses absolute removal energies and an independent nuclear mass measurement. Recomputing from [three raw trap frequency ratios](mass_anchors/README.md) locates the same sign crossing at 56+→57+. The signed interspecies reflection reads the next *increment*. It needs the energy sum as its weight and an absolute mass anchor to recover the cumulative mass ordering. The intermediate charge result is a source-conditioned energetic prediction.

The [five material cards](materials/README.md) distinguish crystal phases, optical lines, ionic radii, isotope discoveries and evaluated nuclear quantities.
