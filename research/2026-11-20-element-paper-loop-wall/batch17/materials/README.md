# Thallium to astatine

**HYPOTHESIS (Jeromie Beasley).** The nuclear centre, electronic wall and gap between two elements carry different records. A peel changes an isolated atom's charge. A contact between equivalent sites can retain a matched clock even when a phase transition changes atomic spacing. A zero junction Γ therefore need not imply a fixed gap in ångströms. This batch compares three independent observables: bound-state decay at matched ionic charge, nearest-neighbour spacings in measured solid phases, and occupied electronic channels in mesoscopic bismuth. ### Thallium stacks differently on heating and compression

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** A single phase name predicted from pressure or temperature without a specified path fails here. At 297 K an hcp cell has a = 3.456, c = 5.525 Å and shortest spacing 3.40774 Å. NIST records the low-pressure hcp→bcc transition at 503 K. A reported bcc cell has a = 3.882 Å, d = √3a/2 = 3.36191 Å. Room-temperature compression to 3.5 GPa drives an hcp→fcc transition, and the 2016 work maintained fcc to 125 GPa. A 1994 fcc structure has a = 4.33 Å and d = 3.06177 Å; its CIF omits a pressure and we do not impute one. Tl's low-pressure superconducting maximum near 2.395 K around 0.195 GPa illustrates that normal- and superconducting-state transport need separate labels. [CIF ledger](crystals.csv) · [2016 high-pressure experiment](https://doi.org/10.1088/0953-8984/28/44/445401) · [Tc versus pressure comparison, 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6379445/) · [NIST 2025](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2312).

### Lead gives a direct loading-history test

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** The room-temperature fcc cell at 298.15 K has a = 4.95004 Å, with twelve neighbours at a/√2 = 3.50021 Å. Slow compression gives fcc→hcp around 14 GPa. A 2019 experiment with He pressure medium tracked hcp to 130.8 GPa without detecting bcc; the measured V_atom reached 16.1940 Å³, down from 22.2010 Å³ at the first tabulated 28.16 GPa point. Other static loading conditions have seen a partial hcp→bcc transformation above about 107 GPa. The 1979 hcp Pb film in the COD ledger is a quenched thin-film specimen and must not substitute for a high-pressure cell. [298.15 K fcc and quenched hcp refinements](crystals.csv) · [Static He loading and Table 2, 2019](https://doi.org/10.3390/min9110684).

**MEASURED / falsifier.** A model making fcc→hcp compulsory on *all* compression paths predicts resolved hcp during sufficiently fast loading, too. September 2026 time-resolved diffraction instead found increasing fcc stacking faults to about 20 GPa followed by bcc at 27 GPa on a nanosecond shock path. At 45.4 ± 2.1 GPa diffuse scattering indicated partial melting; 2695 K there is a calculated temperature. No resolved hcp intermediate appeared on that experimental path. Tracking stacking, deviatoric stress, time and temperature can distinguish these specimens; their common isolated-element ladder cannot. [2026 primary shock experiment](https://doi.org/10.1103/6qmk-qs9l).

### Bismuth has multiple kinds of boundary

**MEASURED.** The 2019 A7 structure at 298(2) K has hexagonal a = 4.5481(5), c = 11.86 Å. The refinement gives each Bi three contacts at 3.0723(4) Å and three at 3.5294(4) Å. A 5%-of-shortest rule counts three and a 15% rule counts six. The longer/shorter ratio is about 1.1488. Monoclinic Bi-II at 2.54 GPa and 295 K has a,b,c = 6.67256, 6.1108, 3.30013 Å and β = 110.412°, giving distinct near distances of 3.15494 and 3.20729 Å. Diffraction under higher pressure finds Bi-III around 6 GPa, and bcc Bi-V above 7.7 GPa; the high-temperature Bi-IV field is a separate region. [2019 refinement with explicit contacts](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/7123352.html) · [Bi-II refinement](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/8100521.html) · [High-pressure diffraction](https://doi.org/10.1080/08957959.2018.1541456).

**MEASURED / falsifier.** If conductivity followed from one bulk on/off switch, very pure ambient Bi, mesoscopic Bi on a superconducting substrate and a thin device under transverse magnetic field could all be assigned the same state from their element label. Ambient crystalline Bi instead shows bulk superconductivity below 0.53 mK, with estimated zero-temperature critical field 5.2 μT. Compressed phases show transitions of order 3.9, 7 and 8 K for II, III and V. A 2025 device about 68 nm thick exhibited an anomalous Hall response from 15 mK to 300 K, without a resolved magnetoresistance through ±30 T in that device. A different 2025 study reported a spin-helical hinge-state loop in mesoscopic Bi on superconducting V₃Si. The latter is a proximity experiment. A real conducting loop at a hinge does not yield a trefoil or a polynomial without tracing and closing the embedded path. [Ambient bulk Meissner study](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaf8227) · [Phase-specific comparison](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-41401-z) · [Thin-Bi Hall experiment](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.066603) · [Hinge loop, 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.07533).

### Polonium gets denser while all six closest contacts lengthen

**HYPOTHESIS / falsifier.** A phase-independent identification d ∝ V_atom^(1/3) predicts that the signs of a contact-length change and volume change agree. Batch 14 Yb failed that identification when its coordination changed. Here we hold the number of strict nearest neighbours **fixed at six** and test it again. [Yb control](../../batch14/materials/ytterbium_structure.md).

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** A 1966 X-ray redetermination used a ²⁰⁸Po/²⁰⁹Po mixture to reduce the self-heating and ingrowth problems associated with ²¹⁰Po. It reports simple-cubic α-Po, a = 3.359(1) Å, and rhombohedral β-Po, a = 3.368(1) Å with α = 98°14.4′. Each primitive rhombohedron contains one site. Writing u = cos α gives V = a³√(1−3u²+2u³). Enumeration finds the six first contacts in each phase have length a. The shortest spacing **grows 0.268%**, while the atomic volume **falls 2.656%** from 37.8992 to 36.8925 Å³. X-ray densities increase from 9.142 to 9.392 g/cm³. Each a has a 0.001 Å quoted uncertainty. Even three printed uncertainties leave d_β−d_α positive. With those worst-case lengths the volume reversal persists for rhombohedral angles above 95.227° up to the reported 98.24°; the accessible abstract supplies no angular uncertainty. NIST's 2025 evaluations list cP→R at 327 ± 1.5 K. Absolute values differ slightly between the 1949 and 1966 refinements. [1966 isotope-controlled diffraction](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1902(66)80270-1) · [Two refinements and distance shells](polonium_cells.csv) · [Calculation and angular sensitivity](polonium_cell.py) · [NIST, 2025](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2312).

**DERIVED.** The strict-neighbour budget allocation is B/(3×6) = B/18 on both sides. The electronic budget B and the individual link weights remain unspecified. A change of angle alone can reverse the density/contact relation without changing this geometric denominator. Each phase also has inversion about every Bravais-lattice site, pairing every odd-determinant walk with its mirror in an inversion-invariant neighbour selection. The nonzero embedded third-moment reading for chiral Yb consequently has an exact zero control on both ideal Po lattices. [Yb statistic definition](../../batch14/materials/yb_neighbour_graph.py).

### Liquid bismuth supplies three different spacing tests

**HYPOTHESIS / falsifier.** If compression at fixed elemental identity simply rescales every length of the ball picture by the same factor, the liquid’s first and second peak positions in its radial pair distribution g(r) must change by the same fraction, holding r₂/r₁ constant. A systematic change of this dimensionless ratio directly rejects uniform rescaling for these observables. Two peaks of a liquid are distribution maxima rather than the first and second bond lengths of a periodic crystal.

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** In the 2025 diffraction study of liquid Bi along its melting line from 1.1 to 4.0 GPa, density increased about 6%. Uniform rescaling would give 1.06^(−1/3) ≈ 0.981, a 1.9% contraction in *both* distances. Instead the first maximum was nearly fixed (slight central expansion), the second moved inward by about 1.5% (0.01 nm), and r₂/r₁ fell from 1.99 to nearly 1.93. The first-shell coordination integrated with fixed limits rose from about 8 to 8.6, while the second rose from about 41 to 44. This extends the solid Yb and Po comparisons to a liquid, where “nearest neighbour” must specify how the shell is defined and site-by-site Yb graphs do not transfer automatically. The numerical source measures a path along the melting line, so pressure and temperature both vary. The public article gives these rounded changes and figures; its authors make the raw curves available on request. [Ben Shalom et al., Communications Materials 6, 246 (2025), Figs. 2–3](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43246-025-00967-0).

**MEASURED / open discriminant.** In separate measurements the liquid's resistivity dropped sharply on heating at constant pressure, in a region approaching the melting line, while the 2025 diffraction study found continuous evolution in its local structural fit. A 2026 X-ray phase-contrast study of solid–liquid interfaces then measured the melting line through 55 GPa and found its slope decreased substantially under compression of Bi-V. The three experiments use different observables and follow different pressure/temperature paths. A common-sample, overlapping-path measurement of resistivity and g(r) would determine whether an electrical change precedes or follows a structural change at the same conditions. [Transport experiment, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.5001916) · [Local order, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43246-025-00967-0) · [Pressure-dependent melt line, October 2026](https://doi.org/10.1103/ldw3-5x6p).

### Astatine separates single-atom data from bulk predictions

**MEASURED / HYPOTHESIS.** The ²¹¹At detachment threshold is precise enough to place the anion rung well inside the wall. The neutral ionization potential is independently measured. Neither supplies a measured crystal structure, lattice spacing, density, four-probe conductivity, thermal conductivity or superconducting transition. The 2013 relativistic structure search proposes a monatomic metal at 1 atm and considers superconductivity possible. A measured insulating molecular solid would falsify that particular bulk prediction, without changing the gas-phase photodetachment result. No elemental At crystal appeared in the COD search used here. [Measured EA](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17599-2) · [Measured IP](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms2819) · [Condensed-phase theory](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.111.116404).

**MEASURED / DERIVED / interface test.** There is an experimental option short of producing a bulk At crystal. A 2025 study measured distributions of At deposited on surfaces, then inferred adsorption energetics and chemical assignments with a Monte Carlo model. The volatile species assigned to At⁰ gave −ΔHₐ𝒹ₛ on ice of 41(+6/−5) kJ/mol and a one-sided value below 43 kJ/mol on silica. The oxide/hydroxide assignment matters: the species assigned to AtOH gave 71(+8/−5) kJ/mol. These are partly overlapping ice/silica constraints for At⁰, so they carry no resolved ordering of those two substrates. The experiment supports keeping a moisture-sensitive interface and the chemical identity of the adsorbate in the ball record. The isolated-atom EA, as well as the substrate-independent element label, cannot specify which chemical species has reached the surface. This supplies evidence for heterogeneous contact, without an At–At lattice, site projector or bulk conduction measurement. [Surface distributions, competing chemical assignments and inferred enthalpies, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10967-025-10335-4).

**HYPOTHESIS / falsifier.** If the element label, isotope A and electronic rung completely specified the nuclear charge geometry, two long-lived nuclear states of the same isotope seen through the same neutral-atom transition would give the same mean-square charge radius. Compare the 2018 laser study’s isomer and ground rows before changing any electron count. Its quoted statistical and electronic-factor systematic errors can be highly correlated, so we add their absolute magnitudes in each difference and check a three-times-quoted-error box, without assigning a Gaussian confidence level. **MEASURED / DERIVED.** The same data array supplies 24 nuclear-state entries from A = 195 to 211 and seven isomer–ground comparisons. All seven central differences are positive; two remain positive at all corners of the three-error boxes. For ¹⁹⁷At the difference is (−0.133)−(−0.296) = +0.163 fm² and the conservative interval is [+0.055,+0.271] fm². For ¹⁹⁹At it is (−0.075)−(−0.265) = +0.190 fm² with interval [+0.088,+0.292] fm². The ²⁰⁵At reference cancels in each difference. In these two nuclei the isomer and ground-state relative order is resolved even with this conservative uncertainty treatment. The fixed Z, N and electronic charge therefore need a nuclear state label to describe the centre's size. The remaining five contrasts retain their unresolved sign at this coverage. The ²⁰⁶At spin alternative is separate: Table II uses I = 6, while its footnote gives Δ⟨r²⟩ = +0.009(7){1} fm² if I = 5; it enters none of these contrasts. **MEASURED / two-colour control.** Table I allows a test *before* the nuclear radius is extracted. In the same neutral ¹⁹⁷At atom, switching between nuclear ground and isomer changes the 216-nm line by [−2975−(−7190)] = **+4215 MHz** and the 795-nm line by [1598−3466] = **−1868 MHz**, reading isomer minus ground. The electronic field-shift response depends on the chosen transition. The 795-nm sign for ¹⁹⁹At is also resolved: 912−3099 = −2187 MHz, twice-error interval [−2577,−1797]. **RECOGNISED / falsifier.** ²⁰⁸Pb, ²⁰⁹Bi, ²¹⁰Po and ²¹¹At give an N = 126 isotonic series, adding a proton at every step. Spin and decay change along it. ²⁰⁹Bi itself undergoes α decay to ²⁰⁵Tl. The later bolometric study measured a total half-life (2.01 ± 0.08) × 10¹⁹ yr and a (98.8 ± 0.3)% ground-state branch. Element-level labels like “stable” cannot replace a specified nuclide, decay channel and time scale. A proposed absolute radius trend all the way through At would require an absolute anchor with propagated uncertainty for the At isotope-shift chain. [First observation, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01541) · [Decay branches and total half-life, 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22401058/).
