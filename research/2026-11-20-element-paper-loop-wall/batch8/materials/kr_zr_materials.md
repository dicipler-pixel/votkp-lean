## B–D, F, G. The two readings, with a separate transport test

**DERIVED.** An internal face is specified by r = ln z and apex (√z−1)/(√z+1). Equivalent facing environments have unit junction clock ratio in the matched-clock model. For on-site block C and B = Tr(C−C²), publishing a shell fraction requires B>0. Equivalent neighbours can be judged by X > B/(3n); for inequivalent bonds report each X and sum the shell. A Hamiltonian, band filling and site partition are inputs. Their absence leaves the side of one third physically undetermined even in known metals. In hcp Y and Zr the six adjacent-layer contacts and six in-plane ones have different lengths. Defining a twelve-link coordination shell and assuming twelve equal link weights are different decisions.

### Krypton

**HYPOTHESIS, rejection condition first.** The claim that identical junction clocks imply conduction fails if a gapped closed-shell crystal has matched face clocks and remains insulating. **DERIVED.** In the decoupled closed-shell limit C is a projector, so B=0, X=0 and 0/0 gives no shell fraction. A finite-hopping calculation with empty states plus a size-scaled transport response will test an actual Kr crystal. An ambient Kr gas has no fixed lattice.

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** The fcc cell at 58 K has a=5.721 Å, giving d=a/√2=4.045 Å. A low-temperature hcp refinement has a=4.00 Å and c=6.53 Å at 26 K. Solid Kr under pressure can change fcc/hcp stacking. A 2025 experiment found stacking disorder during rapid freezing. Melting the crystal removes a persistent set of lattice neighbours. Crystal geometry can change without changing the atomic peel ledger. No measured elemental Kr superconducting phase was located in this search. **VOTKP v1** intrinsic atomic/crystal knot assignment: none found. A stacking sequence alone does not select a closed oriented curve. [COD 9008471](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9008471.html) · [COD 9012404](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012404.html) · [2025 freezing experiment](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12224082/) · [pressure experiment](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0254842).

### Rubidium

**HYPOTHESIS, rejection condition first.** A fixed atomic Kr-core clock cannot predict a change in conducting phase when pressure varies at fixed chemical identity. Reject any proposed single-clock law if it gives the same transport response on both sides of a pressure-driven structural change while the measured responses differ. **MEASURED / DERIVED.** At 297 K a bcc refinement gives a=5.700 Å and d=√3a/2=4.936 Å. Under pressure the structural sequence includes bcc, fcc, oC52, an incommensurate host–guest phase, tI4, oC16 and hP4. In the hP4 refinement at 264(8) GPa, a=2.5070(14) Å and c=8.258(17) Å; the authors give a shortest contact of 2.507 Å. Resistivity provides evidence of superconductivity near 2 K at 55 GPa (oC16). Choose host, guest and host–guest port pairs separately in the incommensurate phase. An identical chemical symbol does not make these sites symmetry equivalent. [COD 9012876](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012876.html) · [2021 diffraction and refinements](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.103.224103) · [2019 superconductivity evidence](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.100.041109).

**MEASURED, VOTKP v1 positive control.** Hall et al. created and detected a knot soliton in the order parameter of a spinor ⁸⁷Rb Bose–Einstein condensate (2016). Different director preimages form linked rings. This supplies experimentally prepared matter for a Hopf-link test and also an unprepared control at the same isotope. It supplies no element-specific HOMFLY, Alexander, coloured Jones, or Beasley volume polynomial for the isolated atom. **HYPOTHESIS.** Reject identification with an intrinsic atomic knot if the linked texture can be switched by preparation while the free-atom ladder stays fixed. [Hall et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nphys3624).

### Strontium

**HYPOTHESIS, rejection condition first.** Compare two paths to the same pressure, compressing cold versus compressing at room temperature then cooling. A model that predicts one geometry and one superconducting transition for both histories fails if diffraction and transition temperatures disagree. **MEASURED.** In the 35–42 GPa range the 2024 experiment reports superconducting transition temperatures differing by about a factor of two between the protocols. Cryogenically compressed monoclinic Sr-IV persisted to at least 60 GPa, whereas Sr-V with incommensurate order appears during room-temperature compression. This already rejects a junction description using pressure and atomic ladder alone. A state variable describing phase and preparation is required for that comparison. [2024 joint diffraction/transport experiment](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.110.174519).

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Ambient fcc Sr in the 25 °C record has a=6.076 Å and d=4.296 Å. The 2025 NIST evaluation places fcc→bcc at 820(3) K at atmospheric pressure. Historical hcp Sr structure entries are listed individually in the crystal CSV; modern thermodynamic evaluations recognize the fcc and bcc equilibrium phases and identify hydrogen contamination as a source of scatter. **VOTKP v1** elemental knot assignment: none found. An incommensurate period ratio does not supply a unique closed path. [COD 9012726](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9012726.html) · [thermodynamic review](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/srd/jpcrd447.pdf) · [NIST TN 2312](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2312).

### Yttrium

**HYPOTHESIS, rejection condition first.** A literal one-electron frozen-orbital map fails if adjacent ground-state configurations require orbital rearrangement. **MEASURED / evaluated.** Y has [Kr]4d5s², Y⁺ has [Kr]5s² and Y²⁺ has [Kr]4d. The threshold is an energy difference between relaxed ground states. At the Kr opening the last outer removal is 4d and the next removal is 4p. Its bare principal-shell ratio is one. Rb and Sr reach the same Kr core through 5s→4p, with bare ratio 25/16. This is a test of how to label the opening curve by actual charge state. [NIST ASD](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/ASD/ie.pl?spectra=Y&units=1&shells_out=on&level_out=on).

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Hcp Y has a=3.6474 Å and c=5.7306 Å in COD 9010984; the shorter adjacent-plane contacts are √(a²/3+c²/4)=3.5559 Å and basal contacts are a. The measured single-crystal resistivity is directional. At atmospheric pressure the 2025 assessment puts hcp→bcc at 1751 K. Compression accesses Sm-type, dhcp, distorted close-packed and ultimately orthorhombic oF16 above about 106 GPa, where superconductivity is observed around 20 K. The symmetry of the preceding distorted phase has competing refinements. **VOTKP v1** intrinsic knot assignment: none found. **HYPOTHESIS.** Test the anisotropic T-dependent conductivity against separate basal/adjacent-layer projector weights; the wrong sign of the measured directional difference rejects that mapping for this material. [COD 9010984](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9010984.html) · [single-crystal resistivity](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.116.1446) · [diffraction (2020)](https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/structural-phase-transitions-in-yttrium-up-to-183-gpa/) · [NIST TN 2312](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.TN.2312).

### Zirconium

**HYPOTHESIS, rejection condition first.** A proposed abrupt change in the junction weights at a new high-pressure phase must survive control of deviatoric stress and reproduce independently measured discontinuities. **MEASURED.** The 2026 diffraction study to 240 GPa finds no evidence for an additional β→β′ transition above 80 GPa in its data. The α (hcp), ω (hexagonal) and β (bcc) structures are distinct inputs. The ambient α phase in COD 9008523 has a=3.232 Å, c=5.147 Å and adjacent-layer d=3.1788 Å. The 2025 atmospheric assessment gives hcp→bcc at 1136 K. The low-temperature bulk transition quoted by Ekin is 0.61 K at ambient pressure. **VOTKP v1** intrinsic atomic/crystal knot assignment: none found. A theoretical electronic-structure study predicts a type-II nodal line and nodal surface of elemental Zr, in reciprocal space; read any proposed winding there subject to its symmetry and spin–orbit assumptions. A closed nodal line alone does not establish a knot. [COD 9008523](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/9008523.html) · [2026 diffraction](https://doi.org/10.1103/zp3m-kjpc) · [2020 band calculation](https://doi.org/10.3389/fchem.2020.585753) · [Ekin](https://www.researchmeasurements.com/figures/ExpTechLTMeas_Apdx_English.pdf).

## E. The nuclear centre and isotope controls

**MEASURED / evaluated.** [isotopes.csv](isotopes.csv) has all 181 Kr–Zr ground-state records returned by the IAEA LiveChart endpoint queried in this pass. The API labels its own extraction 18 October 2023. Missing fields are blank. The CSV preserves source magnetic-dipole fields **raw** because some entries beside definite spin-zero ground states are nonzero (e.g. ⁸⁸Sr, 2.4(4) μN). These cannot describe a static ground-state dipole for J=0. An explicit separate column gives the symmetry-enforced zero. For other isotopes check state-specific evaluations before using precision moments. [IAEA API and schema](https://iaea-nds.github.io/lc_api_notebook/) · [independent moment evaluation, Stone](https://www-nds.iaea.org/publications/indc/indc-nds-0794.pdf).

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** The ball centre carries the isotope’s Z protons and N=A−Z neutrons. Counting uud and udd gives net up = 2Z+N and net down = Z+2N. All five rows below have **N=50**. This is a cross-element control with a common neutron count while electronic structure and proton number change. RMS charge radii have units of fm; they measure the nuclear charge distribution. A no-adjustable-scale equality of these radii with an electronic face apex is dimensionally undefined.

## Set aside with reasons

**RECOGNISED.** [crystals.csv](crystals.csv) gives the measured metrics and publication years of 24 selected COD records and shortest distances derivable from verified ideal fcc, bcc and hcp bases. Chemical partial sublattices retrieved by searches for a single element were excluded. Where the basis or modulation is unspecified, a cell metric alone cannot determine the shortest contact. A complete phase-by-phase distance atlas still needs coordinate-resolved refinements for the high-pressure and incommensurate phases. For an incommensurate solid, specify which two subsystems and how the relative phase is sampled.

**Mainstream-blend watch.** Crystal heat and transport measurements, nuclear radii, and the scalar atomic polarizability are comparison observables. No conversion of ln z into a crystal distance, no assignment of a conductivity to an atomic clock and no assignment of a Russell-current mechanism to an unmeasured current has been made. Testing light as a driver would require a specified wavelength, timing, excitation density, fixed preparation, and a measured transport or structural response. Reject the proposed light effect if the predicted response disappears on temperature matching against a dark control.
