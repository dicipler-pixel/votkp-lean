# Structure-resolved pressure checks for Kr–Zr

**DERIVED.** Run `python pressure_contacts.py` with NumPy and Gemmi installed to regenerate [pressure_contacts.csv](pressure_contacts.csv). Inputs are selected published central cell metrics and fractional coordinates. We expand the named space-group setting, deduplicate symmetry-equivalent sites, verify cell multiplicity, and enumerate periodic displacement vectors. If a trial displacement has length at most R, its j-th fractional component has modulus at most R times reciprocal-axis norm j. This gives the finite image bounds in the code, including the monoclinic cell. Five output decimals allow exact regression comparisons; the underlying refinements usually support only two or three decimals in Å. Pressure values are nominal room-temperature diffraction conditions unless otherwise specified in the references. Full site occupancy and the reported mean coordinates are assumed for the periodic-component calculation.

**DERIVED, rejection conditions specified.** Wrong multiplicity rejects a symmetry setting. Failure of a proposed distorted fcc cell to recover the fcc spacing at the ideal coordinates rejects its origin choice. A discrepancy larger than the experimental precision with the published minimum rejects a distance calculation. All multiplicities, the ideal-Y check and the independent Sr-IV and Rb-VII shortest-distance checks passed. Experimental occupancy uncertainties, atomic thermal motion and coordinate errors are additional physical uncertainties.

## A zero control for the screw test

**DERIVED.** For a screw isometry g(x)=Rx+t with rotational part a half-turn, R²=I. Taking one seed and traversing one repeated screw gives increments d₀=g(p)−p, d₁=R d₀, d₂=R²d₀=d₀. Consequently χ=(d₀×d₁)·d₂=0 for every seed, pitch and axial orientation. This applies to each single 2₁ orbit in Rb-III (C222₁). Crystal handedness remains a separate question. The same single-orbit sign used in β-Mn and trigonal Se can be blind here by exact geometry. For an element-wide knot assignment, choose and justify a multi-bond path, its closure and orientation before inspecting an enantiomer. [Rb-III structure](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.88.155503)

**RECOGNISED, set aside.** A cross-check of Rb-III bond counting found one discrepancy with the reproduced table. Its seven independent sites list 10,10,8,10,11,10,11 contacts between approximately 3.17 and 4.1 Å. Our direct counts through 4.1 Å from its central coordinates are 10,10,8,**11**,11,10,11. For site 4 the eleventh distance is 4.00738 Å and joins sites 4 and 2; the site-2 count agrees. The overall shortest distance is 3.16948 Å, agreeing with the published 3.17 Å. Use an explicitly defined neighbour cutoff for coordination normalization. The check is a flag for review of the reproduced table, not a correction to the original coordinates.

**Set aside with why.** Early intermediate-pressure hR9 and hP4 Y periods still need matched cell parameters, and modulated host–guest phases need relative displacements to determine physical cross-network nearest contacts. The available Kr and Zr entries do not constitute exhaustive temperature–pressure grids. Uncertainties of the derived distances were not propagated from the full crystallographic covariance matrix.
