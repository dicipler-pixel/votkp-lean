# Niobium through rhodium • companion to the peel ledger

Beasley's *The Loop Wall: Peeling the Elements from Hydrogen to Zinc* (21 November 2026), §§5–7, supplies the two separate observables used here. A clock step belongs on an energy link within an atom. Two equivalent facing sites in the same phase have a matched external clock in his model. The measured nuclear and crystal properties below are inputs for testing that assignment. The adjacent batch ledger gives the energies, affinities, directed ratios and face coordinates.

## B–D, F, G. Internal and junction tests by element

**HYPOTHESIS, in Beasley's terms.** “The vector determines the direction, that may be decay.” A direct test of the proposed external-junction rule requires evaluation of Xᵢⱼ=‖Pᵢⱼ‖² and Bᵢ=Tr(Cᵢ−Cᵢ²) in the same orthonormal localized basis and specified phase. A metallic specimen can carry current regardless of whether any particular neighbour passes Xᵢⱼ>Bᵢ/(3n). For all five materials the side of that link cutoff is open in this supplement. In particular the Bruggeman third needs an independently defined volume fraction and phase topology. **DERIVED.** In a perfect elemental crystal with symmetry-equivalent identical neighbours, matching clocks gives z=1, r=0, Γ=0 at their junction. Site equivalence alone fixes no hopping strength, electron population in the gap or distance in Å. The internal face apex remains tanh((ln z)/4). [Beasley, loop-wall research](https://github.com/dicipler-pixel/votkp-lean/tree/element-paper/loop-wall/research/2026-11-20-element-paper-loop-wall).

**DERIVED** nearest contacts in [crystals.csv](crystals.csv) use the cited conventional cell and its ideal monoatomic basis. In bcc, d=√3 a/2. In fcc, d=a/√2. In hcp the two candidate shells have d=a and d=√(a²/3+c²/4). The shorter is retained, even when the second is close. These are nucleus-to-nucleus lengths. Each COD ID resolves at https://www.crystallography.net/cod/ID.html (replace ID with its digits). The older reference cells are explicitly preserved as dated specimens. No physical wall thickness is subtracted. [COD](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/).

**RECOGNISED.** Compare the previous bcc Rb, high-temperature bcc Zr and the bcc Nb/Mo pair. Their symmetry allows the same number of nearest contacts. Their electronic filling, lattice constant and phase differ. Tc and Ru offer the analogous comparison in hcp. Holding lattice symmetry fixed does not hold transport fixed. Across this batch the neutral [Kr] core is the same, its opening moves from k=5 to k=9, and Mo⁺, Tc²⁺, Ru³⁺ and Rh⁴⁺ share a 4d⁵ ground configuration. Nb neutral has 41 electrons as well, but 4d⁴5s. This is a counterexample to inferring the configuration from isoelectronic count alone. **DERIVED** for the shell label 4d→4p the same-principal-n hydrogenic ratio is 1. The measured thresholds include finite screening, angular-momentum dependence and relaxation. [NIST ASD](https://doi.org/10.18434/T4W30F).

**VOTKP v1, element by element.** Nb — none found. Mo — none found. Tc — none found. Ru — none found. Rh — none found. This grade applies to a published assignment of an isolated element's own HOMFLY, coloured Jones, Alexander or Beasley polynomial in the material examined. A genuine related observation is a localized ring-shaped vortex domain in a superconducting Nb film. Its collective state and boundary conditions matter. A ring-shaped domain alone does not identify a closed oriented individual vortex or its knot polynomial. A useful rejection test is to require a specified closed curve in a specified ambient space, including the closure rule in a periodic crystal. Vary origin and boundary choice, then check whether its isotopy class survives. A mirror may reverse a chiral invariant, whereas Γ records reversal of an energy step. **MEASURED / DERIVED.** [isotopes.csv](isotopes.csv) lists the 195 returned ground-state entries for Z=41–45 from the IAEA LiveChart API. Its source snapshot states 18 October 2023, with per-isotope ENSDF cutoffs retained. A blank means unreported in the field. The raw magnetic dipole column is not always ground-state-specific: several 0⁺ entries carry a nonzero source value. Every confirmed spin-zero ground state has exactly zero ground-state magnetic dipole by rotational symmetry. The separate spin-zero column gives that result. Other API dipoles require level-specific confirmation. Net valence quark counts use u=2Z+N, d=Z+2N. The radius is the rms nuclear charge radius. **Mainstream-blend watch / set aside.** Crystal nearest-neighbour distances are measurable lengths. The model's face coordinates are dimensionless. No calibration was fitted to convert between them. These scans cover representative bulk, strained, metastable and pressure phases, with 13 dated conventional cells. A lattice constant and atom-resolved contact net at every reported pressure, film thickness or metastable phase has not been recovered. The isolated atomic spectra supply no unique bulk absorption curve without frequency-dependent optical constants and sample conditions. Tc's affinity in the adjacent ledger is an estimate. Specific measurements of absolute ⁹⁹Tc radius, phase-resolved projectors and closed paths for the knot assignment remain open.
