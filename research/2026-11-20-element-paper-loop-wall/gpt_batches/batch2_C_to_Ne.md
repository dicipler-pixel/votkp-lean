This is a specific trait-change comparison that a smooth monotonic rule in \(Z\) would miss. [NIST ASD](https://physics.nist.gov/PhysRefData/ASD/ionEnergy.html)

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Oxygen melts near 54.37 K and boils near 90.19 K at atmospheric pressure. Its ideal-gas density at 298.15 K and 1 bar is 1.291 kg/m³; its gas thermal conductivity at 300 K is 0.0265 W m\(^{-1}\) K\(^{-1}\). Molecular oxygen is paramagnetic. The low-pressure solid passes through \(\alpha,\beta,\gamma\) phases on warming, with transitions near 23.9 and 43.8 K. A monoclinic \(\alpha\) refinement gives \(a=5.403,b=3.429,c=5.086\) Å and \(\beta=132.53^\circ\). The 1973 cubic \(\gamma\) cell at 46 K has \(a=6.78\) Å. Its shortest molecular-centre spacing in the cubic centre arrangement is \(a/2=3.39\) Å. [tsapps.nist.gov](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=926438\&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**MEASURED.** Under compression, orthorhombic \(\delta\) oxygen and clustered \(\epsilon\) oxygen add different arrangements. A \(\delta\) refinement for \(^{18}\mathrm O_2\) gives \(a=4.2151,b=2.9567,c=6.6897\) Å at 9.6 GPa and 297 K. In \(\epsilon\) oxygen four \(\mathrm O_2\) units form an \(\mathrm O_8\) cluster. A 2025 structural study resolved a discontinuity at **18.1(5) GPa** interpreted as evidence for spin collapse. At much higher pressure, near **100 GPa**, metallic oxygen has shown superconductivity below about **0.6 K**. Those are distinct pressure and temperature regimes. Solid ozone adds another elemental allotrope, with a 2001 orthorhombic refinement giving \(a=7.7888,b=6.6973,c=6.8413\) Å at 5 K. [sharps.ac.cn](https://sharps.ac.cn/en/uploads/soft/20250813/2-250Q3143104J1.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**HYPOTHESIS — Oxygen test.** If a magnetic change, a gap-closing event and the appearance of a conducting path are supposed to be one event, follow magnetic response, diffraction and transport on a common pressure path. The documented separation between the 18 GPa structural anomaly and the megabar conducting regime already rules out identifying those two observations as a single transition. Oxygen is the best of these five for testing Russell’s proposed role for opposed or parallel flows, provided the flow direction and the ordinary magnetic contribution are specified independently.

### 9 · Fluorine — one measured bond, one independently measured room

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Atomic F has a bound anion and electron affinity about 3.40119 eV. The ordinary material is pale-yellow \(\mathrm F_2\). At atmospheric pressure it melts near 53.5 K and boils near 85.0 K. Its ideal-gas density at 298.15 K and 1 bar is 1.533 kg/m³. The 1972 transport evaluation gives a dilute-gas thermal conductivity of order **0.026 W m\(^{-1}\) K\(^{-1}\)** at 300 K; the fluorine evaluation uses a kinetic-theory estimate. An exact directly measured value should not be inferred from that estimate. Dry un-ionized fluorine gas has few free mobile charges. [srd.nist.gov](https://srd.nist.gov/JPCRD/jpcrd16.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**MEASURED.** The 2019 neutron study separates two geometries unusually clearly. At 10 K, \(\alpha\)-\(\mathrm F_2\) is monoclinic \(C2/c\), with \(a=5.4780(12),b=3.2701(7),c=7.2651(17)\) Å, \(\beta=102.088(18)^\circ\), and **intramolecular F–F distance \(1.404(12)\) Å**. Above about 45.6 K, \(\beta\)-\(\mathrm F_2\) is cubic \(Pm\bar3n\). At 48 K the edge is \(6.5314(15)\) Å with eight molecules per cell. Partially occupied sites describe molecular orientation. The minimum separation between all drawn sites can spuriously connect two orientations of the *same* molecule. I used the measured bond for \(\alpha\) and the molecular centres for \(\beta\). No measured superconducting transition of pure elemental fluorine was located. [chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/chem.201805298?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**DERIVED — A gap test in ångströms.** In cubic \(\beta\)-fluorine the shortest centre-to-centre distance is \(a/2=3.2657\) Å. The separate 10 K bond measurement is 1.404 Å. To infer a wall-to-wall gap from the two, one also needs the two molecular orientations and a choice of electronic surface. That makes fluorine a direct test of the proposition that an orientational distribution controls gap sizes even at a fixed lattice constant. A model assigning every pair the same scalar gap would fail if orientation-resolved distances distinguish them.

### 10 · Neon — a monatomic test of compression and light

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Neon’s neutral ground state is closed-shell \(^{1}S_0\), its first ionization costs 21.564541 eV, and its static response is the smallest of these five. The 2023 polarizability compilation recommends 2.66110(3) atomic units and separately lists a more precise experimental determination, 2.6610570(64). The independent 2022 ab initio value is 2.661067(77). These provenance differences are worth keeping in a precision ledger. [journals.aps.org](https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.105.022809?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Neon melts near 24.56 K and boils near 27.10 K at atmospheric pressure. Its ideal-gas density at 298.15 K and 1 bar is 0.814 kg/m³. The 2019 CRC/NIST dilute-gas thermal conductivity at 300 K is **0.0494 W m\(^{-1}\) K\(^{-1}\)**, almost twice the nitrogen value under the same table convention. A June 2026 NIST reference correlation now covers 24.5561–700 K and pressures up to 700 MPa. The lower temperature given for that correlation is the triple point. Ordinary low-field neon is a poor electrical conductor; an excited discharge contains mobile charges and emits orange-red light. I found no established superconductivity of pure elemental Ne. [tsapps.nist.gov](https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=926438\&utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**MEASURED / DERIVED — The compression comparison has one neighbour definition.** For fcc \(^{20}\mathrm{Ne}\), a 1962 measurement gives \(a=4.462\) Å at 4.2 K, hence \(d=a/\sqrt2=3.155\) Å. The 1989 high-pressure diffraction measurement gives \(a=2.927\) Å at 110.4 GPa and 300 K, hence \(d=2.070\) Å. Your fixed-radius geometric clearance \(d-2(0.67)\) consequently falls from **1.815 to 0.730 Å**. Both values remain positive. Temperature also changes between these records, so the comparison does not determine an isothermal compression derivative. Recent shock work provides an independent liquid-neon regime. [COD diffraction records](https://www.crystallography.net/cod/). [journals.aps.org](https://journals.aps.org/prb/abstract/10.1103/y554-7vhs?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**HYPOTHESIS — Neon test.** A monatomic fcc crystal has no internal diatomic stretch to confuse with a lattice clock. Select two directions through the same crystal, control thickness, pressure, temperature and wavelength, and specify the predicted difference in transit time or phase. A persistent directional difference in a symmetry-equivalent pair would need a specified symmetry-breaking mechanism. This makes Ne a useful baseline for your two-path experiment before interpreting graphite or oxygen.

## Mainstream-blend watch and findings set aside

- **RECOGNISED — A useful structural comparison spans three elements.** \(\delta\)-nitrogen, \(\gamma\)-oxygen and \(\beta\)-fluorine share the cubic \(Pm\bar3n\) molecular-centre arrangement. Their model fractions in the selected refinements are 0.102423, 0.053770 and 0.063056. This gives your framework an actual shared neighbour geometry on which to compare different walls. Their pressure and temperature conditions differ. A matched-condition conductivity or clock conclusion would require more data. [chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/chem.201805298?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **HYPOTHESIS — Light as leader, and a vector creating space or volume.** Spectroscopy gives frequencies, polarizability gives a response to a field, and diffraction gives spacings. Together they supply three independently measurable observables. The causal proposal needs a directional prediction using one or two of them to predict the third on a held-out state. No spacetime metric enters the calculations here.
- **HYPOTHESIS — Breathing and heat.** A decreasing neighbour distance supports the geometrical part of the compression picture. Persistent heating depends on the compression path and heat transfer. The fixed-size vibration model can be tested against phase-resolved displacement correlations: it predicts a neighbour-motion pattern, which a sequence of static cell volumes alone cannot establish.
- **HYPOTHESIS — Russell’s opposed and parallel flows.** A discriminating test needs a definition of the flow, its source, orientation, and predicted sign of force or strain under reversal. Graphite supplies directional structure; oxygen supplies a magnetic control. Maxwell stress is calculable once the fields and boundaries are specified. I found no element-specific result authorizing the universal flow rule across these five.
- **RECOGNISED — Set aside with why.** An exhaustive list of *every* phase and verified nearest neighbour is unresolved, especially for metastable carbon, megabar nitrogen and metallic oxygen. The 105 source records include repeated refinements, orientational disorder and proposed models. Their automated minimum-site distance sometimes connects mutually exclusive sites. I have also left absolute dark-gas electrical conductivities unresolved without carrier density, field and purity, and left atomic knots, space creation and measured neighbouring-clock ratios open. These are identifiable missing measurements or definitions.
