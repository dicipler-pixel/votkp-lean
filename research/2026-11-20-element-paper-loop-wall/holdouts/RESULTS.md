Polonium was predicted outside. Three preliminary laser determinations instead put it inside. Its best quoted statistical result gives 1.4761101164 eV and z=5.7028739974. The separate scenario uses the preliminary result in its original units. This is one provisional contrary outcome, with a complete experimental uncertainty budget outstanding. The JyvIS result of 11905.631(9) cm⁻¹ would have to move down approximately 256.47 cm⁻¹ to reach it. The parenthesis on that experimental number covers statistics only. For these eight ratios, 5.8, 5.82, 5.83, 5.85, 5.9 and 6 all reproduce the wall labels, including the provisional polonium label. A cutoff of 5 changes iridium and polonium. Thus seven successful heavy predictions do not single out 3+2√2 among these alternatives.
