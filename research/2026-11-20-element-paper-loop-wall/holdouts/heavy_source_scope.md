# A measurable forward test for Fm, Md, No and Lr

**HYPOTHESIS / falsifier.** The last valence electron before an electronic shell opens is more weakly bound. The test here compares the **next removal from the same element** with the first removal. Between-element changes of IE₁ and isotope-dependent nuclear dynamics are different observables. On the forward branch, with w = 3+2√2, “inside” requires IE₂ < w IE₁; a direct determination entirely above that boundary would reject the predicted inside reading. The 2024 theory and both comparison bounds are already public; this is a prediction against a future two-sided threshold determination, not a blind test against 2024 theory. The atomic-physics inputs were checked on 21 November 2026.

## Which neutral anchors actually exist?

**MEASURED / DERIVED.** Sato et al. determined surface ionization efficiencies in the atom-at-a-time regime and corrected for partition functions. The centre values rise from Fm to No. The quoted one-error boxes for Fm→Md and Md→No differences are [−0.19,+0.33] and [−0.17,+0.22] eV. Each permits both signs. The No→Lr difference is [−1.76,−1.54] eV using the same experiment, so the sharp fall is resolved. A Rydberg-series study independently located the neutral No limit at 53,444.0(4) cm⁻¹ = 6.62621(5) eV. The two No methods use different observables and give consistent values. [Sato et al., 2018, Tables 1–2](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.8b09068) · [Chhetri et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.263003) · [Nagame and Sato review, 2024, Table 2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11105975/).

## What a second, one-atom experiment actually bounds

**MEASURED / experimentally inferred limit.** Kwarsick et al. observed the reduction of ²⁵⁵Lr²⁺ by O₂. The inferred energy floor combines a molecular first threshold of 12.063(1) eV with an estimated 1.2(3) eV Coulomb barrier. Their 2021 journal abstract reports a lower limit of 13.3(3) eV. Table 4 of the author manuscript places a greater-than sign on that value. Its discussion and conclusion explicitly adopt **IE₂ > 13.0 eV** as the conservative limit. The uncertainty belongs to the location of a lower bound; there is no measured two-sided 13.3(3) eV atomic IE₂. The authors describe a prospective upper-limit experiment separately. [J. Phys. Chem. A 125, 6818–6828 (2021), DOI](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jpca.1c01961) · [Author manuscript, Table 4, Discussion and Conclusions](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/8mp9k4g0).

**DERIVED.** At a nominal 13.3 eV lower threshold and central IE₁, the illustrative boundary coordinates are 13.3/4.96 = 2.68145 and Γ = 0.45674. With the authors’ conservative 13.0 eV and the largest quoted IE₁, z₁ > 13.0/5.01 = 2.59481 and Γ₁ > (13.0−5.01)/(13.0+5.01) = 0.44364. A one-sided lower bound below w **leaves the wall classification open**. The central threshold for the transition is 4.96w = 28.90900 eV; the published IE₁ box gives [28.67586,29.20042] eV. This is the discriminating upper-bound target for a strictly experimental classification of the first ordinary rung. An experimental upper bound for IE₂ below 28.67586 eV would settle the inside reading throughout that box.

**DERIVED / theory comparison.** The 2024 relativistic coupled-cluster study, in its *final* comparison table (Table V), gives IE₂ = 14.627(49) eV and EA = 0.446(11) eV. Using measured IE₁ gives z₁ = 2.94899, Γ₁ = 0.49354, inside. Its uncertainty endpoints with those of measured IE₁ yield z₁ = [2.90978,2.98293]. The archive's independent versioned reading, 14.54/4.96 = 2.93145, is also inside; the calculations can share physical approximations and are not independent experiments. The experimental gas-phase limit accommodates both theory estimates. [Guo et al., Physical Review A 110, 022817 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.110.022817).

**HYPOTHESIS / anion falsifier.** The same 2024 theoretical EA gives z₀ = 4.96/0.446 = 11.12108, Γ₀ = 0.83500, outside; the input boxes give z₀ = [10.76586,11.51724]. A future measured EA above 5.01/w = 0.85958 eV throughout its uncertainty box would explicitly falsify the outside call over the measured IE₁ interval. EA below 4.92/w = 0.84414 eV, if a bound anion is demonstrated, would establish outside. The theory-based anion and the charge-exchange lower bound occupy distinct evidence columns. The 2026 neutral-Lr spectral search covered about 1,500 cm⁻¹ without a confirmed transition in the scanned ranges; that finite null observation supplies neither limit here. [Coupled cluster calculation, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.110.022817) · [Range of neutral spectroscopy search, 2026, §6](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjd/s10053-026-01198-9).

**Mainstream-blend watch.** The electronic state sequence of Lr and a measurement of one valence removal carry no information by themselves about the locations of high-charge core-opening thresholds. These four single-atom measurements constrain four separate neutral starts. A country, facility or common technique cannot supply an absent second energy. Each future crossing should name both energy sources, their charge states and whether the result is an interval, theory value or threshold bound.
