## My other papers read at the wall

The wall did not arrive alone. Each of my earlier papers has its own objects, and when I write them in the variables of one peel step the same number appears. I separate three grades here: **EXACT** means the same formula by algebra; **STRUCTURAL** means the same shape on different objects; **LEAD** means untested or tested only in part.

### The Offset Belongs to the Boundary

For an eigenvalue $0<\nu<1$ of a specified restricted correlation operator, define $z = (1-\nu)/\nu$. Then
$$\Gamma = 1-2\nu,\qquad \nu(1-\nu) = \frac{1-\Gamma^2}{4},\qquad S = \frac{4}{1-\Gamma^2}.$$
Every entanglement level is a peel step, and the per-mode offset $\tau_\nu = \ln\big((1-\nu)/\nu\big)$ is the peel rapidity $\ln z$. The equal-face wall gives census $\nu(1-\nu) = 1/8$ — exactly half of the half-filling maximum $1/4$ — and sharpness $S = 8$; the per-mode offset there is $\ln(3+2\sqrt2) = 2\operatorname{arsinh}1$. The census window $|\tau|\le\ln(3+2\sqrt2)$ is the same statement as $\nu(1-\nu)\ge1/8$. **EXACT.**

On the archive, $S > 8$ gives the same call as $z > 3+2\sqrt2$ on every one of the 3,570 ordinary steps from hydrogen to astatine, and on all 44 bound anions from hydrogen to caesium, with no disagreement. At the anion rung $S_0 = (IE+EA)^2/(IE\cdot EA) = 4/(1-\Gamma_0^2)$, so $S_0 = 8$ is exactly electrophilicity equal to hardness; silver has $S_0 = 7.9801$ (inside) and silicon $8.0370$ (outside). **EXACT**, checked.

Putting two atomic threshold energies into $\nu = IE_k/(IE_k+IE_{k+1})$ is an exact numerical encoding. Identifying that number with the correlation eigenvalue of an independently prepared state is a further physical hypothesis. In the Rice–Mele comparison, positive hoppings $t_1$, $t_2$ and staggering $v$ give a squared band-edge ratio of one half exactly at $t_1^2+t_2^2+v^2 = 6t_1t_2$; at $v = 0$, $t_2/t_1 = 3\pm2\sqrt2$. The offset ladder spacing becomes $4\pi$ exactly at the wall, and the offset reaches $\ln(3+2\sqrt2)$ at $v = 2t/\sqrt3$. Finite-block calculations at interior sample points carry a different evidential status from an all-parameter proof. The v31 edition identifies the log-determinant ratio with the empty-region to full-region probability ratio for a number-conserving Gaussian state with $0<C_A<I$. Its large odd-block Rice–Mele result takes a fixed connected gapped model; a dimensional gravitational constant would need a calibration and a variational or selection principle.

### What Transport Keeps

Iterating a real determinant-one matrix with distinct real eigenvalues of different moduli selects the expanding projective direction from a generic starting line. A defective parabolic block selects algebraically; an elliptic block can rotate without selecting any real line. So the trace wall $\operatorname{tr}W = -2$ is exactly where line selection switches on in this family, subject to initial-vector and degeneracy conditions. In the specified corner frame the interior projector conditioning obeys $\kappa^2 = 1/(1-\Gamma_a^2-\Gamma_b^2)$, and Proposition 7's rank-one stress is $S = \Gamma_a^2+\Gamma_b^2$. The divergence near the wall depends on the metric used for norms and on the chosen frame: similarity preserves the eigenvalue classification while a non-unitary change of basis can alter a projector norm, which stops a conditioning plot from becoming a claimed intrinsic material observable. **EXACT** for the algebra.

The rail coordinate makes the wall and Catalan's constant one coordinate. Put $\Gamma = \tan(\psi/2)$. The wall $\Gamma^2 = \tfrac12$ sits at $\cos\psi = 1/3$, that is $\sec\psi = 3$. Catalan's rail root $17+12\sqrt2 = (3+2\sqrt2)^2$ has reflection $2\sqrt2/3 = \tanh(2h_*)$, where $h_* = \operatorname{arsinh}1$ is the wall half-step: the Catalan rail's rapidity is exactly twice the wall's. And
$$G = \int_0^1 \frac{2\operatorname{artanh}\Gamma}{1+\Gamma^2}\,d\Gamma = 0.9159655941772190\ldots,$$
equal to Catalan's constant at 45 digits — the peel half-step weighted by $d\psi/d\Gamma$. This is a direct change of variable of a classical integral; I claim no physical meaning for Catalan's constant from it. **EXACT.** Edition 3 also gives a normalised-Gram test for selection of a dominant right singular projector: tied growth can give a fixed line, a rotating line or an unresolved higher-dimensional block, and discrete plaquette compatibility of its transport and curvature of its eigenline refer to different connections and can coexist.

### The Offset Speed Limit — rate is weight

A differentiable path of projectors supplies a clock that a list of ionisations lacks. With $g = \tfrac12\|\dot P\|_F^2$ and, for $\dot P = -i[H,P]$ with $\hbar=1$, $g = \|PH(I-P)\|_F^2 = E_B$, the speed limit reads $\dot\tau^2\le 4gS$. Put $\theta = \arcsin\Gamma$, so that $\theta = \operatorname{gd}(h)$, the Gudermannian ($\sin\operatorname{gd}h = \tanh h$, $\sinh h = \tan\theta$). For one accessible mode the speed limit is exactly
$$\Big|\frac{d\theta}{dt}\Big| \le 2\sqrt g = 2\sqrt{E_B}.$$
In the same angle, the loop-closing test $\sinh h_a\sinh h_b<1$ is $\tan\theta_a\tan\theta_b<1$, that is $\theta_a+\theta_b<\pi/2$, and the equal-face wall is $\theta = \pi/4$. Then
$$\frac{d(\Gamma^2)}{dt} = \sin2\theta\,\frac{d\theta}{dt},\qquad \Big|\frac{d(\Gamma^2)}{dt}\Big|\le 2\sqrt g\,\sin2\theta\le2\sqrt g ,$$
and the outer bound can be reached only at $\theta = \pi/4$. **The wall is where the speed limit lets the returned power move fastest.** On the paper's saturating trajectory $1-2C = \sin2\Omega t$, $\theta = 2\Omega t$ exactly and the wall is crossed at $t = \pi/(8\Omega)$, the instant at which $\Gamma^2$ changes fastest. A check at $\Omega = 1.3$ finds the peak of $|d(\Gamma^2)/dt|$ at $2.600000 = 2\sqrt{E_B}$, at $\Gamma^2 = 0.50000$ and $t = 0.302076 = \pi/(8\Omega)$. Over 3,301 random one-mode idempotent paths the largest $(d\theta/dt)^2/(4g)$ is $0.999999995$: the bound holds and is not trivially met. This is the saturation point of a bound derived from the paper, not the equality case of its Theorem 1, which is met all along the trajectory. **EXACT** for the algebra; the discrete index of an electron removal supplies neither $\dot P$ nor elapsed time, so a speed test on atoms needs a preparation and a measured time variable. **LEAD.**

The junction sum rule of the ball work, $\sum_{j\ne i}\|P_{ij}\|^2 = \operatorname{Tr}(C_i-C_i^2)$, is the census identity $C - C^2 = P_{12}P_{12}^\dagger$ of this paper read on one site; the localisation second moment $\operatorname{Var}(X) = \operatorname{Tr}\big(PX(1-P)X\big)$ is the coupling energy $E_B$ with the position operator in place of $H$. **EXACT**, residuals below $10^{-13}$. Each removal energy is a difference of charge-state energies, so a common shift cancels in it and the ratio cancels a common scale: applying $E\mapsto sE+c$ to every ladder from hydrogen to astatine changes no $z$ or $\Gamma$ by more than $1.6\times10^{-14}$. The paper's own words name the missing step for the same kind of object: turning the offset, a pure number, into a weight needs a clock to supply the units. The ratio forgets the scale; the difference — a mass, a weight — keeps it. That is exactly what the holmium–dysprosium decay calculation later shows.

### The Weight Between

On one scalar edge carrying two thresholds, the matrix definitions of *The Weight Between* give the two books directly: $E^- = \tfrac18\|[H,H^\dagger]\|^2 = (d_i-d_j)^2$ and $E^+ = \tfrac18\|H^2-H^{\dagger2}\|^2 = (d_i+d_j)^2$, so $E^-/E^+ = \Gamma^2$ and $(E^+-E^-)/E^+ = 4\nu(1-\nu)$, the census. The wall is $E^+ = 2E^-$: the reflected share equals the transmitted share. On all 3,570 archive steps the identity holds to $2.8\times10^{-16}$, and a non-unitary edge block breaks it, as it must. The ordinary steps nearest to half reflected are caesium's first (0.50710), carbon's fourth (0.51480) and nitrogen's fifth (0.48829). Writing $D = \Gamma = \tanh h$ and $V = \operatorname{sech}h$, $V^2+D^2 = 1$ and the wall is $V = D$. Film reflections compose by $u\oplus v = (u+v)/(1+uv)$, so two successive peel steps compose the same way: $\Gamma(IE_{k+2}/IE_k) = \Gamma_k\oplus\Gamma_{k+1}$, checked to $2.2\times10^{-16}$ on 3,486 archive pairs. "Equal books" in the sense of this paper means $\Gamma^2 = 1$, not the wall; equal books on a graph are not the wall. **EXACT.**

### The Ledger Outlives the Metric

In the gravity ledger the local positivity calculation uses a Hermitian form with diagonal entries $a$, $c$ and upper off-diagonal $b + i\omega/2$. Positivity needs $a,c\ge0$ and $4(ac-b^2)\ge\omega^2$. In the normalisation $a = c = 1$, $b = \Gamma_a$, $\omega = 2\Gamma_b$, its boundary is again $\Gamma_a^2+\Gamma_b^2 = 1$: the two-face rule is the rank-one licence of that paper. This is a reversible translation between the clock disc and a positivity domain for that particular form. It does not specify a mass distribution, a rotation curve, a physical spacetime metric or a source for gravitation; a gravitational reading would need predictions in its own measured variables and a map into this disc fixed before fitting them. Edition 7 defines its intrinsic real trace form explicitly as $g_{ij} = \tfrac12\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}(\partial_iP\,\partial_jP)$. A rank-one Riesz projector can cross a positivity wall while its contour stays gapped and the selected trace log varies smoothly. The paper's spectral-screen grid comparison has a largest sampled negative residual of about $-0.07954$ dex against a comparison curve; it is not a fit to individual galaxy points. A source Hessian gives a susceptibility; a force law also needs an energy, a physical Green function and a source calibration.

At the anion rung the same ledger gives a metric determinant $\det g = IE\times EA$, and the wall is a sign change of a two-plane Gram determinant. Reversing an $n$-step path multiplies its signed volume by $(-1)^{n(n+1)/2}$. **EXACT** for the algebra.

### Matter at a Scale

Difference observables are invariant under $K\mapsto K+cI$ and projectors are invariant under $K\mapsto cK$. The peel reflection is a ratio of differences of charge-state energies, so it is invariant under both: $\Gamma$ does not change when every ionisation energy is shifted or scaled together. That is why one threshold can be laid across all the elements, and why an absolute anchor — a mass, a clock — is needed the moment a prediction is stated in units. **EXACT.** The scale paper's anomaly coefficient $A = 2c/\pi^2$ (per two endpoints) belongs to the logarithm between zero and span; whether the wall's logarithm $\ln(3+2\sqrt2)$ has a place in that census is **LEAD**.
