## The question that connects the papers

My work began with questions about Walter Russell, Tesla and the periodic table, and my founding aim has always been to map and measure everything simply, the way light does. This paper brings that aim back to the periodic table. Peel an atom one electron at a time, record each step as a ratio of successive removal energies, and ask what the record of each peel keeps.

My starting point is an act of observation. Something passes a boundary. An observer keeps a record on one side and asks whether the next reading can be predicted from it. In Dirac Time, imposing a boundary condition changes which modes are admissible. In the element ledger, removing an electron changes which threshold comes next. In the ball work, energy can cross a junction while the clocks and walls on either side keep their identities. These operations share one question, and each has its own domain, units, admissible inputs and test.

**HYPOTHESIS.** "The vector determines the direction, that may be decay." Whether the vector form has anything to do with space and volume, with light as the leader, I keep as an open question, not a claim. I take these statements literally enough to ask what has to be measured for each verb. An order on a sequence gives a direction of reading. A physical decay direction also needs specified parent and daughter states and an energy balance. A spatial volume needs enough independent directions and a definition of volume. A leading light field needs a controlled change in the field before a reproducible response. Each added assertion therefore has its own way to fail.

The answer this paper gives for the elements is a single number. A peel step with ratio $z$ has reflection $\Gamma = (z-1)/(z+1)$, and the step lies outside my loop wall when $\Gamma^2>\tfrac12$, that is $z>3+2\sqrt2 = 5.828427$. The wall is not fitted to the elements; it is where two equal faces at a right angle stop closing a loop. Run through every element from hydrogen to astatine, it sorts 3,570 ordinary steps so that only eight cross, and every one of the eight is the first opening of a closed noble-gas core.

**Evidence key.** MEASURED marks an experimental observation or experimentally derived source value, with its source; catalogued energies also carry their source grades, and theoretical and semiempirical entries keep those labels. DERIVED marks an implication of stated premises or the output of a stated computation; certificates about encoded numbers belong here. RECOGNISED marks an observed pattern, fitted relation or comparison still without an independent derivation. HYPOTHESIS marks a proposed physical meaning, mechanism or extrapolation. OPEN marks a question with its test named and not yet run. A test whose kill condition fired is marked as having *failed this way*: it failed in that setup, and the idea stays open for other setups. "Reported" means the cited work supplied the result; "rerun" means a computation done for this edition.

**Scope.** The two meanings of VOTKP in this paper are the Volume of the Knot Potential and the Vector of Traits Kept under Peeling. Calling them "volume" and "vector" names the constructions; it does not date the papers. The core sources are my own long forms, marked editions, notebooks and committed runs, and no substitute reconstruction of VOTKP supplies the definitions here. My numerics are computations on my own laptop, checked against other people's published measurements.
