## What Dirac Time contributes to the element paper

I began with a question about memory. Suppose two preparations have the same description now. Can anything I am allowed to do next tell them apart? My long form uses this question to separate a record, a way to read it, and a physical claim about its carrier. It gives the element programme a way to decide when keeping one more trait — a sign, an interior branch, a second probe — has actually gained information. It also keeps my original motivation: "No core ever. Memory first." In the atomic tables, *core* names a specified electron configuration reached by successive removals. That is a different operational meaning from the philosophical phrase in the long form, and I keep the two apart.

The primary texts are the complete 101-page *The Dirac Time of the Gigantefermion*, long form v3.0.1 (25 September 2026, on Zenodo 26 September), and the 6 November v3.1 package. The later edition sharpens the chiral hypotheses and adds the Smith coordinate in §17.1. Citations below use v3.0.1 unless v3.1 is named, and the original section and theorem identifiers let a reader recover every hypothesis and figure in the originals.

### Memory before clock, §§0–4

**§0, Research lineage.** One chiral object, two phase rails, one flow, hierarchical memory. These are four different commitments. The object is conjectural; the rails need calibrated readouts; a common flow needs a rank condition and compatible generators; and the hierarchy consists of specified record maps. Earlier calculations displaced the proposed generic codimension-one zero walls of the flat torus and the identification of full signed spectral flow with the universal clock. I keep the questions and the failed tests.

**§1, Memory Before Clock.** Write down what is observed, the future operations admitted, and which outcomes distinguish two preparations. An invariant can distinguish objects at one instant. A history records an ordered path. A hidden present distinction earns the name *predictive memory* only when it can affect an admitted future observation. For elemental work, identical first and final thresholds give two common observations. Distinct intermediate ladders are distinguishable records if the corresponding intermediate ions can be prepared and interrogated. A claim of material memory would further need histories prepared at matched endpoints and an observed later difference.

**§2, The Exact Predictive Quotient.** In a finite-dimensional linear state space of dimension $d$, with one fixed evolution $L$ and observation $R$, set
$$\mathcal O_d v = \big(Rv,\ RLv,\ \dots,\ RL^{d-1}v\big), \qquad N_\infty = \bigcap_{n\ge 0}\ker\!\big(RL^{n}\big) = \ker \mathcal O_d .$$
Cayley–Hamilton gives finite termination, and $L$ preserves $N_\infty$. The quotient has dimension $\operatorname{rank}\mathcal O_d$, and every sufficient linear summary must have at least this rank. The present record alone closes exactly when $L\ker R\subseteq\ker R$, equivalently when $RL = AR$ for a descended evolution on the record space. Peel ladders with changing ionic charge and preparation protocols have not yet been given that fixed linear model; the theorem names the evidence needed for the stronger claim of minimal memory.

**§3, What Transport Keeps.** Successive declared reductions produce nested kernels for their compositions. Survival under transport, detectability by a particular probe, and effect on a later response answer separate questions. A finite list of traits can survive a reduction even when the chosen readout cancels.

**§4, Why Dirac.** The spinor (Dirac) operator and its spectral projectors give concrete meanings to orientation and transport. My proposed elementary knot-like electron adds a problem of field realisation, stability and sources.

### The operator, the probe and the clock, §§5–9

**§5, Chiral Negative Control and Projector Orientation.** In a flat-torus block write $a = m+\alpha$, $b = n+\beta$ and $\phi = \arg(a+ib)$. The eigenvalues of $2\pi(a\sigma_1 + b\sigma_2)$ are $\pm 2\pi\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$. The positive projector away from zero is $(I + \cos\phi\,\sigma_1 + \sin\phi\,\sigma_2)/2$. A circle about the degeneracy holds the two eigenvalues fixed and gives spinor holonomy $-1$. For the fixed-domain self-adjoint Fredholm family with fixed chiral involution, the long form states when full spectral flow, eta and odd traces cancel. Projector probes can still read direction. At a zero mode one must use $P_- = I - P_+ - P_0$. The sign change of the elemental reflection under exchange of two energies is an elementary algebraic analogue. Physical mirroring, changing a spinor branch and reversing a peel order each need their own involution.

**§6, Intrinsic Quantum Geometric Tensor.** On a smooth patch of fixed-rank orthogonal projectors, a tangent $V = V^\dagger$ has only cross-block parts in the $P$, $I-P$ split. I form
$$Q_P(V,W) = \operatorname{Tr}\!\big(P V^\dagger W\big), \qquad g_P(V,W) = \tfrac12 \operatorname{Tr}(VW).$$
Positivity, curvature control and unitary covariance are proved on this patch. A spectral gap enters a projector derivative through each coupled pair across an isolated cluster, with weights proportional to $|\dot H_{mn}|^2/(E_n-E_m)^2$. This tensor lives on the projector bundle and is intrinsic. It is not a spacetime metric, and I do not substitute one for it anywhere in this paper. Ionisation energies compare binding thresholds across particle-number sectors. Importing the projector formula for those scalar differences needs the operators, couplings and joint state space to be specified. My broader non-normal framework can keep a contour ledger across the failure of a local tangent description.

**§7, Gauge Phase, Aharonov, and Conditional Measurement.** Wilson holonomy compares paths; a geometric phase needs a cyclic state history; ABL probabilities and weak values need a preparation, an instrument and a post-selection. **§8, Spectral History Ledgers.** An additive, reversal-antisymmetric path ledger descends to an endpoint potential on each connected component precisely when it vanishes on every admitted closed loop. **§9, One Flow, Many Readouts, and Common Time.** Lifted linear phase constraints of rank $n-1$ leave one direction. For $x = A+Bs$, $y = C+Ds$, the angular rate is $(AD-BC)/(x^2+y^2)$ where defined, and the numerator can vanish on a progressing path. In a declared moving frame $R$, two generator descriptions admit one clock when
$$R^\dagger H R - i\hbar R^\dagger \dot R = \dot\tau H_0 + aI,\qquad \dot\tau>0,$$
with the allowed frame class fixed. Rescaling a Hamiltonian scales gaps and leaves its projectors fixed. For an admitted $t$ and $H(t)$, $g_{tt} = \operatorname{Tr}\big(PH(I-P)HP\big)/\hbar^2$. For a pure state $F_Q = 4g_{tt}$, and a specified instrument has $F_M \le F_Q$. A dimensionless face clock needs a comparison protocol and a calibration before it gives a duration.

### What can actually carry the record, §§14–18

**§14, Connection Energy and Non-Normal Transport.** Take a Hermitian block-diagonal $D$, an anti-Hermitian edge operator $A$ with unitary edge blocks, and $H = D + A$. Then $[H,H^\dagger] = -2[D,A]$ and
$$\tfrac18\,\big\|[H,H^\dagger]\big\|_F^2 = \sum_{\{i,j\}\in E} \big\| D_i - A_{ij} D_j A_{ij}^\dagger \big\|_F^2 .$$
This is my connection energy: the non-normality of the whole operator, counted edge by edge as the disagreement that remains after transport. Vanishing means edge-by-edge agreement after transport. It is an exact comparison for the declared graph operator. A real-material junction model would supply the local blocks and transport on each edge before the comparison is made. Its valence-shell projector link $\|P_{ij}\|^2$ has a different definition.

**§15, The Electron and the Maxwell–Dirac Admission Gate.** The Loss–Yau magnetic zero mode gives linked field lines and $B = 12\,jW$ in the chosen normalisation. Its computed field curl fails the required Weyl-source equation: the spin and convective parts cancel in the static Pauli zero-mode current. Within the smooth, square-integrable, static class with finite field energy, the seed cannot close the proposed magnetic source ledger. A stationary model with electric potential, finite energy, linked lines and balanced total angular momentum would answer the open question. My pictures of electron balls in the sea and ordinary particle bookkeeping coexist without my having derived an electron solution yet.

**§16, The Gigantefermion Hypothesis.** A collective or composite chiral carrier is my hypothesis, subject to statistics, a generator, stability and resources. For the exact two-level control $H = \mathbf d\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$, let $\rho = |\mathbf d| > 0$, $\Delta = 2\rho$, $\chi = d_z/\rho$. Then $|\dot{\mathbf d}|^2 = \dot\rho^2 + \Delta^2 g$ and $\rho^2\dot\chi^2 \le \Delta^2 g$: a change in directional asymmetry costs projector motion. A specified Hamiltonian still stands between an elemental threshold and this motion.

**§17, Geometry, Gravity, Horizons, and Hair.** A contour or representation record can survive the failure of a metric chart. In my complex Smith coordinate (added in v3.1 §17.1) $\Gamma = (z-1)/(z+1)$, $|\Gamma|<1$ exactly in the right half-plane. The long form gives a horizon with a positive projector metric, and a clock gradient with zero curvature. The gravitation and boundary-compression extensions keep their own admission gates.

**§18, Dirac Time defined.** The 6 November v3.1 proposes, as a framework, $\mathfrak T = (O, F, \Theta, M, S)$: $O$ orders admitted histories; $F$ measures distinguishability per admitted parameter on a declared state, patch or instrument; $\Theta$ records outcomes under stated interventions; $M$ gives predictive equivalence relative to the reduction and future protocols; $S$ requires compatibility established by named clock-comparison operations. ### Controls and open propositions, §§19–26

**§19** keeps eight counterexamples in view. Full graded scalar spectral flow vanishes. $\operatorname{Tr}\big((2P-I)\dot P\big) = 0$ at fixed rank. Instantaneous coupling loses delayed response. **§21** lists ten technical tasks: predictive quotient, projector tangents, spectral-flow descent, common generator clock, finite-meter history, derived friction, trefoil and $T(3,4)$ exterior APS family, light inheritance, Maxwell–Dirac construction or obstruction, and an arrow with resources. **§24, The Great Winding, Memory Transfer, and CCC,** places the proposed cross-era transfer and the network of coherent structures in an outer layer that needs a defined carrier, transfer map and observable.
