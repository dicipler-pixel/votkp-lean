# SCRIPT: HEAT-SEA-01 — fixed before the run
Question (from the pocket work): each ball is a cut in the sea, occupied (its lower level filled) and surrounded by the sea on its links (the normal layer). Does heating that layer seal the ball's pocket or open it?
Heat = the squeeze of all clock rates, amplitude a in {0, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0} at the in-time frequency 2ω, tied way, sealed ends, 3 balls at z = 3,5,7, push off and on, t = 20.
Read: pocket leak of each ball = ‖C_i(t) − C_i,rest‖ at the end and its maximum over the run; the weight between A and the far ball.
Marks: SEALED if the far ball's maximum leak falls as a rises from 0.1 to 1.0 (the Zeno reading, leak ∝ 1/σ); OPENED if it rises; NEITHER otherwise. The push-on and push-off readings are reported separately; a result that differs between them is reported as such, not merged.
Nothing is in the run that is not in SEA-RUNS-01 except the amplitude.
