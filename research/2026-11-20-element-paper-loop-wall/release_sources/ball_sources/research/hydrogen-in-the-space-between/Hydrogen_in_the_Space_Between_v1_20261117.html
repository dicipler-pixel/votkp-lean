What is drawn

Each atom is the Light at the Hypersurface ball, its own construction. The small spheres are the field sites of the space between, every one a two-component state in H, sized by its occupation: the electrons that fill the gaps. A kick turns the electron on atom A. The sites then light up by how far their state has turned from the ground sea, ‖Ci − Ci,ground‖ on a log scale from 10⁻⁸ to 10⁻¹: that wave is the electron’s turn travelling. The occupation of every site stays one electron (the occupation current 2 Im Tr(HijPji) is zero to 10⁻¹⁵): a turn carries no charge, it carries energy. Light: the energy current from ρE = (HP + PH)/2, gold with it, blue against. Connection energy: ‖Di − UijDjUij†‖² of each link, the mismatch at the wall. Weight between: ‖Pij‖² across each link. An X on a link of the row between the atoms means its weight between is below a third of the largest possible (¼): that gap does not connect (the third is Bruggeman’s threshold from The Space Between, used here as the reading rule).

The model, in one piece

One operator does everything, H = D + A from MM-MATH-V1. The atom’s electron is a two-level site with field 2.4 × the vacuum’s (model units), resolved into a centre and six surface ports at the ball’s own peel endpoints; the space between is a lattice of vacuum sites with field 0.8σz; on the surface links the peel step gives Γ = tanh(δ/2) and c = c₀ sech(δ/2) with δ = ln z for z = 3, 5, 7 on the three axes (VOTKP). The sea is the occupied ground projector at half filling, cut in the gap: the Dirac sea. The run is exact, P(t) = e−iHt P eiHt, no damping. Hydrogen: both atoms carry the same field; opposed turns the second atom’s field against the first’s, parallel along it. The travel time between the atoms is not put in: it comes from the sites between. Limits, stated

The lattice is finite and open at its edges. The vacuum field, the atom’s level splitting and c₀ = 0.22 are model units, not hydrogen’s electron-volts. The occupied cut is at half filling, so the ground energy against separation is not a bond curve (more sites, lower energy) and none is claimed. The atom’s ball is drawn by its own clock; its faces are not yet fed by the sea’s currents.
