The elemental ladders leave an interval in which every cutoff classifies the archived steps alike. Two settings now sit inside that interval: a specified optical return and a resolved oxygen channel. They offer different ways to ask what the geometry predicts.

The gap in the elemental table

Beasley’s original Volume of the Knot Potential uses knot geometry. His Vector of Traits Kept under Peeling records what survives an ordered removal. Here the supplied quantity is an energy ratio. Assigning a knot or a volume to a chemical element requires another observation map.

For consecutive positive removal energies let z be their ratio and let Γ = (z − 1)/(z + 1). Reversing their order changes the sign of Γ while preserving Γ². On a forward equal-face step, Γ² = ½ gives the exact geometric wall w = 3 + 2√2. Comparing a tabulated ratio with w is already fully defined, including the equality case.

The hydrogen-to-astatine ledger contains 3,570 ordinary steps and 67 adopted anion steps. Eight ordinary and 55 anion steps lie outside. The largest inside ratio belongs to the silver anion and the smallest outside ratio to the silicon anion. Every cutoff from 5.80790206… inclusive to 5.86653878… exclusive gives exactly the same 3,637 binary classifications. Twenty-three steps lack a complete input-width box.

A finite collection of mixed historical inputs also leaves freedom in the law that combines them. A strictly increasing piecewise map can fix every sampled reflection power, preserve every historical paired decision and move the diagonal cutoff within the gap. A new setting and an independently observed outcome have different experimental roles.

Holdout classification of heavy-anion steps

A separate rule predicts anion side from the ordinary wall call and the ordinal position of the strongest named opening. Training used numeric H–Xe affinities. On the extended Cs–At test the rule called every case outside: 20/20 outside, 0/4 inside, balanced accuracy 0.50. Ir, Pt, Au and At were missed. The earlier dataset gave 18/18 and 0/4. This is a different test from the earlier eight individually named anion forecasts, seven of which agreed with adopted data (Po is provisionally contrary).

The strongest opening still gives a useful address in the table. It occurs deeper than the first eligible core for 70 of 83 elements. In the extended ledger, adding this ordinal to the anion/ordinary address reduces the number of indistinguishable element pairs from 1,304 to 366.

A specific bridge from Dirac Time

The pump-and-probe construction in §§11.1–11.4 gives two histories with the same one-qubit marginals and different joint phases. In the model’s single-excitation subspace, two calibrated probes recover that phase difference at common visibility v. With respective expectation differences ΔZ₁ and ΔZ₂, the trace distance is

D = v |sin[(φ₁ − φ₂)/2]| = ½√[(ΔZ₁)² + (ΔZ₂)²].

Complete dephasing removes both signals. For oxygen, a quantitative proposition still needs a specific joint state, retained partner, preparation and a map from z to φ or another independent response. A comparison at matched excess energy, with an erased-record control, would then distinguish retained path information from ordinary kinematics. No such oxygen response has been scored.

What to measure

A proposed passive optical realization uses four calibrated polarisation-dependent filters. Each amplitude ratio z requires principal-axis intensity contrast z². Calibrate the settings independently, including attenuation and relative axes. For the full return, reconstruct the Stokes/Mueller matrix M directly from prepared input polarisations and detected outputs.

For a coherent real Jones return K, the measured combination L = MII + MQQ + MUU − 3MVV equals (tr K)² − 4 det K. Geometry gives L = +0.000032998589 in incident-intensity units at 35/6, exactly zero at (9,4) and −0.01351833 at (4,4). With four component errors each bounded by ε, the error in L is at most 6ε. The ideal 35/6 signal therefore requires ε < 5.50 × 10−6 before extra loss. Its intensity-sign test alone cannot recover a phase-referenced trace sign.

Freeze the input acceptance box, response statistic, controls and unresolved criterion before measuring the return. An interval containing zero is unresolved. A resolved contrary sign challenges the calibrated optical realization. A match would test the optical geometry under those conditions. The physical atomic interpretation needs its own observable.

A microscopic meaning for the face apex

For a sudden, same-mass, nonrelativistic Coulomb quench between two hydrogenic 1s states with inverse lengths a and b, the binding-energy ratio is z = (b/a)². Direct orbital integration gives an overlap of 8(ab)3/2/(a + b)³. Thus the face apex x = tanh(ln z/4) gives the exact ground-state projection probability F = (1 − x²)³. At the geometric wall F = 8(5√2 − 7) ≈ 0.568543. At charge doubling, z = 4 gives 1s fidelity 512/729. At this same ratio, the 2s → 2s and 2p → 2p fidelities are 2048/6561 ≈ 0.31215 and 32768/59049 ≈ 0.55493. Even in the Coulomb problem the orbital label changes which side of one-half the fidelity occupies.

Russell · a bounded historical test

What the evidence currently supports

What Russell Saw supplied the octave comparison. Russell, Corrected asked which observations preserve their reading across a specified reflection. In the peel table, exchanging the order of two energies reverses Γ and preserves Γ². This is a defined operation. A spatial mirror and a pump reversal need their own observation maps. The element-focused audit covers 132 of 585 original claim and law positions. Li/F, Be/O and B/N agree in conventional valence magnitude under the short-period pairing, yet each pair straddles the helium-core opening wall.

U240 · the three printed iron columns

On printed page 204 of The Universal One, each column contains 23 wavelengths. An unshifted ±0.20 Å air-wavelength search against the selected neutral-iron list gives 21/23, 20/23 and 19/23 in the iron, recent and remote columns. Across 92 translated control lists per column, maximum hit counts were 12, 12 and 10. A separate extraction of Nave et al.’s laboratory line table reproduces those three counts. The same rule on ionised iron gives 2, 4 and 2. A 1910 comparison prints at least one neutral-iron line in each column that rounds to Russell’s tenth of an ångström on the old Rowland scale. The source scale and intensity selection matter. These counts locate ordinary iron-line coincidences in all three columns. They do not identify every line uniquely, establish atomic ancestry, or score Russell’s complete historical record. Formal audit of this scoped result is pending. Primary U240 comparison.

Russell’s 1926 plate places vacancies at the later Tc, Pm, At and Fr positions. Bohr’s 1922 plate already displays those vacancies. Russell also leaves surplus places. The dates credit an early correct placement where supported and leave historical priority as a separate claim.
