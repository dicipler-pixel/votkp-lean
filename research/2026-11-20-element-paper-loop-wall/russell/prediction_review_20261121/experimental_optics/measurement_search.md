# Does published optical data resolve the four-filter wall?

I found no independently measured return for the specified four-filter commutator near the proposed wall in a targeted search of linear-polarizer Lorentz rotations, weak-measurement polarization sequences and measured non-Hermitian Jones matrices. The following records identify what can be checked experimentally and which measurement is still missing.

| Primary experiment | Controlled input and measured output | Scope for this bench |
|---|---|---|
| Kitano and Yabuzaki (1989) | Linearly polarized light traversed an array of linear polarizers. The authors measured rotation of the polarization plane. | Closest physical arrangement I located. The accessible publisher abstract establishes rotation. It gives neither separate stage contrasts nor enough return measurements to evaluate the discriminant. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/0375-9601(89)90373-3) |
| *Topological transitions of the generalized Pancharatnam-Berry phase* (2023), Fig. 3B | Three wave-plate/beam-displacer stages followed by a vertical polarization projection. Three public files at input beam waists 0.6, 1.2 and 1.7 mm each contain 20 reference-phase settings × 40 wave-plate angles of measured interference intensity. The experiment placed its geometric-phase transition near 1.2 mm. The ideal calculation gave 0.85 mm. | Closest accessible raw sequential-filtering data I located. At each setting the interferometer probes one input/output polarization projection. Different wave-plate angles change the process being tested. The data cannot determine the trace and determinant of any one complete return. [Paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10672158/) · [Raw measurements and README](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.41ns1rnmg) |
| *Observation of an exceptional point in a non-Hermitian metasurface* (2020), §2.3 | THz time-domain measurements with two input and two analyzer polarizations recover all four complex field transmissions, normalized to a bare substrate. | Establishes how to measure the full output matrix. The sample is a metasurface characterized by frequency and geometry. There are no two separately calibrated filter ratios or inverse-partner stages defining the proposed word. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1515/nanoph-2019-0489) |

A scoreable run requires measured principal intensity transmissions for the two filters and their axis-swapped partners, followed by independent polarization-resolved measurements of the assembled return at the same settings. For real coherent Jones operation, the four measured Mueller diagonals give L = M_II + M_QQ + M_UU − 3 M_VV. An uncertainty interval excluding zero determines its sign. Independent characterization must bound retardance, depolarization, and background at the precision of that sign decision. None of the located records supplies that paired input/output observation, so there is no experimental score for the Be/Rb or 35/6 settings here.
