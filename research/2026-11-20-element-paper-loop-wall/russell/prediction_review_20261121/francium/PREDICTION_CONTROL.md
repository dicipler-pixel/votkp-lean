# Independent electronic-structure controls
Freeze: use the available PySCF 2.11.0 Stuttgart energy-consistent spin–orbit pseudopotentials (Lim et al. 2005), 46 core electrons for Cs, 78 for Fr, and 28 for Rb. Compute ΔSCF for the singly charged ground state against the optimized one-hole dication, with the core spin–orbit term active and separately disabled, in common, even-tempered uncontracted basis sets. Ground-state electronic energies use the same basis on each side. Match the Rb and Cs controls to their published second ionization energies before interpreting Fr. Numerical basis convergence and agreement on lighter controls do not supply a rigorous bound for the Fr answer. The known Groningen 2016 X2C and nonrelativistic CCSD(T) results and the 2018 de Farias values were already seen. This is an independent implementation, not a blind physical test. No exponential basis optimizer will be fitted to experimental Fr data.

Predeclared extension after first HF scan, before correlated runs: also use spin-orbital MP2 energy differences on the same converged determinants, first for Rb and Cs controls and then for Fr. This perturbative correction uses the full available virtual space and frozen pseudopotential core. Check the occupied/virtual separation in each determinant, minimum two-electron denominator, the leading p-hole multiplet, and angular/radial basis changes. Compare with the older all-electron X2C CCSD(T) point. MP2, HF calibration residuals, and agreement with other calculations remain model evidence, not an experimental interval.

Second extension, after the initial MP2 runs: check the explicit spin-orbital Coulomb transform against PySCF's scalar restricted MP2 reference, and, if feasible, use closed-cation ionization-potential equation-of-motion coupled cluster with singles and doubles (IP-EOM-CCSD). Limit the virtual space using the full-space second-order virtual density; keep complete near-degenerate occupation multiplets and semicanonicalize each selected space. Increase the virtual-space budget and compare Rb, Cs, Fr at the same settings. Predeclare caps of 24, 36, 48 and (resources permitting) 60 virtual spinors. A truncated-basis EOM value is a convergence check, with no extrapolation or fitted correction. A different scalar ΔCCSD(T) composite is unnecessary if this works. The FNO/IP-EOM calculations do not model nuclear recoil or QED explicitly.

After checking Lim et al. §II B, both cationic sectors were included in the pseudopotential fit to calculated all-electron Dirac–Hartree–Fock energies. For an independent treatment of the core and the direction, also run Quantum ESPRESSO 6.7 `ld1.x` in the radial all-electron mode. Use the same neutral, monocation, ground dication and (only with spin–orbit active) the excited p₁⁄₂-hole dication for Rb, Cs and Fr. Compare nonrelativistic Schrödinger, scalar relativistic and full Dirac modes, without fitting any parameters here. Use spherical PBE as the primary functional, Perdew–Zunger LDA as sensitivity, radial log-grid spacing 0.00625 and 0.0125, and the code's point-nuclear Coulomb potential. Record convergence and compare to known neutral/ionized Rb and Cs thresholds and ionic fine structure. DFAs and spherical averaged open shells have model error much larger than numerical grid convergence. Score the hour's physical forecast only from a future independent ground-channel measurement.

Isoelectronic cross-check after the group-1 runs: use the same radial PBE and PZ calculations for closed 86-electron ions from Rn through U and both members of the residual 85-electron p-hole multiplet. Compare the ground threshold with the archive grades, plus the assigned Rn II ground fine-structure interval (30,895.1 cm⁻¹). Include the same residual-ion assignment check as the group-1 runs. This can test the curvature assumption of a local three-point interpolation within a model with consistent inputs. The Ra and Ac reference values retain their theory and semiempirical grades.

Core-polarization sensitivity after the first EOM roots and radial runs: Lim et al. eq. 10 and Table IV give fixed Cs and Fr parameters. Include their full dipolar core polarization potential in the spin–orbit ECP calculation without retuning, first as a variational ΔHF / ΔMP2 sensitivity. Test one- and two-electron CPP quadrature against direct expectation and against radial quadrature refinement. Compare Cs to the measured benchmark; no Rb CPP parameters were given. Occupied core polarization, basis changes and correlation truncation need separate labels.
