# U240, iron column test protocol v1

Freeze this specification before computing the matches. The unit is an ångström and the statistical unit is a printed wavelength. Use all 23 rows of each of the three columns on printed page 204, confirmed against the scan. Keep the three illustrative values in the preceding paragraph separate. Match air wavelengths to the nearest neutral-iron line in the pinned NIST-derived intensity-bearing list, restricted to the surrounding 3,400–8,100 Å window. Primary unshifted tolerance ±0.20 Å, with ±0.05, 0.10, 0.30 and 0.50 Å sensitivities. Count each printed value once per tolerance. Attach stars as the author's intensity annotations without assuming that they correspond to a calibrated source.

Null control: translate the full wavelength list by each integer offset from −50 through −5 and from +5 through +50 Å. This preserves clustering and approximates local line density while omitting the immediate neighborhood being tested. For each column, compare its hit count to the 92 translated counts; quote their median, range and fraction at least as large as the original (with the real unshifted case added to numerator and denominator). These offsets are diagnostic controls, not independent samples or a calibrated population p-value. Repeat the same primary test against Fe II, Ni I and Ti I. Compare against the NIST Handbook strong Fe I list separately. Do not count an absent match in the intensity-selected list as evidence that no iron line exists. Do not promote nearest-neighbour agreement into unique identification or inference about atomic ancestry.

A historical wavelength-scale sensitivity subtracts 0.16 or 0.19 Å from every printed value before matching, independently prescribed in early comparison literature. The source scale of Russell's printed columns is unknown. No estimated per-column shifts, intensity threshold or tolerance chosen after inspecting the fit will count as confirmation.

The hypothesis that only the first column contains ordinary neutral-iron lines loses support if both historical columns also have excess Fe I matches in this test and specific independently compiled strong-line or transition matches can be identified. A match for every entry would require individual corroboration beyond a wavelength neighborhood. If the control does as well, or neither historical column has independent strong-line evidence, report that limitation explicitly.
