# Exact exclusion of half-survival at the peel wall

For the ideal same-orbital, same-mass one-electron Coulomb quench, the all-orbital result gives F(x)=(1−x)^(2ℓ+3)[P_k^(0,2ℓ+1)(1−2x)]², where x is the square of the face apex and k=n−ℓ−1. This is a polynomial in x with rational coefficients. At z=3+2√2, x=3−2√2 lies in (0,1). Its algebraic conjugate x′=3+2√2 lies above 1. The zeros of the Jacobi factor in the x variable are all strictly between zero and one for these parameters. At x′ the polynomial value F(x′) is strictly negative because 1−x′<0 and 2ℓ+3 is odd. If F(x) vanished then its conjugate would vanish as well. Thus F(x)>0. Its algebraic conjugate has the opposite sign, which excludes rational F(x). In particular **no same-orbital fidelity in this entire Coulomb family equals exactly one half at the elemental wall**. This is a consequence of the explicit analytic model and the Jacobi zero theorem, not a Lean-certified assertion. It applies to a selected orbital's survival. Other observables of the same apparatus can satisfy other equalities.
