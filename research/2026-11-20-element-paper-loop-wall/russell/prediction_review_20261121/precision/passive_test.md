# A bench observable and its intensity budget
Jeromie Beasley's two-face comparison, research hour, 21 November 2026. Proposed instrument; all numbers below are computed. The optical realization encodes the two input ratios by design. It tests the proposed geometry with independently measured output. It supplies no measurement of a joint Be–Rb atomic transition.

## Which dynamics can carry the signal?
On a closed two-state Hilbert space the commutator of two unitary evolutions is unitary with determinant one. Its eigenvalues are exp(±iθ), so its trace is real and belongs to [−2,2]. A real trace of −2.006465 requires a different operation. Postselected filtering offers one. A real linear optical diattenuator with principal intensity transmissions T_max,T_min realizes a normalized amplitude contrast z=√(T_max/T_min). Calibrate in *intensities* using z²; the face input is z. For each ratio orient one filter with unit major-axis amplitude and minor-axis amplitude 1/z; swapping the axes realizes its projective inverse, with attenuation. The elementary Jones form is documented by [NIST](https://pages.nist.gov/pySCATMECH/mueller4.html) and [Optics Letters](https://opg.optica.org/viewmedia.cfm?seq=0&uri=ol-29-20-2402).

At axes 0 and 45 degrees let A_n,B_n be the single-filter matrices and place opposite-axis partners in the commutator order (rightmost factor acts first). The raw field return K=W/(ab) when each major-axis transmission is one; additional common loss scales K. All four stages are passive. Eigenvalue magnitudes of the normalized W may exceed one while every raw transmission probability stays at most one. For 35/6 equal faces the unpolarized throughput is 2.86155%, the best-polarized throughput 5.72179%, the least-transmitted polarization 0.00130357% in that ideal model. Be/Rb gives respectively 2.78400%, 5.56687%, 0.00113021%. Phase sign, throughput and projective spectral type are distinct observations.

## A scalar obtainable directly from intensities
For a real coherent Jones map K = [[p,q],[r,s]] with positive determinant, denote by M its Stokes/Mueller matrix, in I,Q,U,V order with one handedness convention throughout. Using the output/input coherency transformation KρKᵀ gives

    M_II = (p²+q²+r²+s²)/2
    M_QQ = (p²−q²−r²+s²)/2
    M_UU = ps+qr
    M_VV = ps−qr

Hence the directly observable contrast L=M_II+M_QQ+M_UU−3M_VV is exactly (tr K)²−4 det K. Its sign survives any positive throughput scaling and any global Jones sign. It reads hyperbolic vs elliptic type without a phase-reference sign choice. To claim a *negative trace* additionally requires a fixed phase convention or a phase-referenced measurement. Use complementary prepared polarizations and balanced polarization-resolved output intensities to estimate each M_jj; average total output for complementary inputs to estimate M_II. Do not infer the M entries from the four stage settings. Include uncertainty in analyzer response, backgrounds and source normalization. Test whether the return admits a single real Jones representative up to global phase, including phase leakage and depolarization, using independent process characterization. Real-Jones mode is an assumption to certify. A complex retarder or incoherent mixture can break the interpretation even if it produces an appealing numerical scalar.

At the 35/6 setting, L=+0.000032998589 per unit incident intensity (0.115317% of unpolarized transmission); Be/Rb +0.000020545531 (0.073799%); the (9,4) null gives exactly zero; (4,4) gives −0.01351833. For four diagonal estimates each bounded in absolute error by ε, the worst-case error in L is 6ε. To resolve the ideal equal-face case this way requires ε below 5.49976×10⁻⁶ in incident-intensity units; for Be/Rb below 3.42425×10⁻⁶. Extra common insertion loss multiplies both L and the required absolute budget by its total intensity factor. In a real run, make the decision from the observed interval and report unresolved when it includes zero. The input intervals must separately stay in the preregistered disagreement region. This is an instrument criterion, not an assertion that a given bench meets it.

## Reproduction
Keep passive_optics.py, stokes_test.py and map_precision.py together; each uses Python's standard library. Polynomial identities are checked on the supplied settings by rational arithmetic. Output tables give transmission and discriminant numbers with the sign controls. A separate oblique-face note demonstrates that simply rotating the four-stage commutator does not reproduce a tilted disk incidence test. The present optical protocol is for the orthogonal construction only.
