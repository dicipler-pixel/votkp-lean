What the readings mean

For a forward face, g(z) = (z−1)/(z+1) and q(z) = g(z)². The geometric margin is q(A)+q(B)−1. A positive margin opens the loop. Each rival displays its own signed margin. The units of the product margin differ from those of the sums.

For the ideal four-stage transfer, put u(z) = (z−1)/(2√z). The predicted trace is 2−4u(A)²u(B)². A measured return would require independent calibration, two input probes, and a determinant and alignment uncertainty budget.

The source ledger covers hydrogen through astatine at pinned revision 893bd101. Atomic settings encode isolated removal-energy ratios and drive a dimensionless comparison model. The return trace has the status of a prediction. A joint atomic response would need a separate physical observable.
