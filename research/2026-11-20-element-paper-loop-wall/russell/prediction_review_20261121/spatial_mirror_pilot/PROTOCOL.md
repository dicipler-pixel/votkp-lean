# A small blinded mirror test

Select 24 claims from the public September ledger of 386 book entries plus 199 separate laws. Use the first four pure-T and four pure-F records in each of G, U, L under the SHA-256 ordering specified in select_pilot.py. Shuffle again. Sampling balances the historical grades for this discrimination test. It gives no estimate of the prevalence of true claims in Russell's books. The old grades label the author's assessment and may be contested.

Raters receive only shuffled claim text and this codebook; do not open source ledgers, known grades, maps or other raters' answers until all classifications are frozen. Under active spatial inversion about the origin, transform the whole apparatus, polar vectors (−), axial vectors (+), and electric charge (unchanged). A scalar inward/outward radial relation can be invariant even if the Cartesian components reverse. For the principal physical readout actually named in the quotation, select E if it is an invariant scalar/relation, O if a signed component or pseudoscalar necessarily changes, M if genuinely inseparable opposite parities are stated, U if no unique observable or transformation can be identified. Do not pick one feature because it rescues the historical verdict. Record a one-line reason. These operations are not global charge conjugation, exchanging the order of two ionization energies, or a reversal of loop traversal.

Before seeing the answers, define evaluation: count E/O/M/U, cross-tabulate with previous T/F, evaluate coverage of joint E/O decisions and agreement between raters, count E→T and O→F agreements among jointly decisive same-label records, list contrary examples and abstentions. Measured here denotes reproducible judgments with named sources and operation, not a new truth assessment of physics. Twenty-four is a pilot. A source claim phrased as a sign-free proposition can be true or false. The November paper's mirror can involve other named operations; a result for spatial inversion alone cannot settle every potential formulation. Never publish the private blind questionnaire verbatim. Public source + code allow reproduction.
