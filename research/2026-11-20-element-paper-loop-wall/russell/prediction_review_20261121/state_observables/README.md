# Can the excited carbon or oxygen thresholds predict a separately measured outcome?

**Result.** The excited-terminal-channel extension gives carbon and oxygen ratios where the frozen midpoint (5.837220418) and geometry (5.828427125) disagree. A particular carbon level already has measured decay times. Oxygen supplies the better matched control and a counterexample to identifying a route classification with an intrinsic terminal-state decay class. These are retrospective findings. No reported atomic decay or yield constitutes a successful advance prediction of the wall.

## 1. Oxygen keeps the terminal level fixed and moves the route across both cutoffs

**Derived.** Use the same ground-state O⁺ intermediate, the same final O²⁺ 2s²2p4s ³P°₀ at 356736.30 cm⁻¹ and the same final-state photon threshold, 79.35078421 eV. Vary only the neutral O starting fine-structure state. With neutral excitation p, measured from its ground state, the ratio is (I₂+E_final)/(I₁−p). The change in denominator is the physical reduction in the first ionization energy for the excited precursor. The starting-state extension is explicit. The published ground-to-ground ladder still has one entry. The two boundary shifts for this residual level are p=29.13919 and 194.55575 cm⁻¹. A terminal-level lifetime τ=(Σ A)⁻¹ in a fixed environment is intrinsic to the prepared decaying state. A unique such lifetime cannot simultaneously diagnose these three preparation routes. If the initial state is fixed to the neutral ground by definition, only the first row for this residual level is in that hypothesis. Restricting the definition removes this counterexample to that narrower claim and still leaves the wall-to-lifetime relation unspecified. [NIST lifetime definition](https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-spectroscopy-compendium-basic-ideas-notation-data-and-formulas/atomic-spectroscopy-atomic)

A state-selected ionization cross section after resetting the O⁺ intermediate has a related control. Match the intermediate ion density matrix (including magnetic sublevel populations and coherences), environment, normalized ion counts, and second photon beam. Then p(second outcome)=Tr(Mρ) is the same for all antecedents at fixed ρ and measurement M. A path-memory hypothesis would need to identify what survives this matched preparation and predict a signed change in a specified output. Ordinary differences in first-stage yield, alignment, unresolved metastables or fields can otherwise carry preparation information.

## 2. Ground-start oxygen fine-structure comparison and an independent observable

Using only neutral-ground starts, O²⁺ 2s²2p4s ³P° with J=0,1,2 has z=5.826880873, 5.827870339, 5.830347009. Geometry flags J=2 outside. The midpoint puts all three inside. The same configuration and parity limit the confounds, although the angular momentum and angular factors change. A 1971 beam-foil experiment lists **0.44 ± 0.06 ns** for the 1588 Å 2p3p ³D ← 2p4s ³P multiplet. Its table does not assign distinct lifetimes to these three upper J levels. It cannot determine a J=2 contrast. [1971 beam-foil paper](https://doi.org/10.1364/JOSA.61.000519)

**Prospective design, conditional on a physical bridge.** Before observing any new time traces, specify whether the wall changes a rate or a yield, the signed contrast and smallest effect the model commits to, and the preparation convention. For lifetimes, compare fitted decay slopes at the three tags, blind the analysis labels, fit cascade contamination and instrumental response, and resolve contaminant branches near each tag. The closest two chosen tags are 0.610 Å apart near 1590 Å (λ/Δλ ≈ 2600 just to separate centroids; calibrate actual profile overlap). An independent theory uncertainty is needed before interpreting a small departure from 1.00026. If the measured interval excludes the prespecified effect while precision and control criteria are met, reject that bridge. A wall that gives no sign or effect size cannot be scored with a lifetime contrast. For production instead measure level-tagged O⁺ photoionization at matched excess energy above each channel with corrected branching fractions. A neutral-to-ion ratio alone supplies neither amplitude.

## 3. What exists for carbon

For the C²⁺ 2p3p ¹D₂ even target, E_final=333118.21 cm⁻¹; the C⁺ photoionization threshold is 65.6845373 eV and z=5.833291054. Allard et al., Table IIIa (p. 583), record two **separate measurements**, 0.38 ns (Poulizac & Buchet 1971) and 0.53 ns (Poulizac, Druetta & Ceyzeriat 1971), for the 3p′ ¹D level at 41.30 eV. They are individual results, not confidence interval endpoints. The more recent structure calculation gives 0.440 ns. The same compilation gives far shorter lifetimes for the nearby 3d′ ¹D° level, 0.14 ns and 0.191 ± 0.013 ns, even though its ratio 5.828590235 gives the **same** geometric and rival classifications as the even target. That odd state is a poor yield control: E1 from ground C⁺ ²P° gives even total continuum parity, so the parity of the outgoing orbital angular momentum is opposite in these two channels. Actual even-singlet-D controls 2s4d (z=5.73523) and 2s5d (5.98238) have different excitation character. All three carbon examples refer to distinct, bound excited residual states. [Allard et al., Table IIIa](https://articles.adsabs.harvard.edu/pdf/1990A%26AS...84..563A) · [Poulizac & Buchet 1971](https://doi.org/10.1088/0031-8949/4/4-5/007) · [Aggarwal & Keenan 2015](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/450/2/1151/981993)

The older ground/metastable C⁺ near-threshold photoionization work does not report a partial cross section for producing the 65.6845 eV residual channel. The C²⁺ photoionization experiment starts with the wrong charge for that reaction. Inverse detailed balance would need a recombination channel with the specific excited C²⁺ parent and the correct final C⁺ state, spin weights and collision energy. A generic ground-parent dielectronic rate cannot supply that partial amplitude. The optical lifetime after preparing C²⁺ measures its decay, independently of the peel energies, but does not give a C⁺ production amplitude. [Kjeldsen et al. 1999](https://pure.au.dk/portal/en/publications/measurement-of-the-absolute-photoionization-cross-section-of-csup/) · [Nahar 2002](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.65.052702) · [C²⁺ photoionization, 2003](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-583X(02)01969-9)

**Identifiability.** For fixed energy eigenvalues, multiplying an allowed reduced dipole by s changes its line strength by s² and leaves every peel energy, wall side and photon onset unchanged. This is a counterexample to inference from the stated energy identities alone; it need not describe an independently tunable physical dipole in the same ion. Threshold onset simply returns the energy difference used to construct z. Spectral line-shape signs also depend on continuum and discrete transition amplitudes; tagging a Fano peak or dip would add an unprovided physical premise. [Fano 1961](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.124.1866)
