# The Power Grid and the Universe, Scaled to Each Other
*Energy Everywhere All at Once - Jeromie Beasley*

Tags: **SAME PHYSICS** = literally the same law; **STRUCTURAL** = same shape, different physics; **DIFFERENT** = breaks across scales. Where standard cosmology is used it is called "the standard picture". Every item keeps the energy books closed: nothing is drawn from the vacuum for free. Anything speculative is marked "untested".

## Similarities and differences

**1. The Field Carries, the Matter Guides - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: energy flows in the fields around the wires (Poynting vector E x H), mostly through the air; the copper only sets the direction. Universe: starlight, radio and every other wave carry energy as fields; matter (lenses, plasma, mirrors) steers and absorbs it. Scaling: the law is identical from 1 m of cable to 1 light-year of beam; only the guide changes.

**2. Slow Drift, Fast Message - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: electrons drift at ~0.1 mm/s (and just jiggle in AC), yet power arrives at near light speed; electrons are never used up. Universe: matter in a plasma or star moves slowly compared with the light that carries its energy outward. Scaling: ratio of carrier speed to energy speed is ~1e-12 on the grid (0.1 mm/s vs ~3e8 m/s); same pattern, different physics.

**3. Free Space Is a Transmission Line - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: an overhead line has Z0 = sqrt(L'/C') ~ 60 ln(D/r) ohm, typically a few hundred ohm (bundling lowers it). Universe: vacuum itself has Z0 = sqrt(mu0/eps0) = 376.7 ohm, the ratio of E to H in any plane wave. Scaling: line Z0 sits within a factor ~1-1.5 of free space, which is why a line can radiate or be fed like an antenna at all.

**4. Reflection at Every Edge - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: a mismatched load reflects Gamma = (z-1)/(z+1), z = load/Z0. Universe: any boundary (glass, plasma edge, mirror, a conducting wall) reflects with the same formula using wave impedances. Scaling: at his wall z = 3 + 2 sqrt2 = 5.83, Gamma = 1/sqrt2, so Gamma^2 = 1/2: half the power returns, half goes on.

**5. Matched Means Flat - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: at surge impedance loading (P = V^2/Z0, ~1 GW for a 500 kV line with Z0 ~ 260 ohm) line capacitance and inductance cancel and voltage is flat along the line. Universe: an absorber or antenna matched to 376.7 ohm takes all the wave and reflects none. Scaling: the same condition, z = 1, Gamma = 0, at 50 Hz and at light.

**6. The Light-Load Swell - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: an unloaded line's far-end voltage rises by 1/cos(beta l): +15% at 500 km, x2 at 1000 km, x9.6 at 1400 km (50 Hz, wavelength 6000 km). Spacing and bundling change Z0 but not this rise, since wave speed stays ~c. Universe: an open-ended cavity piles up its standing wave the same way near resonance. Scaling: the swell is set by length/wavelength alone; at a quarter wavelength (1500 km at 50 Hz) it diverges until losses cap it.

**7. Earth Rings Like a Line - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: a line of length l resonates when l = (2n-1) lambda/4. Universe: the Earth-ionosphere cavity (circumference ~40,000 km) rings near c/circumference ~ 7.5 Hz (Schumann resonance, observed ~7.8 Hz); stellar and galactic plasma cavities behave likewise. Scaling: a 3000 km continental grid is 0.5 lambda at 50 Hz, so grid scale is already a resonant scale, not a lumped one.

**8. Charge and Momentum Trade Places - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: capacitance stores charge build-up (electric energy), inductance stores momentum (magnetic energy); they swap each quarter cycle. Universe: every electromagnetic wave swaps electric and magnetic energy, equal in a free wave. Scaling: a linear charge/momentum pair neither gains nor loses energy by itself; it is neutral, in both settings.

**9. Heat First, Light Last - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: losses are wire heat (I^2 R), corona in air, insulator leakage, tiny radiation; total a few percent to ~8% of delivered energy. Universe: waves lose energy by scattering and absorption, which becomes heat in the absorber. Scaling: both are conversions to disordered heat, never destruction; the grid's budget is lost per 100 km, the sky's per light-year of dust and gas.

**10. The Antenna That Isn't - DIFFERENT**
Grid: at 50 Hz a 1 km conductor is l/lambda = 1/6000 of a wavelength, with radiation resistance ~80 pi^2 (l/lambda)^2 ~ 2e-5 ohm, so radiation is negligible. Universe: radiation is the main energy channel, because stars and gas radiate at wavelengths comparable to their size. Scaling: grid radiation scales as (l/lambda)^2, ~1e-8 of the line's own scale; universe objects sit near l/lambda ~ 1.

**11. Storage in the Fields, Storage in the Binding - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: energy is stored in C and L fields and in rotating mass, but only seconds' worth; supply matches demand in real time. Universe: energy is held in fields and in binding; ~91% of proton mass is binding/field (lattice QCD: quark mass term 9%, quark motion 32%, glue 36%, anomaly 23%). Scaling: grid stores seconds, the proton stores essentially all of its mass as relation; same idea, opposite durations.

**12. Weight Is the Relation - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: a coil's stored energy lives in the relation (the field linking turns), not in any one wire. Universe: electron mass is the coupling between two massless chiral halves (connection energy exactly 4m^2 across the chiral split); a single photon is massless, but photons in different directions have invariant mass. Scaling: the coil analogy is structural only; untested as a calculation.

**13. Tuned by Hand, or by Itself - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: shunt reactors and capacitors are switched in by operators to tame the Ferranti rise and hold voltage. Universe: a van der Pol-type pair, with gain at small amplitude and loss at large, settles to one amplitude from any start (2.008 for mu = 1); the Aalto autonomous quantum heat engine (Uusnakki et al., arXiv 2603.15355, as supplied) self-starts from heat flow and emits ~5e-18 W of coherent microwaves. Scaling: controller power on a grid is ~1e-2 of throughput; the self-regulating engine pays for control out of its own heat flow.

**14. The Speed Limit Is the Same One - SAME PHYSICS**
Grid: signals and fields cross a 3000 km continent in ~10 ms, half a 50 Hz period (20 ms). Universe: light crosses the Earth-Sun gap in ~499 s and a galaxy (~1e5 light-years) in 1e5 years. Scaling: c is the only speed that matters for the field; the grid is already "relativistic" in the sense that its size is comparable to a wavelength.

**15. The Refill Rate Is the Real Limit - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: a generator's power is capped by how fast fuel and steam can be refilled, not by the physics of the coil. Memory as fuel: a blank bit yields at most kT ln2 of work (2.87e-21 J at 300 K); a slow ladder of qubits reaches 99.7% of it; power is capped by kT ln2 x record rate and by bath refill, 0.2785 gamma kT per qubit. Scaling: both are rate-limited by supply, not by the conversion step.

**16. Spent Records Must Not Flow Back - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: reflected power from a mismatch returns to the source and cuts delivered power by 1 - Gamma^2. Memory engine: if half the spent records return, output falls from 0.69 kT to 0.13 kT. Scaling: both are a "return trip" penalty; the match between the two functional forms is untested.

**17. The Wall Pays for the Light - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: moving a conductor in a field (a generator, a parametric pump) costs mechanical work equal to the electrical energy gained. Universe: between two of his faces at the wall the Casimir pull is 45.6% of that of perfect mirrors; dynamic Casimir light comes from moving the wall, at ~(v/c)^2/(4 pi^2) photons per mode for slow breathing. Scaling: with v/c ~ 1e-3, that is ~2.5e-8 photons per mode; the energy is supplied by the mover, never the vacuum.

**18. Light Carries, Walls Book - STRUCTURAL**
Grid: line energy moves as fields; the terminations and meters book it as heat, work or storage. Universe, in his ball pages: light carries energy between balls, walls and electrons guide, and the vacuum between balls appears exactly at his wall z = 3 + 2 sqrt2. Scaling: carrier and accountant are different objects in both pictures; the ball-page mapping is untested against a measurement.

## WHERE THE ANALOGY BREAKS

1. **Dispatcher versus none.** The grid has an operator who sets demand and can trip a line; the universe has no controller, only equations. Self-regulation (item 13) is the only control that carries over.
2. **Linear AC versus everything else.** A power line is a linear, 50/60 Hz, single-mode system; the universe has gravity, nuclear forces, and nonlinear plasma, none of which have a grid analog.
3. **Global energy ledger.** A grid has one well-defined total energy; in the standard picture of an expanding spacetime, energy is conserved locally but not globally in a single frame, so "the books" must be kept patch by patch.
4. **Z0 is not universal.** 376.7 ohm is vacuum only; inside matter the wave impedance is 376.7/n, so "free space as a line" ends at the first medium.
5. **Scale of loss.** A grid line is engineered to lose a few percent; astrophysical paths are not engineered at all, and the loss per length can be orders of magnitude different.
6. **Gravity.** No grid effect corresponds to gravity pulling on the carried energy; this item stays outside the comparison.

## THREE TESTABLE BRIDGES

1. **Wall load on a real line.** Compute (and then measure with a network analyzer) a 50 ohm line terminated in (3 + 2 sqrt2) x 50 = 291.4 ohm. Prediction: VSWR = 5.83, |Gamma| = 0.7071, return loss exactly 3.01 dB, half the power reflected. If it differs, the wall's reflection rule is wrong. Fully computable today.
2. **Poynting integral equals VI.** For a three-phase bundled geometry (e.g. 500 kV, D and r given), solve the 2D electrostatics/magnetostatics, integrate E x H over the plane, and compare with V I. Prediction: equality to numerical error, with most of the flux outside the conductors; that is item 1 quantified. Also tabulates the air fraction.
3. **Return penalty, two forms.** Take the spent-record model (0.69 kT at no return, 0.13 kT at half return) and fit output versus return fraction f. Test whether it follows (1 - f) like the power-wave 1 - Gamma^2 law, or a steeper form; the grid law is linear in f, so a steeper curve means item 16 is only structural. Untested.
