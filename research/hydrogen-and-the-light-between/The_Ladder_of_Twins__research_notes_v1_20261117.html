The Ladder of Twins: research notes

Jeromie Beasley · 17 November 2026 · working notes for the moving matrix of balls

These notes were put together with AI assistance (Claude). Every statement carries where it comes from: a proved theorem, a computation run today or on a dated page, a hypothesis, or an open question. Nothing here is a result unless it says so.

The stance being tested

For light there are no balls and no space between. There is one tree, and the light meets only its layers. Each layer it meets is a rung, and a rung is never one wall: it is a pair, the face it leaves and the face it meets, with a stretch between them. A twin. The whole row of atoms, the inside of the Sun, the sea of electrons, are one ladder of twins at different scales, and the breathing of each ball is what moves the rungs. The balls are our view from outside; the ladder is the light's view, and the ladder decides what gets through.

Three pictures of the same object. One ball with its layers, infinitely many treeballs in one, every layer itself a ball. A row of balls, each a rung for the light. Two balls pushing on each other, flat: one rung seen face on. The flat picture is the unit; the others are the unit repeated.

1. What one ball already holds computed live on the page

From Balls in One Ball, Mixer v3 (14 November 2026), built on Two Trees in One Ball v4, Definition 4.1, Theorems 5.3, 5.4, 8.4; BallJoins J3–J7.

The centre O and its three neighbours I, J, K are four balls of one tree, each neighbour one peel step from the centre along its own axis. A ball sits at Smith coordinate Γ along its axis, at hyperbolic distance ln z from the centre; the face toward it sits halfway.

Γ = (z − 1)/(z + 1) = tanh(ln z / 2) face at ln z / 2 two faces: g = sinh(ln z₁/2) · sinh(ln z₂/2) g < 1 the faces cross, the neighbours overlap g > 1 the faces stand apart by arccosh g

The square of steps, a then b then back a then back b, does not close on this sheet. When the faces cross it is a turn by 4·arccos g about a fixed point and repeating it carries the centre round a ring: the walk is held. When the faces stand apart it is a run of length 4·arccosh g and each pass carries the centre toward the rim: nothing returns. The line between the two is Γ² = 1/2, z = 3 + 2√2, where the fixed point reaches the rim and the ring opens (Two Trees Theorem 5.3, Lean: schottky_five_below, schottky_seven_above). Ratio 5 sits inside the line, ratio 7 outside it. proved (Two Trees, Lean)

Two steps along different arms leave a turn of the frame, tan(θ/2) = Γ₁Γ₂; with whole steps it is a whole-number right triangle, 5 and 5 give 65, 72, 97. proved With one step on every arm the tree closes into a lattice only at the steps where a whole number of balls fits round each edge, q = (z − 1)²/(4z) = cos(360°/n); at other steps new balls keep landing between old ones and the smallest distance keeps falling. The whole numbers 3 and 5 are not closing steps; 7 is past the line. computed to depth 5

The twin-prime chain is a path in this tree: each twin pair (a, a + 2) is a ball, the step to the next pair is a peel step of ratio a′/a along the arm of the next pair's end digit (1, 7, 9 → i, j, k), ratios multiply and lengths add, so the length walked is exactly ln(a/11). end-digit rule in Lean (J6) path computed live

This is the setup the ladder inherits: a rung is a step ratio, a pair of faces, a Γ; two rungs either cross (held, overlap) or stand apart (gap, run); and the sequence of rungs is a path whose lengths add.

2. The breathing moves the rungs

From the Light at the Hypersurface page (the ball's own schedule), Two Trees Theorem 5.3, and the breathing-row pages built today (HW-WORLD-V4, HW-CHECK-03).

The ball's step breathes on its page's own schedule, and in a row of balls each one is a third of a breath behind the one before.

r_k(t) = ln z + A sin(2π (t − k·T/3) / T) T = 8, A = 0.35 (the page's), k = 0, 1, 2

A step is a boost of rapidity ln z along its axis and carries the wall with it, the plane at −δ to r − δ (Two Trees, proof of Theorem 5.3). So when r breathes the wall moves, and the light has to meet the wall where it is now. proved (the boost); applied here Everything else is read from r(t): the wall's place on the disk Γ = tanh(r/2); the face between two neighbours by their two breaths' clock ratio, Γ_AB = tanh((r_A − r_B)/2), open when they breathe alike, shut when one is large and the other small; the stretch between walls and its gap layer, passing sech²(κ_g δ) (the Flock breakdown's gap element).

Run today with the walls moving every step, three balls, the Flock engine unchanged: the ledger balls + gaps = 1 holds to 10⁻¹³ through three breaths; C is never lit before the light could reach it (5.36 for a distance 5.33 in time, 8.64 for 8.63 out of time); with the breath wave at the light's speed, s = T/3, the most light reaching C is 0.037, out of time (s = T/3·φ) it is 0.017. computed, HW-CHECK-03 This is the sealed-container result of the Swing test seen again: the hand-off down the row carries more in time than out of time. One mark fell and is recorded: the moving-wall scattering and the fixed-mark scattering differ by 1.7·10⁻³ at step 0.01, halving with the step; a half-sample of where a wall sits, not a disagreement in the physics.

3. The space between, and what fills it

From The Space Between: research notes (6 November 2026) and VOTKP vacuum disk v1, §10.

An element is its atoms plus the space between them. The space between does not conduct; it is ground, the vacuum, the reference everything is read from.

room per atom V = M / (ρ N_A) r_room = (3V/4π)^(1/3) space between E = 1 − (r_cov / r_room)³ the line E = 2/3 (Bruggeman: a conductor stops connecting below one-third fill)

Eighty of eighty-one elements fall on the right side of the 2/3 line; boron sits on it. Bonded atoms (Hg, Si, Ge, I) cross on that line when they melt, expand or are squeezed; loose closed atoms, fluid hydrogen among them, follow Herzfeld's rule instead, sized by the whole cloud. computed on published data (the notes) "The electron decides where matter sits on the disk: free electrons push the return to the rim, ground; bound electrons keep it near the vacuum. The metal/non-metal line and the 2/3 line are the electron's choice to conduct." (VOTKP §10.) The dynamic term is open: the static room does not see helium freeze; "the shake against the spacing decides." open (the notes)

Read into the ladder: a rung's stretch is the space between; while it is above the third it is a layer that returns, below it the two faces have merged and the electron belongs to neither wall. Hydrogen next to hydrogen has equal clocks, Γ = 0 at the wall, the centre of the disk: nothing returns there, and only the stretch stands in the way. The one open item, the shake, is exactly the breathing: the room is static, the breath is the dynamic term the notes asked for. hypothesis

4. Compression, friction, heat

From the layers section of the Light at the Hypersurface page (echoes and Smith addition), the Swing test, Dirac Time v3.1 §9.4 and §12.6 (= IQGT §5.3), and The Space Between.

Compressing the room is E falling: each cloud reaches a larger share of a smaller room, the walls of neighbours come closer, then press. Between two pressing walls there is a stretch between two faces, and light caught there echoes, face to face, each pass returning Γ²; the series adds by Smith addition, the way the single ball's echoes add. The energy held in that stretch is the heat: light that cannot leave by either side, crossing a shrinking distance. At the line the walls merge, the stretch is gone, the electron is shared, the room conducts. Hydrogen under pressure goes metal on exactly this path. hypothesis, in the model's own terms

Friction has a form in the programme already, and it needs a declared bath. For a slowly driven family in contact with a bath at inverse temperature β, relaxing to the instantaneous Gibbs state in a time τ, the excess work of the drive is

ρ̇ = −ik[H, ρ] + (ω(H(t)) − ρ)/τ W_ex = W − ΔF δρ_mn = −τ ω̇_mn / (1 + ikτ(E_m − E_n)) + O(λ̇²)

(Dirac Time §12.6, theorem level; the same law as IQGT §5.3.) The breath is the slow drive; the walls pressing is the drive on the stretch's levels; the excess work per breath is the friction, and it is zero without a bath. proved (the law) hypothesis (that the breath is the drive it bounds) What the projector cannot do is set an absolute rate: H → sH keeps the occupied projector and scales every gap, so geometry fixes directions and a rate is read only against another clock, "rate is weight" (Dirac Time §9.4, in its Lean library). proved That is why the row's clocks are compared pairwise, Γ_AB from r_A − r_B, and never against a clock the picture does not contain.

Heat on the clocks, as run earlier in this session (HEAT-SEA-01/02): a squeeze of the clock rates opens the balls' pockets in proportion to its amplitude and does not seal them; the sealing seen in the pocket work came from a damped layer, and that sea had no bath. computed Consistent with §12.6: without Γ_diss there is no friction, only a drive.

5. The wall is where the record is written

From What the Neutrino State Must Keep v10 (13 November 2026) and Dirac Time v3.1 §§1.1, 10.5, 16.2.

The neutrino collision operator read as a cut between what is kept and what streams: all of its non-normality sits on the single link between density and flux, ⅛‖[L, L*]‖² = κ²k²/3, and the weight across the cut is the sound speed, √E_B = k/√3. The kept population obeys Ȧ² ≤ 4E_B A(1 − A). The memory has a location: structure set at or after decoupling survives in later density, flux and stress by several per cent; set deep in the coupled era it is erased by exp[−ε_dec^n/(nτ_s^n)]. theorem-level identities computed on McCormick's code, kill lines fixed first

Read into the ladder: a rung is a cut, and the cut is where a record is written or erased. A stretch above the third holds its light and holds its record; a stretch that has merged erases it into the sea. The programme's order is memory before clock, "retained distinction → predictive continuation → possible temporal structure" (Dirac Time §1.1): the rungs come first and the clock is read off them. A fine record can be lost while a deeper one survives and is later made visible again (§16.2): the light that goes into the stretch and comes out breaths later is that transfer. hypothesis

6. The Sun, and scale

From Ballad of a Dead Photon, 3D mixer v2c (12 November 2026).

A photon born at the centre is absorbed and re-emitted in a new direction at every step and needs about (R/ℓ)² steps, tens of thousands of years, to reach the photosphere; the interior is Eddington's standard model, density ρ = ρ_c θ³ and temperature T = T_c θ from the Lane–Emden equation with n = 3, the rings at ρ_c/10, ρ_c/100, ρ_c/1000. model + estimate (the page's grade) The Sun is one ball with its layers; the photon's walk out is the light's path up a ladder of rungs, each ring a wall with its own return, and the straight run to Earth is the last rung's pass. The three hydrogens in a row are the Sun's layers laid out; the Sun is balls nested. hypothesis (the reading)

7. What the pages built today got wrong, and why

Three balls drawn with a "room" and a "gap layer" between them are the outside view with the ladder cut into pieces: the light goes ball, space, ball, space, ball, when it should go rung, rung, rung. The four caps that faced a real neighbour were the single-ball page's imagined neighbours, painted on an open door; they are now off. The walls were at their rest places; they now move with the breath. What is still wrong: the stretch is drawn as a thing between balls instead of as a rung of the one ladder, the electrons between are drawn by the filled share instead of being computed, and the ball's own layers (the stack, the echoes, the level shells) are not rungs of the same ladder yet. The next build is one ladder.
