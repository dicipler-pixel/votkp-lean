# Light at the Hypersurface

Jeromie Beasley, 15 November 2026. AI-assisted.

Two interactive 3D mixers of light meeting the faces of the peel tree in one Poincaré ball, with everything moving on one clock.

**`Light_at_the_Hypersurface_Mixer_v2_20261115.html`, the planned build.** Ten eyes on one shared object:
- A. The object: four balls, faces, rim.
- B. One face is one reflecting step, R = Γ².
- C. Refraction at the face, a sin²θ conserved.
- D. Total return and the gap.
- E. Layers: echoes and Smith addition.
- F. Pressure and the kick record.
- G. Rotation within and outside the layers: the Thomas turn and the square.
- H. Light on the rim: aberration as ⊕, and the caps.
- I. The hypothesis layer.
- J. Everything at once, and the ledger.

v1 is kept beside it. The plan is in `PLAN_light_at_the_hypersurface.md`, the review in `REVIEW_fable_v1.md`, and every check outcome, including the killed lines, in `LEDGER_build_v2.md`.

**`surprise/Light_at_the_Hypersurface_Surprise_v1.html`, the surprise build.** An independent take, with its own check: 49 lines, all hold.
