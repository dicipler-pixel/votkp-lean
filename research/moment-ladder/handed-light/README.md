# Handed light and the curvature half of the tensor

Jeromie Beasley. 24 November 2026.

Tensor: Q_ij = sum over m of <0|D_i|m><m|D_j|0> / gap_m^2 = g_ij - (i/2) Omega_ij.

Test (direct time evolution, four levels, complex dipole elements, two drive frequencies):
induced dipole fitted on the field and its rate of change.

- Sideways rate coefficients divided by the curvature: 1.005 and -1.023 (frequency 0.04), 1.010 and -0.974 (0.02),
  1.005 and -1.012 (0.01). Antisymmetric, equal to the curvature within 1 to 3 percent.
- Same-axis rate coefficients (where the metric half would show): 0.2 to 3 percent of the curvature. Not seen.
- Same coefficients for both senses of rotation, so the sideways dipole itself reverses with the handedness.
- Control with real dipole elements: curvature zero, rate coefficients below 4e-4.

The 2 percent line written before the run was missed by two of six ratios. The miss does not fall with
frequency, so it is scatter in the fit and not a next-rung term; its source is not pinned.
HANDED-LIGHT-01 (one frequency on both axes) was a badly posed fit and is not a result.

Exact statement behind it (sum over states): alpha_xy - alpha_yx = sum over m of 4 i w Im(D_x D_y) / (gap_m^2 - w^2),
so the antisymmetric response runs on the odd rungs: w times rung -3, w^3 times rung -5, and so on.
