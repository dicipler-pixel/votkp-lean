# Twin Primes in the Tree v1 (Jeromie Beasley, 24 Nov 2026, AI-assisted)

Companion page to One Tree, Every Shape v2.1: the twin chain read three ways. `TWIN-TRICK-DATA-01` (twin_trick_data.py) computes everything to 1e8; `TWIN-TWO-CLOCKS-01` (two_clocks.py) the information split.

1. Chain endpoint and turn: d/L = 0.5841, frame turn 12.205° at 1e8 (reproduces harness T2: 12.204756°). 50 arm-shuffled chains: d/L 0.582–0.612, turn 7.2–42.8°; real inside both (low edge). The order shows in total turning instead (WHAT-BENDS-TURN-01).
2. Ladder of twins: air ≡ 4 mod 6 exact; shares by decade, 28-share / 4-share = 1.95, 2.21, 2.31, 2.44, 2.57, rising toward the singular-series limit 3.
3. Two clocks: next air carries 2.22 bits; the start residue mod 30 gives 0.64 bits; mean gap / (t²/2C₂) by start 1.009 / 0.989 / 1.002; single-gap CV 0.94 (open).
