### IQGT — what "his tensor" is, in one paragraph (for recognising the others)

Real part ½Tr(VW) = Hilbert–Schmidt metric = Σ sin² of principal angles (rate of subspace rotation); imaginary part iTr(Π[V,W]) = Berry curvature (phase = the only memory of the path). Built premetric from the Riesz contour, so every entry is "coupling ÷ own gap", and every physical tensor is that one pair set read with a power of the gap.
