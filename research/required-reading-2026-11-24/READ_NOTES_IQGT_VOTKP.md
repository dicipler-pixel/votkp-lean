### C. Recognition guide — how to tell what another tensor is, against his
Ask of any tensor T these questions, in order:
1. **Is there a ratio inside it?** Write every entry as coupling/gap or as a ratio of two levels. If yes, map each ratio to Γ = tanh(½ ln z). If the entries are (gap)^{-k} × |coupling|², T is a **gap-power member** of his family: k=2 → metric g (QGT real part / Fubini–Study / Bures for pure states), k=1 → static susceptibility/polarizability, k=−2 → commutator norm, k=3 → oscillator-strength metric ∫t⁻³dF (A.16).
2. **Symmetric or antisymmetric part?** Symmetric (g, books E±, |Γ|², reflected power) = the **even record**: blind to sign, order, mirror (Ad(−U) = Ad(U)). Antisymmetric (Ω Berry curvature, optical phase, frame turn, signature) = the **odd record**: carries direction/order. Any tensor that only has the even part cannot see the twin-chain turn or the alkali phase.
3. **Does it sum over pairs with a positive weight?** IQGT's "one pair sum, many weights": Σ_{m∈P,n∉P} w(Δ_mn)|⟨m|∂H|n⟩|². Weight Δ⁻² = QGT; SLD/Bures uses 2/(p_m+p_n) with (p_m−p_n)² — that is E−/E+ structure, i.e. Bures weight = (p_m−p_n)²/(p_m+p_n) ∝ Γ_mn²·(p_m+p_n) with Γ_mn = (p_m−p_n)/(p_m+p_n): **Bures is his peel coordinate on populations**. Friction tensor (thermodynamic length) uses a bath weight w(τΔ) = his Γ_u form. Wigner–Yanase/other monotone metrics = other weight choices.
4. **Collinear or not?** If all the tensor's generators commute (one axis), it is a ladder: climbs, no turn, associative. If two non-commuting directions appear, compute the loop trace 2 − 4sinh⁴(r/2): below Γ² = ½ it turns; at it, Jordan/exceptional point; beyond, runs (evanescent). Petermann K = 1/(1−2Γ²) tells you which.
5. **Where are its three places?** Find its shell wall, half line (Γ² = ½, R = T, K→∞, tr = −2 for a loop / |tr| = 6 for one step), rim (|Γ| = 1, lossless/evanescent). A tensor with a "critical" value at a ratio of 3+2√2 (silver ratio squared) or at equal returned/passed share is sitting on his half line.
6. **What does it lose?** Use his representation-loss table: scalar summary, footprint, endpoint each lose different things; the books lose sign; the ladder loses order; power loses phase.
