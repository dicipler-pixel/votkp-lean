The Swing: notes and test

The ground state as a swing, the open end, and the bigger container · Jeromie Beasley · 16 November 2026 · AI-assisted

These are the notes of one evening's talk turned into a test. The kill lines were written to a file before any run. One part was killed on the first run; it is recorded, and its rerun has its own file written before that run. The model is one line of light, faces and ends. It is small on purpose: every number in it can be derived by hand first.

1 · The notes

The swing. The ground state, the sea of electrons, is like a swing, a cosmic sync beam.

The crux. Is it a charged loop like structure that holds itself together shut, all energy conserved, or an open container on some end, or no end no background?

The bigger container. An open end loses a little each cycle and needs feeding, unless it is in another even bigger container, then it may just be a compression of sorts.

The kick. If you want to get energy you must kick it like a photon.

The beam. Everywhere at once, or must the sync travel along the links like light. A third option from the talk: the swing at rest is everywhere, and only changes to it travel.

2 · The model

A line of stretches of field joined by faces. Light crosses one cell per step. A face of step ratio z scatters by the proved rule, and an end is one of the two extremes of the same rule.

Γ = (z − 1)/(z + 1)  ·  return Γ², pass 1 − Γ²  ·  mirror: |Γ| = 1  ·  matched load: Γ = 0

A swing is the light held between two faces; its period is the round trip, 2L. There is no law of motion for the faces here and no torque, so nothing in this model can lock. Choices made to close it: one dimension, and the face sign (+ρ seen from the left).

3 · The loss comes back

The same swing, twice. With an open end it keeps a quarter at every meeting with its face (z = 3, Γ² = ¼) and nothing returns. Inside a bigger container of the same length, what left meets the swing again at the face, and the face hands all of it back: ¼, then 1, then ¼, then 1.

Share of the light held by the swing against step. Both runs are the same until what left has had time to come back.

4 · At rest everywhere; changes travel

Two swings joined by a link of 20 cells, sealed. The sea is half of the step matrix's spectrum, with the cut in a gap. At rest the two swings are already tied: the weight between them is 0.00805. Kick one cell of the first swing, and the sea on the second does not move at all until light could have crossed.

Before step 24 the change is 3.4e-15 (rounding). In the Moving Matrix, where one matrix with direct links moves everything together, the far ball has already changed by 0.0016 at 1% of the crossing time.

7 · What this says and does not say

Says. In this model an open end and a bigger container are told apart by one thing: whether what left comes back, and when. A container in time with the swing returns the whole loss. A squeeze in time feeds the swing and out of time does not, and the books of the squeeze close against the wall's work. A sea can be tied everywhere at rest and still pass no change faster than light: the third option is a consistent machine. The all-at-once machine differs from it in when the far sea first moves.

Grades. T2, T3, T3b: derived from the proved face rule and checked. T4, T5: reproduction of the moving-mirror Doppler law and radiation pressure, shown in the face language; at v → 0 the wall pressure is the 2R of the ball pages with R = 1. T6′: checked on one layout with a declared choice of the filled half; a tie at rest in a filled sea is known physics, the point here is that it sits with the travel rule in one machine. T6d: checked on one three-ball matrix.

Does not say. Which machine nature uses. Whether the universe is sealed, open or nested. Anything about locking or gear locking: this model is linear and cannot lock. Anything about the clock ratio z. The sea here has no interactions, is in one dimension, and the tie depends on which half is filled.

# PREREG_SWING_01 — the swing, the open end and the bigger container
Jeromie Beasley. Written 16 Nov 2026 (Central), before any run. AI-assisted.
Script: SWING_TEST_01 (swing_test_01.py). Nothing in this file is changed after the first run; results go in a separate file.

## The ideas under test (his, in his words where given)
N1. The ground state, the sea of electrons, is "like a swing", a "cosmic sync beam".
N2. Is it "a charged loop like structure that holds itself together shut, all energy conserved", or "an open container on some end",
    or "no end no background"?
N3. An open end loses a little each cycle and needs feeding, "unless it is in another even bigger container, then it may just be a
    compression of sorts".
N4. "If you want to get energy you must kick it like a photon" (in time with the swing).
N5. The open question on the beam: everywhere at once, or must the sync travel along the links like light. A third option raised in
    talk: the swing at rest is everywhere, and only changes to it travel.

## The model (stated so every line can be checked)
A line of stretches of field joined by faces. Light crosses one cell per step at speed 1. A face with step ratio z has Smith coordinate
Γ = (z − 1)/(z + 1) and scatters by the proved rule: return Γ², pass 1 − Γ² (step_matching, flux_balance). An end is a mirror (|Γ| = 1,
everything returns) or a matched load (Γ = 0, nothing returns). A swing is the light held between two faces; its period is the round
trip, 2L. No law of motion for the faces, no torque, no locking: this model is linear. MODEL CHOICE: one dimension; face signs
(+ρ seen from the left, −ρ from the right).

## Tests, why each decides its claim, and the kill line (fixed now)
T1 SEALED. Mirror, stretch L, mirror. If the loop is shut, nothing can leave, so the swing must return exactly.
   KILL: energy differs from 1 by more than 1e-12 at any step, or the state at step 2L differs from the start by more than 1e-12.
T2 OPEN END. Mirror, swing L, face Γ, stretch, matched load. Each meeting with the face keeps Γ² and passes the rest, and the load
   returns nothing. So after n meetings the swing holds exactly Γ^(2n).
   KILL: |held − Γ^(2n)| > 1e-12 for any n ≤ 20; or held + absorbed + in transit ≠ 1 to 1e-12.
T3 BIGGER CONTAINER (N3). Same swing, but the outside stretch D ends in a mirror. Derived before the run: what leaves at step L comes
   back into the swing at step L + 2D and not one step earlier, so the two runs (open end, bigger container) must agree exactly until
   then and differ from then on. Books over swing plus container stay 1.
   KILL: the two runs differ before step L + 2D; or do not differ at step L + 2D; or total ≠ 1 to 1e-12.
T3b IN TIME (N3, "a compression of sorts"). D = L, so what left meets the swing again at the face at the same instant. Derived: a
   lossless two-sided face is its own inverse on the pair (inside, outside) (a real symmetric scattering with determinant −1), so the
   swing must hold Γ² after odd meetings and exactly 1 after even meetings: the whole loss comes back. Holds for either face sign.
   KILL: held after meeting n differs from Γ² (n odd) or 1 (n even) by more than 1e-12, n ≤ 20. Run with both face signs.
T4 THE SQUEEZE DOES WORK (N3, N4). Swing between a fixed mirror and a mirror that moves, L(t) = L0(1 + a sin(Ωt + φ)); exact rays
   (each ray's meeting time with the wall is solved, nothing is imposed). Derived: one meeting with a wall moving at v multiplies the
   pulse energy by D = (1 − v)/(1 + v) (v > 0 moving away). With Ω = π/L0 the pulse meets the wall at the same phase every time.
   a = 0.01, so v0 = aπ and D0 = (1 + v0)/(1 − v0).
   T4a IN TIME, wall moving in at each meeting (φ = 0): energy after 20 meetings = D0^20.   KILL: off by more than 1%.
   T4b IN TIME, wall moving out (φ = π): energy after 5 meetings = D0^(−5) (energy handed back).   KILL: off by more than 1%.
   T4c OUT OF TIME (Ω × golden ratio): no net gain.   KILL: |mean log gain per meeting| over 200 meetings > 0.1 ln D0.
   T4d BOOKS: energy gained = work done by the wall, with wall pressure 2 × (incident energy density) × D. At v → 0 this is the
       pressure 2R with R = 1.   KILL: |gain − work| / |gain| > 1e-3 over the 20 meetings of T4a.
   Grade if it holds: REPRODUCTION (Doppler at a moving mirror and radiation pressure are textbook), shown in the face language.
T5 THE SWING'S RATE MOVES WITH THE SQUEEZE (N3). Wall moves in slowly at v = 1e-3 from L0 to 0.8 L0. Derived: energy × length is kept,
   so energy rises by 1/0.8 = 1.25, and the period falls to 0.8 of its start. A leak (T2) changes neither the period nor raises energy.
   KILL: energy ratio off 1.25 by more than 1%; period ratio off 0.8 by more than 1%.
T6 THE SEA: EVERYWHERE AT REST, CHANGES TRAVEL (N1, N5). Two swings A and B (6 cells each, faces z = 3) joined by a link of 20 cells,
   mirrors at both ends: a sealed loop. One step is a matrix V. The rest sea is the projector P onto half of V's spectrum (the half
   with negative imaginary part). MODEL CHOICE: that half; it is a rest state of V by construction, checked.
   T6a REST: V P V† = P.   KILL: differs by more than 1e-12.
   T6b TIED AT REST: the weight between A and B, X = ‖P_AB‖², is not zero though nothing is moving.   KILL: X ≤ 1e-12.
   T6c CHANGES TRAVEL: kick one cell of A (a local reflection), then step. The sea on B must not change by even 1e-12 before the
       crossing step t× = 24 (the kicked right-mover sits at cell 2; B starts at cell 26), and must change after.
       KILL: any change above 1e-12 on B before step 24; or no change above 1e-9 by step 72.
   T6d ALL AT ONCE (the Moving Matrix way), for contrast: three balls, one matrix H = D + A with direct links c0·sech(d/2), sea = levels
       below the largest gap, kick ball A, move by e^(−iHt). The far ball must already have changed at 1% of the light-crossing time.
       KILL: change on the far ball at t = 0.01·d is below 1e-8.
   What T6 would say if all hold: the third option is a consistent machine (tied at rest and causal together), and the two machines
   differ in reading 1 (when the far sea first moves). It would NOT say which one nature uses.

## What happened in run 01 (stands as recorded)
T6a was KILLED: V P V† differed from P by 0.075. The cause, read from the output: the step matrix V has spectrum exactly on the cut
(smallest distance 4e-17; real V on this layout has eigenvalues at +1 or −1). "The half with negative imaginary part" is then not a
defined set, so the P of run 01 was not a rest sea. T6c failed with it (a sea that is not at rest changes on B by itself from step 1).
T6b and E2 used the same P and are withdrawn with it. This is the need the Flock session had already written down: the arc has to end
in gaps. T1 to T5 and T6d do not use P and are not rerun.

## The one change
The filled half is the arc of V's spectrum from angle φ to φ + π, where φ is the angle whose two ends (φ and φ + π) are farthest from
any spectrum point. The script finds φ; nothing else is chosen. MODEL CHOICE: a half-circle arc; its ends in the widest gaps.
RUNNABLE ONLY IF the margin (distance from each end to the nearest spectrum point) is above 1e-6; otherwise the report is "no gapped
half exists for this layout" and no verdict is given.

## Tests and kill lines (the same lines as run 01)
T6a' REST: V P V† = P.   KILL: differs by more than 1e-12.
T6b' TIED AT REST: X = ‖P_AB‖² between swings A and B.   KILL: X ≤ 1e-12.
T6c' CHANGES TRAVEL: kick cell 2 of A; the sea on B must not change by 1e-12 before step 24 and must change after.
     KILL: any change above 1e-12 on B before step 24; or no change above 1e-9 by step 72.
The opposite, run under the same lines: the other half of the circle (the complement arc).

import json, math
import numpy as np
from scipy.linalg import expm
OUT = {}
def line(seg, zs, right, sign=1):
    """A line of stretches (cell counts seg) joined by faces of step ratio zs. Left end: mirror. right: 'mirror' or 'load'."""
    N = sum(seg); b = np.cumsum(seg)[:-1] - 1; g = [(z - 1.0)/(z + 1.0) for z in zs]
    def step(R, L):
        nR = np.zeros_like(R); nL = np.zeros_like(L); nR[1:] = R[:-1]; nL[:-1] = L[1:]
        for k, rho in zip(b, g):
            tau = math.sqrt(1 - rho*rho); nR[k+1] = tau*R[k] - sign*rho*L[k+1]; nL[k] = sign*rho*R[k] + tau*L[k+1]
        nR[0] = -L[0]; ab = 0.0
        if right == 'mirror': nL[N-1] = -R[N-1]
        else: nL[N-1] = 0.0; ab = abs(R[N-1])**2
        return nR, nL, ab
    return N, step
def run(seg, zs, right, steps, sign=1):
    N, step = line(seg, zs, right, sign); R = np.zeros(N); L = np.zeros(N); R[0] = 1.0; Lc = seg[0]; held = []; tot = []; ab = 0.0
    for t in range(steps + 1):
        held.append(float(np.sum(R[:Lc]**2 + L[:Lc]**2))); tot.append(float(np.sum(R**2 + L**2)) + ab)
        R, L, a = step(R, L); ab += a
    return np.array(held), np.array(tot), (R, L)

def Vmat(seg, zs):
    N, step = line(seg, zs, 'mirror'); V = np.zeros((2*N, 2*N))
    for j in range(2*N):
        x = np.zeros(2*N); x[j] = 1; R, L, _ = step(x[:N].copy(), x[N:].copy()); V[:N, j] = R; V[N:, j] = L
    return N, V
def sea(V):

def Vmat(seg, zs):

        for k, rho in zip(b, g):
            tau = math.sqrt(1 - rho*rho); nR[k+1] = tau*R[k] - rho*L[k+1]; nL[k] = rho*R[k] + tau*L[k+1]
        nR[0] = -L[0]; nL[N-1] = -R[N-1]; V[:N, j] = nR; V[N:, j] = nL

def gapped_sea(V, flip=False):
